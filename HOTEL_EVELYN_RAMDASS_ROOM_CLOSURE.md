## Executive closure

Ram Dass is securely placed **inside Hotel Evelyn, Nainital, in 1971**, and the evidence localizes several communal hotel spaces much more tightly than a generic hotel-only claim: the **main terrace**, **front patio/veranda**, and a **lake-facing balcony**. His own later account also proves that he had a **private hotel room with a door** at Evelyn, because he describes putting a meditation notice on his door and visitors being unable to enter.

What the public record examined in this closure does **not** establish is the room number, floor, wing, room category, exact door, or a defensible present-day successor room. No public source found in this pass supplies an old guest register, 1971 floor plan, room key/number, room photograph, or first-hand statement identifying Ram Dass's specific guest room. Therefore no exact room number should be attached to the atlas.

The best physical closure is two-tiered:

- **SLEEP:** Hotel Evelyn itself is proven; Ram Dass's exact bedroom remains unresolved within it. **R4 — hotel only.**
- **STAND:** the historic **main terrace / front-patio / veranda complex** is independently documented and photographed as a 1971 Ram Dass group-use zone. The current hotel's principal lake-facing terrace/veranda is the strongest present-day successor candidate, but fabric/geometry continuity has not been proven. **R3 — strong localized floor/zone, not exact current slab/spot.**

This is intentionally conservative. The hotel's own/current historical description says that a pre-1947 guesthouse on the site was later transformed by S.L. Sah into a roughly 60-room hotel. That makes unproven building continuity a real risk, not a technicality.

## 1. What can be proved about Ram Dass's accommodation?

### 1.1 Hotel: proven

Ram Dass's memoir *Being Ram Dass* states that after the Kausani retreat in the summer of 1971 the group returned to Nainital and re-established themselves at Hotel Evelyn. He describes traveling from Evelyn to Kainchi as Maharajji allowed, reading spiritual texts on the hotel veranda in fair weather, and calls Evelyn part ashram, part hangout and part therapy office.

Parvati Markus's *Love Everyone* independently places Ram Dass at Evelyn in September 1971 and describes the Western group living there as a de facto ashram. It also records the front-patio Sunday readings and the private-room meditation-door incident.

**Closure:** Hotel Evelyn = secure.

Sources:

- Parvati Markus, *Love Everyone* searchable text: https://dokumen.pub/love-everyone-the-transcendent-wisdom-of-neem-karoli-baba-told-through-the-stories-of-the-westerners-whose-lives-he-transformed-9780062342997.html

### 1.2 A private Ram Dass room existed: proven

In the Evelyn section of *Love Everyone*, Ram Dass recounts that while living with the Westerners at Hotel Evelyn he once put a notice on **his door** saying he was going to meditate. Radha and Mohan came during an argument but could not get in; Maharajji later asked where Ram Dass had been. This is useful physical evidence: Ram Dass was not merely visiting common areas; he had a private room/door within the hotel.

What the passage does **not** say:
- room number;
- floor;
- wing;
- whether it had a balcony;
- whether it opened onto the front veranda;
- whether the room survives today.

**Closure:** existence of a private room is secure; localization inside the building is not. `R4` for sleep.

## 2. Balcony, patio, veranda, terrace, garden, stairs and entrance

### 2.1 Lake-facing balcony — historically secure, private-room linkage NOT secure

The strongest balcony testimony comes from Parvati Markus. In September 1971 she and Ram Dass stood together leaning over a balcony at Evelyn, looking across Naini Lake and the Himalayan foothills after returning from Kainchi. She also describes incense coming from various rooms where Westerners were meditating or doing devotional practices.

This securely proves a **Ram Dass + Evelyn + balcony + lake-view** scene. It does **not** identify whose balcony it was or whether it was attached to Ram Dass's bedroom. Current reviews/listings show both private balconies and long/common balcony/veranda arrangements, so converting this memory into “Ram Dass's balcony room” would be unjustified.

### 2.2 Front patio / veranda — historically secure

Two independent narrative lines converge here:

- Ram Dass's memoir: in good weather the group sat on the **veranda** and read spiritual texts; on Sundays Ram Dass read from the New Testament.
- *Love Everyone*: after Kausani, Evelyn became a mini-ashram; on Sundays the group gathered on the **front patio** while Ram Dass read from the Bible and other spiritual books.

The terminology may describe overlapping parts of the same lake-facing open-gallery/terrace complex, but the sources do not prove that `front patio`, `veranda` and `main terrace` were architecturally identical surfaces. They should remain separate historical labels until an on-site owner/family identification closes them.

This is the strongest actual photo-localization in the task. It proves a specific historical *kind of place* within Evelyn — the main terrace — and Ram Dass's physical presence there.

This history is incompatible with blindly assuming that every present room or wall existed in 1971. Without dated plans, building permits, staged construction photos, Sah-family architectural records or room registers, the exact survival of Ram Dass's bedroom remains **unknown**.

## 6. What happened at Hotel Evelyn?

### Summer/autumn 1971 — Western satsang base

After the Kausani meditation-retreat period, Ram Dass and the group reinstalled themselves at Evelyn while Neem Karoli Baba was at Kainchi. Evelyn became the Western devotees' Nainital base, with regular travel to Kainchi. Ram Dass later characterized the hotel as part ashram, hangout and informal therapy office.

Sources:
- https://dokumen.pub/being-ram-dass-9781683646280-9781683646297.html
- https://dokumen.pub/love-everyone-the-transcendent-wisdom-of-neem-karoli-baba-told-through-the-stories-of-the-westerners-whose-lives-he-transformed-9780062342997.html

### Sunday readings on veranda/front patio

Ram Dass read from Christian and other spiritual texts to the group outdoors at Evelyn when weather allowed. His memoir specifically recalls Sunday New Testament readings; *Love Everyone* calls the gathering place the front patio.

**Physical relevance:** this is one of the two strongest standable Ram Dass zones in the hotel.

### K.K. Sah teaching aarti at the hotel

The group often ate together at Evelyn; on special evenings K.K. Sah came to teach aarti. This places K.K. physically at Evelyn in the 1971 satsang context, not merely elsewhere in Nainital.

### September 1971 balcony conversation with Parvati Markus

Parvati Markus records standing with Ram Dass at a lake-facing Evelyn balcony after a day at Kainchi, during which Ram Dass explained that they were seeing Maharajji in order to bear witness. This is a highly specific Ram Dass-in-the-building memory, but it remains **balcony-zone**, not bedroom proof.

Source:
- https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/love-everyone-wisdom-of-neem-karoli-baba-told-through-stories-of-westerners-whose-lives-he-transformed-nap666/

### “Nainital High” group portrait on the main terrace

The iconic 1971 group portrait was made at Hotel Evelyn. University of Kent's photo appendix identifies the spot as the **main terrace**. Ram Dass, K.K. Sah, M.L. Sah, Krishna Das and other members of the Western satsang are identified in the image.

**Physical relevance:** this is the strongest photo-anchored historic spot in the closure.

Sources:
- https://kar.kent.ac.uk/id/document/3229531
- https://ashramsofindia.com/sita-sharan-susan-mccarthy/

### The private-room meditation-door incident

Ram Dass recounts putting a meditation notice on his Evelyn room door; Radha and Mohan sought him during a fight but did not enter. Maharajji referred to his absence the next day. The episode proves a private room/door but gives no physical localization beyond the hotel.

Source:
- https://dokumen.pub/love-everyone-the-transcendent-wisdom-of-neem-karoli-baba-told-through-the-stories-of-the-westerners-whose-lives-he-transformed-9780062342997.html

### Evelyn as referral/handoff point for later Westerners

Ram Dass directed some seekers to Hotel Evelyn and the Sah family. *Love Everyone* records instructions to go to Evelyn, look for M.L. or S.L. Sah, and later cases in 1972 where arrivals stayed at Evelyn and were taken onward toward Kainchi. This gives the hotel a real operational role in the early Western NKB network, beyond merely being accommodation.

## 7. People and physical relationships

### K.K. Sah — secure Hotel Evelyn connection, but distinguish from ownership

K.K. Sah was Ram Dass's translator, mentor, close family friend and an important bridge into Nainital/Kainchi life. Ramdass.org confirms the deep relationship going back to 1967 and that K.K. took Ram Dass into his own home.

At Evelyn in 1971 K.K. is independently tied to:
- aarti teaching/evenings;
- the “Nainital High” main-terrace portrait;
- the broader satsang support network.

However, the best evidence distinguishes K.K. from the hotel operators. Krishna Das/Western-satsang accounts identify **M.L. Sah and S.L. Sah**, K.K.'s cousins, as the Evelyn family operators/owners. Do not rewrite K.K. as “owner of Hotel Evelyn.”

Sources:
- https://www.ramdass.org/honoring-kk-sah/
- https://ashramsofindia.com/the-hotels-they-stayed-in-hotel-evelyn/
- https://kar.kent.ac.uk/id/document/3229531

### M.L. Sah / S.L. Sah — hotel-side Sah family

M.L. Sah appears in the 1971 Nainital High photograph. S.L. Sah is repeatedly associated with the hotel's ownership/operation, and later seekers were told by Ram Dass to find him at Evelyn. Current hotel histories state that the Sah family transformed the earlier small guesthouse into the larger hotel.

**On-site research importance:** surviving Sah-family memory is the single most promising path to a room-level upgrade.

### Neem Karoli Baba / Maharajji — central cause of the Evelyn satsang, but no Hotel Evelyn presence proved

The group stayed at Evelyn because Maharajji was at Kainchi and they traveled there for darshan. The sources in this closure do **not** establish Maharajji sleeping at, visiting, or teaching inside Hotel Evelyn itself.

Do not turn “devotees' hotel / mini-ashram” into “Maharajji stayed here.” His relevant physical locus in these episodes is Kainchi; Evelyn was the Western base.

### Bhagavan Das — important to Ram Dass history; Hotel Evelyn localization NOT established

A potentially misleading photograph must be explicitly excluded. University of Kent's Appendix 6 identifies Photograph 14 — K.K. Photograph 15, separately, is the Nainital High image on Evelyn's main terrace.

Therefore the famous K.K.–Ram Dass–Bhagavan Das rooftop image is **not Hotel Evelyn evidence**.

The public sources examined in this closure did not supply a specific Bhagavan Das room/terrace/event at Evelyn. Do not use him to localize Ram Dass's Evelyn room unless a new source explicitly does so.

Source:
- https://kar.kent.ac.uk/id/document/3229531

### Krishna Das and other Westerners

Krishna Das is identified in the 1971 main-terrace portrait and was part of the Evelyn/Kainchi circle. Numerous other Western devotees lived at the hotel during “Nainital High.” Their presence confirms a communal satsang environment but does not map Ram Dass's room relative to theirs.

## 9. Field protocol — how Mark can close the room on site

The hotel itself is now more valuable as an **oral-history and archive lead** than another generic web search. Use the following order:

1. **Ask for the owner/Sah family or the longest-serving manager/staff member**, not simply any reception employee.
2. Show the 1971 `Nainital High` photograph and ask:  
   “Which present terrace is the old *main terrace* where this photograph was taken in 1971?”
3. Ask separately:  
   “Where was the old *front patio/veranda* where Ram Dass used to read to the group on Sundays?”
4. Ask whether the balcony where Ram Dass and Parvati Markus looked over Naini Lake is remembered as a common balcony or a room balcony.
5. Only then ask:  
   “Do you know which room Richard Alpert / Ram Dass used in 1971 — number, floor or wing?”
6. If a room number is given, ask **what the identification is based on**:
   - old guest register;
   - family memory from S.L./M.L. Sah;
   - old room-number plan;
   - photograph;
   - inherited staff tradition;
   - or merely recent guest lore.
7. Photograph/record, with permission:
   - current room number/door;
   - corridor and floor position;
   - relation to main terrace/front veranda;
   - window/balcony orientation toward Naini Lake;
   - any old register/page/plaque/photo used as evidence.
8. If old numbering changed, obtain both **historic number and current successor number** and ask when the renumbering/rebuild occurred.
9. Do not upgrade to R1/R2 from “staff says so” alone unless the speaker is a credible family/first-hand source and can explain continuity.

### Exact wording worth using at reception

> I am researching Ram Dass's 1971 stay here, the period known as “Nainital High.” I am not asking for the best room; I am trying to identify the historic part of the hotel. Does the Sah family or a long-serving staff member know which room Richard Alpert/Ram Dass used, or where the original main terrace/front patio was? Is there an old guest register or old room-number plan I could see?
