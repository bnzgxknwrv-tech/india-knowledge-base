Status: BINDING CURRENT TRUTH — closes the "Bodh Gaya 2 vs 3 nights / Tiruvannamalai 5 vs 4 nights" trade-off that `governance/CURRENT_TRUTH.md` had explicitly listed as open and deliberately deferred since at least 2026-09-14.

## DECISION

Mark locks:
- **Bodh Gaya: 3 nights** (1, 2, 3 Jan 2027; arrival Fri 1 Jan, depart Mon 4 Jan).
- **Tiruvannamalai: 4 nights** (15, 16, 17, 18 Jan 2027; arrival Fri 15 Jan, depart Tue 19 Jan).

This matches the split already shown in the `INDIA19_Kloktijdplanning_A79` PDF Mark received earlier in the project (arrival Bodh Gaya 1 Jan, departure 4 Jan), rather than the 2-night/5-night default that `governance/CURRENT_TRUTH.md`'s skeleton table had been showing provisionally. Mark's own memory of the PDF surfaced this; it was not a new preference invented in this session.

## WHY THIS KEEPS THE REST OF THE SKELETON UNCHANGED

The 33-night frame (19 Dec–20 Jan), the Chennai buffer (19 Jan), and the final Delhi night (20 Jan) are fixed and do not move. Moving one night from Tiruvannamalai to Bodh Gaya shifts only the internal dates between them:

- Varanasi arrival shifts from Sun 3 Jan to **Mon 4 Jan**; Varanasi's own 8-night length is unchanged, so it now runs **4–11 Jan** instead of 3–10 Jan.
- Kolkata arrival shifts from Mon 11 Jan to **Tue 12 Jan**; Kolkata's own 3-night length is unchanged, so it now runs **12–14 Jan** instead of 11–13 Jan.
- Tiruvannamalai arrival shifts from Thu 14 Jan to **Fri 15 Jan**, and its length drops from 5 to 4 nights, so it still departs on **Tue 19 Jan** — exactly the same date as before this change.
- Chennai buffer (19 Jan) and final Delhi night (20 Jan) are therefore **completely unaffected**.

- `governance/CURRENT_TRUTH.md`'s 33-night skeleton table, Bodh Gaya section, and Tiruvannamalai section are updated to reflect this lock.
