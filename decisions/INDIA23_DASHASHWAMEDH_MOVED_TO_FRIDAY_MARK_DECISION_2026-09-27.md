## CONTEXT

Two connected problems Mark identified in the reconciled plan:
1. Friday's new morning content (Sankat Mochan/Durga/Lalita/Nepali, 07:00–10:45) is compact, then the entire rest of the day (10:45 onward) was unstructured reserve — a lot of unused time on a day surrounded by heavy days.
2. Wednesday ended at 20:15 (Dashashwamedh Ghat + Ganga Aarti tail), and Thursday starts at 05:45 — under 10 hours between returning to the hotel and having to wake up again, too short a turnaround between two substantive days.

## DECISION

**Dashashwamedh Ghat + Ganga Aarti moves from Wednesday evening to Friday evening.** This uses Friday's real dead time and removes Wednesday's latest, longest tail, giving genuine rest before Thursday's early start.

**New Wednesday end:** ends after Maa Annapurna Temple (~16:00) + return (~20 min) ≈ **~16:20–16:30 hotel return**, instead of 20:15. Gap to Thursday's 05:45 wake grows from ~9.5 hours to **~13 hours**.

## WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT FIT, NOT JUST A SWAP

Dashashwamedh's Ganga Aarti happens every evening — it was never date-locked to Wednesday specifically, only thematically paired with that day's other Lahiri content. Placing it on Friday instead is actually more coherent: Friday has no early start the next morning (departure is that same night by train), so a late evening block costs nothing there, whereas on Wednesday it directly caused the short Wed-Thu turnaround Mark flagged. The AOAY/Kriya recognition (Babaji-Mataji-Lahiri-Ram Gopal) stays fully attached to the place in all documentation — moving the calendar day does not weaken it.
