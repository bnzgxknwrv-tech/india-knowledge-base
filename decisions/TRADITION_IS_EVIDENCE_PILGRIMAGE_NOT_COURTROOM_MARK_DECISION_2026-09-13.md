## THE RULE

1. **Devotional tradition (overlevering) is sufficient evidence in itself for a pilgrimage.** A place does not need historical/textual/archival proof of a person's physical presence to be presented as a genuine, valuable connection. If tradition says Neem Karoli Baba had a kuti at Hanuman Garhi, or that a cave is Mahavatar Babaji's, that tradition IS the reason to go — it is not a placeholder for "real" evidence that happens to be missing.
2. **Never use language that frames tradition as weak, unconfirmed, or lesser.** Retire phrasing like "PLAUSIBLE, not CONFIRMED", "weak/matig evidence", "not independently verified", "no direct proof". Do not grade or rank places by evidentiary strength as if this were a legal or academic standard.
3. **Present the tradition straightforwardly**: what the tradition says, whose tradition it is (temple's own claim, a specific lineage's claim, general regional devotional belief, etc.) if known — not to hedge it, but to give it its own recognizable texture.
4. **This does NOT mean inventing connections that don't exist.** If genuinely no tradition or claim links a place to any of Mark's priority persons (e.g. `A050` Sankatha Devi Temple — no known tradition connects it to anyone in Mark's Top-X), say that plainly; do not manufacture one. The rule changes how *existing* tradition is treated, not license to fabricate.
5. **A real factual contradiction is still worth surfacing — but framed as a correction, not a doubt about tradition itself.** Example: presenting Yogananda's AOAY-documented meeting with Anandamayi Ma as having happened at the Bhadaini ashram, when the text itself places it in Calcutta/Ranchi, is a factual error to fix (wrong location for a specific documented episode) — this is different from questioning whether Anandamayi Ma herself had a real connection to Bhadaini (she did; that part is tradition/repeated-residence and needs no defense).
6. **This applies retroactively** to the existing `A79_PERSON_PROVENANCE_RESTORATION.csv`/`.md` and `WORK_A79_RELEVANCE_MATRIX.csv` framing (their `EVIDENCE_STRENGTH` / hedging language) and to every future Mark-facing place card, PDF, or A/A+ review presentation.
