# MARK DECISION — NIRMAL DHAM MOVES TO FINAL DELHI BUFFER

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: MARK_DECISION / LATEST OVERRIDE

## Decision

Nirmal Dham / Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi remains IN and retains its existing grade/status, but its default calendar placement changes.

- DO NOT plan Nirmal Dham on arrival day 19 Dec 2026.
- DO NOT consume arrival-day recovery or onward-travel margin for Nirmal Dham.
- PLAN Nirmal Dham during the final Delhi safety/buffer period, normally on 20 Jan 2027 after the domestic arrival/positioning into Delhi and before the final Delhi hotel night / AI155 next day.
- The final international-flight safety function remains higher priority than sightseeing/content. If disruption on 20 Jan consumes the buffer, Nirmal Dham may be shortened or skipped operationally rather than weakening AI155 protection.

## Route-planning consequence

19 Dec 2026 is now free to be optimized solely for the best whole-trip onward movement after AI156 arrival at ~10:15. Optimizers must no longer penalize same-day onward flight/rail options because they would miss Nirmal Dham on arrival day.

This means direct same-day continuation candidates such as DEL->GAY, DEL->VNS, DEL->CCU, DEL->MAA, or direct onward movement toward Kumaon must be compared without an arrival-day Nirmal Dham constraint.

## Supersession

This supersedes any older itinerary wording that places Nirmal Dham on 19 Dec 2026 before the Gurugram/Kumaon train or other onward travel.

It does NOT change:
- Nirmal Dham's subjective grade/status;
- the exact-one-final-Delhi-night Mark lock;
- AI155 on 21 Jan 2027;
- FINAL OUT worlds;
- any other Mark grade or duration lock.
