## DECISION

After the Thursday/Friday content reorder (locked same day, see below), two remaining Varanasi days were both under 4 hours of active time: the southern/Assi cluster day (~3u15) and the Kedar Ghat + Bhrigu Karyalaya day (~3u30). Mark chose to merge these two into a single day rather than keep them separate:

**Merged day: southern/Assi cluster (Assi–Tulsi dawn walk, Anandamayi Ma Ashram Bhadaini) → Kedar Ghat/Kedareshwar Temple → New Bhrigu Karyalaya/Bhadury Sadan, Ramapura-Luxa** — geographically plausible as one continuous route (Assi/Bhadaini in the south, Kedar Ghat is a natural continuation north along the same ghats, Ramapura-Luxa a further inland extension for the Bhrigu appointment), estimated ~6-7 hours combined active time, still lighter than the Lahiri/AOAY day (~7u45).

Varanasi drops from **6 nights to 5 nights** (4–8 Jan 2027; arrival Mon 4 Jan, depart Sat 9 Jan).

**Day structure (content order, exact clock times still pending the WORK/CCI dual-solve below):**
1. Mon 4 Jan — arrival
2. Tue 5 Jan — merged day (southern cluster + Kedar Ghat + Bhrigu Karyalaya)
3. Wed 6 Jan — Lahiri Mahasaya/AOAY core day → Dashashwamedh Aarti
4. Thu 7 Jan — Manikarnika/cremation-ghats (protected, open-ended)
5. Fri 8 Jan — reserve/buffer day, KEPT (not cut) — see reasoning below
6. Sat 9 Jan — depart to Kolkata

## THE RESERVE DAY IS KEPT, NOT CUT

Mark also asked whether the reserve/buffer day itself could be cut (going to 4 nights). CCI's assessment, which Mark accepted: the reserve day's original justification (Bhrigu appointment timing uncertainty + Manikarnika's open-ended dwell both risking collision with departure) is weaker now that Bhrigu moved earlier in the stay, but the Varanasi→Kolkata transport leg (~01:10 preliminary departure) is still unverified. Keeping the reserve day preserves a real safety margin until that transport leg is confirmed. This is the deliberate "golden middle" — 5 nights, not 4.

## WHAT HAPPENS TO THE FREED NIGHT

Combined with the earlier Sarnath cut and first reserve-day cut, Varanasi has now freed **3 nights total** relative to the original 8-night baseline. These flow to Kolkata by the same calendar-arithmetic mechanism as before (Tiruvannamalai's 15-Jan arrival stays fixed): **Kolkata's default grows from 3 to 6 nights.** CCI's assessment (accepted by Mark): 6 nights comfortably fits Kolkata's current locked content (6× A+: Dakshineswar Kali Temple, Yogoda Satsanga Math Dakshineswar, Kalighat Kali Temple, Vivekananda Birthplace, Cossipore/Kashipur Udyanbati, Balaram Mandir; 2× A: Belur Math, Shyampukur Bati; the full Garpar microcluster lock; Serampore as a day trip) without being wasteful, and specifically gives Dakshineswar itself (Ramakrishna's room, twelve Shiva temples) room to be done properly rather than rushed. This is a default, not yet Kolkata's own clock-time decision — that is separate, later, cluster-by-cluster work.
