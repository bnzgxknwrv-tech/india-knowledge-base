# MARK DECISION — KAKRIGHAT MUST ALWAYS BE PRESENTED AS TWO DISTINCT SUBPLACES

Date: 2026-09-16
Status: BINDING MARK DECISION
Scope: all future Mark-facing itinerary, travel-guide, PDF, dayplan, handoff, CURRENT_TRUTH summaries, successor-session summaries, maps, and route presentations.

## Decision

Kakrighat may NEVER again be presented as a single generic stop or as only one of its two core spiritual layers.

Whenever Kakrighat appears in a Mark-facing output, both of the following must be named separately and visibly as TWO DISTINCT PHYSICAL SUBPLACES within the Kakrighat cluster:

1. **Swami Vivekananda Jnana Vriksha / Karkateshwar Mahadev side**
   - Vivekananda layer.
   - Preserve the recognition hook: Vivekananda's microcosm–macrocosm realization/meditation association.

2. **Neem Karoli Baba Kakrighat Dham / Hanuman-temple-ashram side**
   - Neem Karoli Baba / Maharajji layer.
   - Preserve the Hanuman-idol/temple association and the Sombari (Somwari) Baba / Panjabi Baba shared-sacred-place context where evidentially supported.

## Hard presentation rule

- NEVER collapse these into one label such as only `Kakrighat`, only `Jnana Vriksha`, or only `Neem Karoli Baba Ashram`.
- NEVER let one of the two disappear because the cluster is already represented by the other.
- A single travel stop is allowed operationally if the two subplaces are close enough, but the guide/dayplan must still show them as TWO separate things to visit.
- If later microtopology research proves a more precise official name, entrance, coordinate, or distance, update the exact labels/details but preserve the dual-subplace rule.
- This rule exists specifically to prevent successor/handoff semantic loss.

## Route status

The exact best date/placement (23 Dec Nainital→Dunagiri/Kukuchina versus 29 Dec Haidakhan→Kathgodam) remains under active CCI research. This decision does NOT prejudge that routing outcome.
