Status: BINDING CURRENT TRUTH — narrow amendment to `decisions/INDIA22_HAIDAKHAN_GHAZIABAD_GREATER_NOIDA_VRINDAVAN_AGRA_DEFINITIVE_LOCK_2026-09-23.md`, which listed Madan Mohan Temple among stops not to add. That lock's "exactly two fixed A+ stops" and "no broader Braj/Mathura reopening" remain fully intact; this decision adds a zero-cost optional reserve only, using the same semantics already established for Dhokaney Waterfall / Lolark Kund / the Agra A013–A015 food bycatch.

## DECISION

Mark restores **Madan Mohan Temple, Vrindavan** (direct Yogananda/AOAY temple, previously graded A — `runs/active/INDIA8-ALL-FINDINGS-LOCATION-CLOSURE-001/VRINDAVAN_MARK_DECISIONS_V2.md` item 2) to **A\***, conditional corridor-bycatch.

Mark's own reasoning, in substance: he finds the temple visually beautiful and worth seeing, but does not want any stress or planning pressure around it, because — unlike Katyayani Peeth and the Neem Karoli Baba Vrindavan Ashram — it is not a site tied to his own spiritual lineage. It is purely an opportunistic extra, only worth taking if the day has ample slack and the temple is genuinely nearby at that point, not something to plan for or feel obligated to reach.

## OPERATIONAL RULE

- Madan Mohan Temple is **NOT** part of the timed/scheduled base itinerary for 30 Dec.
- **Zero planned time** is allocated to it in the base schedule; the day must not depend on it, be restructured around it, or have its Agra arrival target pushed to accommodate it.
- It must be **visibly shown** on 30 Dec as an optional reserve, to be judged **on the day itself, after both fixed A+ visits** (Katyayani Peeth, then Neem Karoli Baba Vrindavan Ashram) — never before or between them, and never at the cost of shortening either A+ dwell.
- Recognition text for any Mark-facing rendering must state plainly: real AOAY/Yogananda site, genuinely attractive, but explicitly zero-priority and only worth a look if time and proximity make it effectively free.

## EFFECT

- Madan Mohan Temple is graded **A\*** and visible as an optional reserve on 30 Dec, after the two locked A+ stops.
- Does not change the fixed 30-Dec chain (07:00 depart Greater Noida → Katyayani Peeth → Neem Karoli Baba Vrindavan Ashram → Agra), its timing, or the "no broader Braj/Mathura reopening" rule from the 23-Sep lock.
- Does not reopen Anandamayi Ma Vrindavan Ashram, Nidhivan, or any other Braj/Mathura candidate — this decision is scoped to Madan Mohan Temple only.
