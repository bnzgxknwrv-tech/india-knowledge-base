## Existing canon recovered

The current planning canon already protects the Bodh Gaya pilgrimage sequence:

`Mahabodhi -> Sujata -> Dungeshwari (+ ancient Pragbodhi/Dungeshwari ridge) WALK OUT / PREARRANGED CAR BACK`

This is not a newly invented route. It was already part of the retained Bodh Gaya day architecture and must remain visible in future Mark-facing overviews, PDFs and day cards.

## Why it matters

The route connects the principal physical landscape of Siddhartha Gautama's transition from extreme austerity toward enlightenment:
- Dungeshwari / Mahakala caves: the ascetic-period landscape before enlightenment;
- Sujata / Bakraur: the milk-rice offering and return to nourishment / Middle Way narrative;
- Niranjana / Neranjara (modern local river system commonly rendered Phalgu/Lilajan in mapping/tourism context): the river landscape separating Bakraur/Sujata from the Bodh Gaya sacred core;
- Mahabodhi / Bodhi Tree: enlightenment locus.

For Mark, the walk is therefore not merely transport between A-sites. It is an embodied pilgrimage through one connected Buddha-life landscape and must be described as such.

## Historical direction versus practical itinerary direction

The broad biographical sequence runs from the Dungeshwari austerity period, down toward the river/Sujata episode, and then to the Bodh Gaya enlightenment locus.

The current practical itinerary is deliberately the reverse/outward execution:
`Mahabodhi -> Sujata -> Dungeshwari -> prearranged car back`

Do not describe that practical direction as proof that Buddha walked the exact same route in that direction. Describe it as a present-day pilgrimage walk linking the same sacred life landscape.

## River / pedestrian-access rule

The Sujata site is across the river from the Mahabodhi core and the wider Dungeshwari-Sujata pilgrimage landscape includes a river crossing. The exact safe pedestrian crossing/ford/bridge to use on the travel date is operational and must be checked live; do not let that access detail erase the walk itself from the overview.
