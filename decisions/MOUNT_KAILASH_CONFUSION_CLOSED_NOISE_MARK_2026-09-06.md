## What happened

Mark read a reference to "Kailash" somewhere and asked whether a visit was
possible. On checking, this turned out to be the Tibetan Mount Kailash — a
different country (Tibet/China), not reachable within this itinerary — and a
different Kailash entirely from the small "Kumaon Mount Kailash"
hill/temple that sits at the foot of the Haidakhan Babaji cave (which
remains part of the Kumaon/Haidakhan visit on its own, unrelated merits —
not affected by this closure).

Mark's own words: "Ik las ergens kailash en wilde daarheen heb ik gevraagd
of dat kon. Bleek heel ander land te zijn en een andere kailash. Vergeet
die hele kailash. Ruis."

## Decision

The Tibetan Mount Kailash / Adi Kailash / Om Parvat / Badrinath question is
CLOSED as noise — not a real candidate for this trip, not to be tracked as
an open "deferred" item, and not to be re-raised in future research or
comparison surfaces. This supersedes the earlier "deferred, not rejected"
framing: it is simply closed.

This does NOT affect the separate, in-scope Kumaon Mount Kailash
hill/temple beside the Haidakhan cave — that stays part of the Haidakhan
visit under its own existing research, untouched by this note.
