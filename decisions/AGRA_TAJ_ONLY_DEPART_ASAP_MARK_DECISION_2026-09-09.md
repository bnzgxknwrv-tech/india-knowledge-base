# MARK DECISION — AGRA TAJ ONLY, DEPART AS SOON AS A GOOD ONWARD CONNECTION EXISTS

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: MARK_DECISION / CURRENT

## Decision

Agra has only one protected fixed-core visit for Mark: **Taj Mahal [A+]**.

Operational rule:
- Visit the Taj Mahal as early as practical on the Agra morning, ideally around opening/sunrise.
- After the Taj, DO NOT pad the day with optional Agra sightseeing merely to fill time.
- Leave Agra as soon as the first genuinely good onward connection permits.
- Agra Fort, Mehtab Bagh and other Agra sights remain optional/non-required unless Mark later explicitly promotes them.

## Important transport nuance

The current strong onward edge remains train 12988, currently Agra Fort about 18:45 -> Gaya about 07:50. Its value is that the long eastbound movement is largely overnight/sleepable.

Therefore a long post-Taj daytime gap in Agra is not automatically sightseeing time; it may simply be waiting/recovery before the objectively best sleeper connection.

Do not call that gap a reason to add more Agra content. Instead:
1. test whether an earlier same-day onward train/flight/road option is materially better door-to-door;
2. if not, preserve 12988 and use the daytime gap for hotel/day-room rest, meals and recovery before the sleeper.

## Constraint

Do not sacrifice a meaningful Taj Mahal visit or create a worse daytime-heavy connection just to leave Agra earlier by the clock.

Shorthand:
`AGRA_FIXED_CORE = TAJ_ONLY`
`POST_TAJ = DEPART_ASAP_ON_BEST_WHOLE_HUMAN_CONNECTION; NO_FILLER_SIGHTSEEING`
