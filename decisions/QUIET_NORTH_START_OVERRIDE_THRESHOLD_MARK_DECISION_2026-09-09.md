# MARK DECISION — QUIET NORTH START PREFERENCE + OVERRIDE THRESHOLD

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: MARK_DECISION / ROUTE TIE-BREAK + OVERRIDE RULE
Branch: `agent/india8-cluster-casting`

## Decision

Mark explicitly prefers to START the India trip calmly in the north/Kumaon world rather than begin in a high-intensity city such as Varanasi.

This is not an absolute hard lock against all other first destinations, but it is a strong route preference that should control whenever the objective travel advantage of an alternative is only modest.

### Practical rule

- Default/leading human-experience start = **Kumaon / north India first**.
- Varanasi-first, Gaya-first, Kolkata-first, Chennai-first or any other same-day onward flight from Delhi may override that preference ONLY if the alternative produces a **clearly material whole-trip benefit**.
- A gain of only a few waking travel hours (roughly 2–5h) is NOT enough to justify abandoning the calm north-first start.
- The intended override threshold is approximately **a real half to full usable day of net trip value**, e.g. about 8–10+ waking hours saved, or equivalent substantial gain in usable day-content / recovery / safety that survives whole-trip accounting.
- Do not use spectacular paper savings that disappear after airport buffers, luggage, sleep disruption, extra transfer days or risk.

## Consequence for current Varanasi-first challenger

The currently discussed Varanasi-first route appears to offer at most a modest waking-hour advantage over Kumaon-first, plus a better hotel first night, but it starts the trip in a city Mark considers too intense for his preferred opening.

Therefore, under this new explicit Mark preference:

`KUMAON_FIRST = LEADING_PREFERENCE`

`VARANASI_FIRST = CHALLENGER_ONLY_UNLESS_MATERIAL_GAIN_PROVEN`

The fact that Varanasi-first may be technically feasible or even slightly faster is not sufficient by itself.

## Interaction with Nirmal Dham override

Nirmal Dham remains moved to the final Delhi buffer, normally 20 Jan. This decision does NOT restore Nirmal Dham to 19 Dec.

19 Dec remains free for onward movement, but the preferred onward movement is now toward a calm north/Kumaon start unless a different first destination demonstrates a materially superior whole-trip result above the threshold described here.

## Planning shorthand

`START_PREFERENCE = QUIET_NORTH_KUMAON`

`OVERRIDE_ONLY_IF = MATERIAL_NET_GAIN (~8-10+ waking hours / real half-to-full usable day or equivalent higher-tier benefit)`

Do not treat 2–5h modeled savings as sufficient.
