## TRANSFER
From **Bodh Gaya — Buddha's enlightenment city with the Bodhi Tree and Mahabodhi Temple (Bihar)** to **Varanasi — sacred Ganges city with ghats and cremation culture (Uttar Pradesh)**.

## MARK DECISION
**TRAIN PREFERRED OVER PRIVATE CAR.**

Reason from Mark:
- the road transfer is long;
- sitting 5+ hours in a car is not pleasant;
- train is more comfortable even when total door-to-door time is similar or somewhat longer;
- therefore prefer a practical direct train rather than private car.

Operational hierarchy:
1. Prefer a **direct daytime train** from **Gaya Junction — main railway station serving Bodh Gaya (Gaya, Bihar)** to **Varanasi Junction / Banaras — railway arrival for Varanasi (Uttar Pradesh)**.
2. Current strong candidate as of 2026-08-29: **20887 Vande Bharat Express**, currently Gaya Junction 09:55 -> Varanasi Junction 13:00, ~3h05, running on six days per week according to current timetable aggregators. This is LIVE/PROVISIONAL until exact travel date is frozen and Indian Railways/IRCTC schedule + class availability are rechecked.
3. Prefer best practical comfort class available on the selected daytime service; do not force 1A if the train type does not offer it and a comfortable seated premium class is the natural product.
4. Private car is fallback only if the exact-date train is operationally poor, unavailable, disrupted, or creates clearly worse total burden.
5. No night train preferred for this short edge unless exact-date circumstances make all daytime options materially worse.
