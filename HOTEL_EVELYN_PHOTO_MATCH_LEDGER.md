## Match rules

- `HISTORIC_ANCHOR` — dated/identified historic Hotel Evelyn evidence.
- `CURRENT_COMPARATOR` — modern building image/material useful for geometry only.
- `TEXT_ONLY_ANCHOR` — physically specific historical testimony without usable image.
- `FALSE_FRIEND` — image/event relevant to Ram Dass/Nainital but explicitly not Hotel Evelyn.
- `NEGATIVE_SEARCH` — requested class of evidence searched but not surfaced in usable form.

Match confidence:
- `MATCHED_ZONE` — historic zone identified, current exact fabric not necessarily proven.
- `POSSIBLE_SUCCESSOR` — current place is geometrically/functionally plausible but continuity is unproved.
- `NO_MATCH` — insufficient common features/evidence for one-to-one match.
- `EXCLUDED` — wrong property/event.

# Structural comparison synthesis

## Historic P01 background

The 1971 Nainital High image contains enough architecture to prove that the portrait occurred against Hotel Evelyn structures, but not enough uniquely persistent geometry to identify a modern room bay. The visually useful historic markers are:

1. pale/cream wall planes;
2. tall multi-pane window(s), at least one with a stronger colored/red-toned frame in available scan;
3. low/shallow projecting roof/canopy;
4. trees/hillside directly behind;
5. rough/gravel terrace foreground.

## Modern structure

Modern imagery shows:

1. a much larger apparent multi-storey façade;
2. long white-railed open galleries/balconies;
3. broad curved/stepped chalet-like roof and façade lines;
4. substantial terrace/common outdoor space;
5. lake-facing balcony/view function still central to the property.

## Safe inference

`Hotel Evelyn main-terrace / front-veranda complex` is a defensible **historic zone**. The modern principal terrace is the best **successor candidate** because it occupies the same functional relation — open hotel gathering/viewing space against the façade — and is explicitly identified as “hotel terrace” in current photo captions.

## Unsafe inference

The following are prohibited by the evidence currently available:

- “this exact window is behind Ram Dass in 1971”;
- “this third-floor room is his room”;
- “the balcony passage proves he slept in a balcony room”;
- “the current terrace paving is the original 1971 terrace”;
- “the K.K./Ram Dass/Bhagavan Das rooftop photo was taken at Evelyn”;
- “Maharajji stayed at Evelyn.”
