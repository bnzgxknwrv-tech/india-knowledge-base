## HARD RULES THAT GO WITH THE TEMPLATE

1. **Every field that applies must be filled with real content**, not a placeholder or a one-word answer. "Geen lineage-link" alone is never a complete `WAAROM`-answer — always say what the actual reason for inclusion is.
2. **Never a bare Indian name.** Every occurrence — including recaps, tables, "verplaatst naar"-notes and burden tables — uses the full recognition format from `MARK_LOCATION_NAMING_CONTEXT_PROTOCOL.md`.
3. **No breakfast/wake/shower clock items.** The operational day starts at `VERTREK HOTEL` per `MARK_DAY_BLOCK_ONLY_PDF_RULE_2026-09-13.md`.
4. **One day = one complete block.** Nothing decision-critical lives only in a front-matter/appendix/burden-table-only note.
5. **Lead with meaning, not logistics.** Practical reality (queues, security, access) comes AFTER the spiritual/experiential content, never before it.
6. **Original WHY, not invented WHY.** Before filling `WIE WAS HIER / WAT GEBEURDE HIER`, search the owning research (grade-provenance files, AOAY/person sweeps, decision files) for why the place actually entered the retained set — do not fabricate a person-link that was never the real reason, and do not flatten a real one into silence.
7. **Photos where appearance matters and one is available**, per FOUT 5 of the successor handoff.

## PRE-SEND TEST — MANDATORY, EVERY LOCATION, EVERY TIME

Before sending any Mark-facing presentation containing locations, for EACH location ask:
- Can Mark answer, from the card alone: what is this, why might I personally care, what will I see/do/feel, what would I miss if I skipped it?
- Is there a real Dutch recognition hook, not a bare Indian name?
- Is the person/event link stated, or explicitly confirmed absent with a real alternative reason given?
- Is the exact sub-place (room/kuti/cave/ghat-zone) named where known?
- Are both time-accounting fields present where a predecessor/successor exists?
- Does the day read as one complete block with no breakfast/wake items?

If any answer is NO, the card is not ready. Fix it before sending — do not send a "I'll add detail if you want" version.
