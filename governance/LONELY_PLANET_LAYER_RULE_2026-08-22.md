# LONELY PLANET LAYER — MARK TRIP RULE
Date: 2026-08-22
Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose
Maintain a third independent discovery layer alongside:
1. PERSON layer — Top-X people, biographies, AOAY/person events.
2. SPIRITUAL / BUILDING / ANCHOR layer — major temples, samadhis, shrines, sacred complexes, historic religious anchors.
3. LONELY PLANET layer — major non-spiritual or not-necessarily-spiritual travel magnets that would still justify attention during an India trip.

## Definition
A Lonely Planet candidate is NOT merely 'nice nearby'. It should be nationally, internationally, or strongly regionally exceptional: a place or experience that many travelers deliberately seek out because it is unusually beautiful, rare, historic, iconic, extreme, atmospheric, culinary, architectural or natural.

Examples include:
- outstanding natural landscapes, waterfalls, caves, lakes, rivers, canyons, viewpoints, wildlife or geological features;
- nationally iconic monuments, museums, historic districts, palaces, forts, engineering works;
- records/superlatives: oldest, highest, largest, clearest, deepest, rarest, etc., when genuinely meaningful;
- exceptional food/café/bakery/confectionery institutions with real regional or national reputation/history;
- highly distinctive local experiences, markets, trains, walks, crafts or heritage sites;
- world-class photographic or scenic locations.

## Threshold
Do NOT flood the trip with generic attractions.
- 'best waterfall in a random town' is insufficient.
- Candidate should normally be one of the strongest attractions in the wider region, India-significant, internationally notable, or exceptionally aligned with Mark's interests.
- Prefer fewer high-signal findings over many ordinary sightseeing suggestions.

## Mark preferences relevant to this layer
- Strong preference for nature and visually exceptional places.
- Water, waterfalls, caves, mountains, exceptionally clear/blue water and unusual landscapes are high-interest.
- Characterful historic cafés, bakeries, confectioners and food institutions can qualify when genuinely special.
- The layer has no requirement to be spiritual.

## Route interaction
Do NOT search all India exhaustively before route skeleton exists.
First apply the layer to:
- locked/likely A clusters;
- B/reserve clusters only enough to determine whether a major non-spiritual magnet materially changes their cluster weight;
- direct transfer corridors between likely A clusters.

A major Lonely Planet magnet may:
- strengthen a marginal cluster from B to A;
- justify a stopover en route;
- add a half-day/day to an already selected cluster;
- rarely reopen an otherwise dropped region if the attraction is genuinely exceptional.

## Presentation
For each candidate tell Mark:
- WHAT it is;
- WHY it is exceptional beyond local level;
- whether people deliberately travel for it;
- practical proximity to the spiritual/person cluster or transfer route;
- whether it costs minutes, half a day, a day, or a major detour;
- no automatic A/B/C recommendation unless requested or needed for cluster-level triage.
