# MARK PERSON-PROVENANCE / PLACE-MEANING RULE

Status: HARD / USER-PRESENTATION / SUCCESSOR-REQUIRED
Effective: 2026-09-13
Branch: `agent/india8-cluster-casting`

## WHY THIS RULE EXISTS

Mark's India trip is primarily a spiritual/pilgrimage journey through physical places connected with people, events, lineages and sacred landscapes that matter to him. It is NOT a forensic court case, skeptical debunking exercise or burden-of-proof contest.

Research rigor exists to prevent real mistakes: wrong place, wrong person, wrong historic site, inaccessible target, modern successor confused with the original, or a materially false story. Research rigor must NOT flatten, cool or litigate the spiritual meaning of a place.

The governing Mark-facing question is:

`WHY WOULD MARK WANT TO BE HERE, WHO/WHAT MAKES THIS PLACE SPIRITUALLY MEANINGFUL, WHAT HAPPENED HERE, AND WHAT SHOULD HE ACTUALLY SEEK OUT OR SIT WITH?`

## PRIMARY PRESENTATION RULE

For every A+/A/A* place, lead with the strongest positive spiritual/biographical meaning in ordinary Dutch.

Examples:
- Do not lead with `Hanuman temple`; lead with `Maharajji founded/stayed here; seek the Maharajji kuti and sit there.`
- Do not lead with `large Shiva temple`; lead with `this is where young Ramana disappeared into deep samadhi in the Patala Lingam vault.`
- Do not lead with `ashram associated with Shri Mataji`; lead with `this is Shri Mataji's samadhi/mausoleum.`
- Do not lead with `Buddhist monument`; lead with `this marks the Sujata milk-rice episode / first sermon / enlightenment landscape`, as applicable.
- If a place is visually/experientially distinctive, say that immediately too: e.g. `the bell temple`, `the leaning temple`, `the cremation ghat`, `the cave reached by mountain climb`.

Indian names are never assumed to mean anything to Mark.

## HARD DISTINCTION: INTERNAL AUDIT VS MARK-FACING PDF

### Internal research/audit layer
CCI/WORK/INDIA may keep:
- confidence fields;
- source conflicts;
- skeptical challenges;
- strongest counterarguments;
- modern-vs-historic distinctions;
- provenance weaknesses;
- unresolved source disputes.

These are useful backstage for quality control.

### Mark-facing layer
The Mark-facing PDF MUST NOT contain a routine field such as:
- `STERKSTE TEGENARGUMENT`;
- `CASE AGAINST OVERVALUING`;
- courtroom-like burden-of-proof prose;
- repetitive `not proven / plausible but not definitive` caveats where the location is the accepted spiritual/pilgrimage site and there is no serious alternative-location problem.

The PDF should communicate spiritual meaning, lived connection, sacred tradition, practical relevance and the felt reason to be there.

## WHEN UNCERTAINTY SHOULD BE SHOWN TO MARK

Only elevate uncertainty prominently when it can materially change Mark's decision or prevent a real mistake, for example:

1. WRONG PLACE RISK — credible evidence suggests the event happened somewhere else.
2. WRONG PERSON RISK — a person-link has been misattributed.
3. MODERN SUCCESSOR VS HISTORIC SITE — the present center/memorial is not the historic house/room/event site and the distinction matters.
4. ACCESS RISK — the meaningful room/house/cave/samadhi is probably inaccessible during Mark's dates.
5. IDENTITY CONFUSION — e.g. Haidakhan Babaji must not be presented as automatically identical to Mahavatar Babaji.
6. COMPETING PHYSICAL SITES — there are genuinely multiple candidate locations and choosing the wrong one matters.

Even then, write neutrally and constructively.

Prefer:
`Dit is de traditioneel aanvaarde plek; exacte historische pinpointing is niet mogelijk.`

not:
`Bewijs slechts plausibel / niet definitief.`

Prefer:
`De huidige YSS-locatie is een levende opvolger; Yogananda's historische huis is A064.`

not:
`Directe link onvoldoende bewezen.`

## DO NOT CONFUSE SPIRITUAL TRADITION WITH ERROR

For Babaji, Buddha, ancient saints, sacred caves, samadhi traditions and similar pilgrimage material, tradition itself can be the legitimate reason to go.

If a lineage has long treated a cave, tree, ghat, shrine or mountain point as the relevant place, the Mark-facing card may say so affirmatively:

`Binnen de Kriya-traditie is dit de grot van de Babaji-Lahiri ontmoeting en initiatie.`

A short nuance may be added only if useful:

`De moderne locatie-identificatie berust op lijntraditie; AOAY zelf noemt Drongiri Mountain en niet de huidige trailhead-naam.`

That nuance must not dominate the card.

## REQUIRED MARK-FACING FIELDS PER PLACE

Every future detailed PDF/day-card for an A+/A item should contain, where applicable:

1. `WAT IS DIT IN GEWOON NEDERLANDS?`
   Explain the physical place and its most recognizable feature. If it is the bell temple, say `bell temple`.

2. `WAAROM IS DIT BELANGRIJK VOOR JOU?`
   State the spiritual/personal reason first.

3. `WIE WAS HIER / WAT GEBEURDE HIER?`
   Name priority persons and the concrete event: lived, taught, meditated, met, initiated, wrote, visited, attained realization, died, cremated, ashes/samadhi, etc.

4. `WAT MOET JE HIER PRECIES ZOEKEN?`
   Name the room, kuti, cave, tree, samadhi, ghat zone, shrine, bench, house, vault or practice space if known.

5. `HOE WIL JE HIER ZIJN?`
   Distinguish natural visit style: kijken, bezoek, 30 min zitten, 60+ min zitten, of echt pelgrimsblok. Presence and silence are valid goals even when little is visually spectacular.

6. `UNIEK HERKENNINGSPUNT`
   Include the physical/experiential feature that makes the place recognizable and memorable: bells, underground vault, leaning temple, cremation fires, mountain cave, exact room, etc.

7. `KORTE NUANCE — ALLEEN ALS DIE ERTOE DOET`
   One short sentence only when needed to prevent a material misunderstanding. No routine skepticism field.

8. `VAN VORIGE / NAAR VOLGENDE A`
   Preserve distance/reistime so spiritual value and physical burden remain visible together.

## ORIGINAL WHY / PROVENANCE

Before rebuilding a Mark-facing PDF from a compact ledger, INDIA must search backward through the owning research, AOAY/person sweeps, Mark grading/decision records and earlier discovery files to recover why the place entered the retained set.

If a place was retained because of a person/event, that must be visible.
If it was retained for experience, nature, architecture, food, ritual or recovery instead, say that plainly without inventing a person-link.

## ABSOLUTE DISTINCTION THAT DOES MATTER

A later temple, ashram, memorial or institution `in the name of` a person is not automatically equivalent to a place where that person physically lived, taught, meditated, founded the site, died, was cremated or had a major event.

Show this distinction ONLY when it materially affects what Mark is going there for.

Examples:
- modern Babaji memorial near the cave: say `modern memorial; the cave is the actual pilgrimage anchor`;
- current YSS Garpar center vs Yogananda family house: keep both distinct;
- modern Mulagandha Kuti Vihara vs ancient Buddha residence foundations: do not merge them.

## DECISION SAFETY

Mark must never be asked to skip, downgrade, shorten or remove an A/A+ based on a card that omits its person/event provenance or unique experiential identity.

Before pace-based A->B review:

`PERSON_PROVENANCE_RESTORED_FOR_ALL_A_PLUS_A = YES`

and

`UNIQUE_PLACE_IDENTITY_RESTORED_FOR_ALL_A_PLUS_A = YES`

must both be true.

Independent relevance challenge may exist internally, but its adversarial fields are NOT automatically user-facing.

## PRESENTATION TONE

The tone should be spiritually literate, concrete, respectful and affirmative without inventing facts.

Do not sound like a prosecutor trying to disprove the site's sacred meaning.
Do not reduce accepted pilgrimage tradition to `unproven` unless there is a real competing-location or factual problem.
Do not let caution become the main message.

Good:
`Binnen de Kriya-traditie is dit de grot waar Babaji Lahiri Mahasaya initieerde. Dit is een van de centrale pelgrimsplekken van de hele reis.`

Bad:
`De claim is niet definitief bewezen en berust slechts op traditie.`

Good:
`Hanuman Garhi is voor jou vooral Maharajji-geografie: hij stichtte de plek en de kuti is het doelpunt; de tempel zelf is secundair.`

Bad:
`Bewijs voor de specifieke kamer is plausibel maar niet definitief.`

## INTERNAL RELEVANCE CHALLENGE RULE

CCI/WORK may still produce `CASE FOR`, `CASE AGAINST`, evidence-strength and adversarial findings internally. Those remain valuable for quality control and reconciliation.

INDIA must translate that internal audit into a Mark-facing spiritual presentation, surfacing only the parts that materially change meaning, location, identity, access or decision safety.

## SUCCESSOR TEST

A successor must be able to answer for every retained A+/A:
- why is this important to Mark personally/spiritually?
- which person/event anchors it?
- what happened there?
- what exact sub-place should Mark seek out?
- what is the unique recognizable feature?
- how should Mark spend time there?
- is there any material caveat that truly changes the visit?
- where in GitHub is the original reason/grade provenance?

If the answer is buried under skepticism, the Mark-facing description is wrong even if the underlying research is technically accurate.
