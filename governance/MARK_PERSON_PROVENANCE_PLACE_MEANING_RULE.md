# MARK PERSON-PROVENANCE / PLACE-MEANING RULE

Status: HARD / USER-PRESENTATION / SUCCESSOR-REQUIRED
Effective: 2026-09-11
Branch: `agent/india8-cluster-casting`

## WHY THIS RULE EXISTS

Mark's India trip is not primarily temple-, monument-, architecture- or checklist-driven. The core value is being physically present at places that have a real, evidenced connection to the people, lives, events and lineages that matter to him.

A place description that says only `Hanuman temple`, `ashram`, `ghat`, `monastery`, `house`, `cave`, `stupa`, etc. is insufficient and can materially distort Mark's choices. It can make an A/A+ location look generic even when the real reason it was graded highly is that an important person lived there, taught there, meditated there, met someone there, was born there, died there, has samadhi/ashes there, had a decisive spiritual event there, or repeatedly used the site.

The governing question is therefore not merely `WHAT IS THIS PLACE?` but:

`WHICH PERSON OR EVENT MAKES THIS PHYSICAL PLACE IMPORTANT TO MARK, WHAT EXACTLY HAPPENED HERE, HOW DIRECT IS THE LINK, AND WHY DID MARK ORIGINALLY RETAIN/GRADE IT?`

## PRIORITY

For every Mark-facing A+, A, A* or serious decision candidate, restore and show the complete person/event provenance before Mark is asked to judge tempo, duration, deletion, downgrade or visit priority.

This includes, but is not limited to:
- Autobiography of a Yogi / AOAY-linked persons and exact sites;
- Mark's established Top-X person priority set in current canon;
- Neem Karoli Baba / Maharajji and Ram Dass-related physical-life sites;
- Buddha and exact life/event sites;
- Anandamayi Ma, Ramakrishna, Ramana Maharshi and other retained high-priority persons;
- exact birth, residence, teaching, practice, meeting, enlightenment, samadhi, cremation/ashes, cave, kuti, ashram, temple or institutional sites where the person actually had a documented physical relationship.

## REQUIRED FIELDS PER PLACE

Every future detailed PDF/day-card/decision-card for an A+/A item must contain, where applicable:

1. `WAT IS HET FYSIEK?`
   Plain Dutch explanation; never assume the Indian name means anything to Mark.

2. `BELANGRIJKE PERSOON/PERSONEN`
   Name every relevant priority person explicitly.

3. `WAT GEBEURDE HIER PRECIES?`
   Concrete historical/biographical event(s): born, lived, stayed, taught, meditated, met, initiated, wrote, visited repeatedly, attained realization/enlightenment, died, cremated, ashes/samadhi, etc.

4. `WAS DE PERSOON HIER DAADWERKELIJK?`
   Explicit answer: YES / PROBABLY / TRADITION-CLAIM / NO-DIRECT-PRESENCE / UNKNOWN.

5. `LINK-TYPE`
   One or more of:
   - EXACT_LIFE_SITE
   - EXACT_EVENT_SITE
   - EXACT_RESIDENCE
   - EXACT_TEACHING_SITE
   - EXACT_MEDITATION/PRACTICE_SITE
   - EXACT_BIRTH_SITE
   - EXACT_DEATH/SAMADHI/ASHES_SITE
   - EXACT_MEETING/INITIATION_SITE
   - REPEATED_VISIT_SITE
   - PERSON_FOUNDED_SITE
   - LINEAGE_INSTITUTION
   - MODERN_MEMORIAL_ONLY
   - NAME_ONLY/DEDICATION_ONLY
   - TRADITION_CLAIM
   - INDIRECT_CONTEXT

6. `HOE DIRECT IS DE LINK?`
   Explain the difference between an original physical-life site and a later memorial/institution/temple named after the person.

7. `WAAROM WIL MARK HIER ZIJN?`
   Recover the original reason from GitHub provenance/grade history. Do not invent a new rationale from the current itinerary.

8. `OORSPRONKELIJKE A/A+ REDEN / PROVENANCE`
   Point to the earlier research/decision/Mark-grade source(s) that explain why the place entered the retained set.

9. `WAT IS OP DE PLEK HET EIGENLIJKE DOELPUNT?`
   If the meaningful thing is a specific room, kuti, cave, tree, samadhi, ghat step, shrine, room, house, or meditation spot inside a larger complex, name that exact sub-place. Do not let the larger complex hide the real target.

10. `STILTE-/ZITTijd`
    If the value is presence rather than sightseeing, distinguish contemplative dwell from sightseeing dwell. A site may deserve 30–90+ minutes of simply sitting even if there is little to 'see'.

11. `BEWIJSSTERKTE / ONZEKERHEID`
    Separate well-documented physical presence from later devotional tradition, marketing, lineage association or uncertain identification.

12. `VAN VORIGE / NAAR VOLGENDE A`
    Keep the already-required distance/reistime context so spiritual value and practical burden can be judged together.

## INDEPENDENT RELEVANCE / RECOMMENDATION LAYER

Facts alone are not enough for the final Mark-facing surface. After the factual person-place provenance is reconstructed, there must also be an independent relevance analysis.

For every retained A+/A, INDIA/CCI/WORK must distinguish:

1. `FACTUAL PHYSICAL LINK`
   What is actually evidenced about person, place and event.

2. `CASE FOR MARK`
   The strongest reason this exact place may be personally valuable to Mark, especially if it is a true physical-life site of one of his priority people.

3. `CASE AGAINST OVERVALUING`
   The strongest reason the place may be less important than the current grade/presentation suggests: e.g. later memorial only, name-only dedication, indirect lineage association, weak/uncertain tradition, duplicated experience elsewhere, or little unique physical continuity.

4. `INDEPENDENT RELEVANCE SIGNAL`
   An advisory assessment such as VERY_STRONG / STRONG / MIXED / WEAK. This is NOT a Mark grade and may never autonomously change A+/A/A*/B/C.

5. `DWELL RECOMMENDATION`
   Explain whether the place is best treated as LOOK / VISIT / SIT_30M / SIT_60M_OR_MORE / PILGRIMAGE_BLOCK, and WHY. The recommendation should reflect the person-link and the likely value of simply being there, not merely sightseeing duration.

6. `NEW DISCOVERY`
   Any web-researched fact that materially changes the meaning of the place for Mark must be surfaced even if it was absent from the earlier GitHub record.

7. `CHALLENGE`
   If earlier GitHub wording materially overstates or understates the person-place link, explicitly challenge it with sources. Do not preserve a bad rationale merely because it is old canon; preserve Mark's grade while escalating the factual discrepancy.

This relevance layer must use both repository provenance and fresh external research where useful. The web pass should prefer primary/official lineage, ashram, temple, museum, archival and government sources, then strong biographies/scholarly sources. Secondary devotional/travel sources may support discovery but must not be promoted to certainty without qualification.

## ABSOLUTE DISTINCTION

A later temple, ashram, memorial or institution `in the name of` a person is NOT equivalent to a place where that person actually lived, taught, meditated, met someone, founded the site, died, was cremated, or had another documented physical event.

The PDF must say this explicitly when relevant.

Examples:
- `Neem Karoli Baba temple` with no proven physical-life link != a place Maharajji founded, stayed or taught.
- modern lineage centre != exact historic house/room.
- a memorial to Babaji != a cave/site tied by the project's evidence to the Babaji tradition as an AOAY event site.
- a generic Buddhist temple != an exact Buddha-life/event site such as enlightenment, Sujata episode, first sermon, etc.

## SOURCE-RESTORATION RULE

Before rebuilding a Mark-facing PDF from a reduced ledger, INDIA must not rely only on the compact current ledger text. For every A+/A, it must search backward through the owning research, AOAY/person sweeps, discovery reports, Mark grading/decision records and relevant historical provenance to recover the original `WHY`.

If the original reason cannot be recovered, label that as a data/provenance gap. Do NOT silently replace it with a generic tourist description.

## DECISION SAFETY

Mark must never be asked to skip, downgrade, shorten or remove an A/A+ based on a card that omits its person/event provenance. Such a decision surface is invalid.

Before any pace-based A→B proposal or Mark tempo review:

`PERSON_PROVENANCE_RESTORED_FOR_ALL_A_PLUS_A = YES`

must be true.

For the fully decision-safe surface, also require:

`INDEPENDENT_RELEVANCE_CHALLENGE_COMPLETE = YES`

so Mark sees not only the restored historical reason but also the strongest sourced argument for and against the location's present personal relevance.

## PRESENTATION PRINCIPLE

For Mark, the important sentence is often not:

`Dit is een Hanuman-tempel.`

but:

`Neem Karoli Baba stichtte deze plek, verbleef hier, en dit terrein behoort tot zijn eigen fysieke levensgeografie; de tempel zelf is secundair aan die persoonslink.`

Likewise, when an ashram was the central home/base of a person for years, write `DE belangrijkste/centrale ashram of levensbasis` when the evidence supports that, not merely `een ashram`.

## SUCCESSOR TEST

A successor must be able to answer for every retained A+/A:

- why is this important to Mark personally?
- which person/event anchors it?
- was that person physically here?
- what exactly happened here?
- what exact sub-place should Mark seek out?
- is this an original site or a later memorial/institution?
- where in GitHub is the original reason/grade provenance?
- what is the strongest sourced case FOR Mark spending time here?
- what is the strongest sourced case AGAINST overvaluing it?
- what dwell style fits the true person-place meaning?

If any answer is missing, the Mark-facing place description is incomplete.
