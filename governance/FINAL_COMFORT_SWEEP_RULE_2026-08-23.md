# FINAL COMFORT SWEEP — HARD TRAVEL PHASE

Date: 2026-08-23
Status: MANDATORY AFTER ROUTE / NIGHTS ARE STABLE

## PURPOSE
After clusters, sleep bases, route order and approximate nights are fixed, run a separate COMFORT SWEEP per retained sleep base / day corridor.

This phase is intentionally deferred until geography is stable. It must NOT be forgotten.

## REQUIRED SEARCH CATEGORIES
Per sleep base / day area, research at minimum:
- historic / cult pastry shops and bakeries;
- local signature sweets and regional specialties;
- strongest banketbakker / patisserie-type stops;
- best coffee / serious coffee addresses;
- best restaurants by local relevance and traveler consensus;
- breakfast options where relevant;
- one or two exceptionally characterful cafes/tea houses;
- high-quality small food stops that are worth a short detour;
- practical comfort stops useful on long transfer days.

## USER-PREFERENCE WEIGHTING
Mark is especially sensitive to:
- old/historic pastry shops and bakeries;
- local sweet specialties;
- characterful pastry/cafe stops;
- genuinely good coffee;
- restaurants that are strong enough to justify being deliberately chosen rather than random convenience.

A tiny exceptional pastry shop may matter more than a generic major attraction.

## QUALITY RULE
Do not produce generic “top restaurants” lists. Use forum/community consensus, long-running local reputation, specialist food writing and current traveler signal. Prefer a small shortlist with reasons.

## OUTPUT REQUIREMENT
For each retained sleep base / corridor, create a compact comfort card with:
- name
- type
- exact area
- why special
- what specifically to order / try
- distance/time from sleep base or route
- opening risk / reservation need if relevant
- confidence / source notes

## ORDER OF OPERATIONS
HEAVY A CLUSTERS -> ROUTE/NIGHTS -> CALENDAR/CLOSURES -> HOTELS/BASES -> DAY STRUCTURE -> FINAL COMFORT SWEEP -> FINAL DAY CARDS.

A route is not considered final-travel-guide-ready until this comfort sweep has been completed for every retained base.
