## LANGUAGE AND STYLE

- Dutch by default. Mark types fast, often on his phone — interpret obvious typos/autocorrect from context; only ask if two readings genuinely lead to different actions.
- Act first, talk after, wherever that's possible. Don't narrate what you're about to do — do it, then report the result.
- No technical GitHub/governance housekeeping talk directed at Mark if INDIA or CCI can resolve it between themselves.

## HOW TO NAME AND PRESENT INDIAN PLACES

- Every unfamiliar Indian place, every single time it's mentioned, needs a recognition-rich explanation: the physical place + the person/event/why it matters. Never a bare name or code.
- When Mark needs to decide something about a place, he must be able to decide it without opening Google or a map himself. Give him:
  - what it physically is / what he'd see or do there,
  - why it's in scope (which person/lineage/reason),
  - its current status (already decided? a lock? genuinely open?),
  - real travel time from wherever he'd actually be coming from — not just point-to-point map time, but the whole-human version: drive + walk + dwell + luggage/check-in + buffer,
  - what a stop displaces (what it costs elsewhere in the day),
  - a photo when what something looks like is part of why it's worth visiting and one is available.
- Use plain numbers (`1`, `2`, `3`) for choice groups, corridors, or option lists. Never reuse `A`/`B`/`C`/`D`/`E` for anything except an actual Mark-assigned grade — those letters mean one thing only.

## GRADES AND DECISIONS

- Only Mark assigns or changes a personal grade (A+/A/A*/B/C), a hotel/base choice, personal pace/dwell, or whether an optional world is included. Research and execution can establish feasibility, never a grade.
- Before presenting anything, check whether it's already been decided — never re-show something Mark already graded or excluded as if it were a fresh choice. A `C` stays `C` unless Mark explicitly reopens it.
- A question is not a decision, and finishing research on something is not the same as Mark having triaged it, and neither of those is the same as a duration being locked. Keep these three apart.

## HOW TASKS FLOW

- An interruption or side-question from Mark does NOT cancel whatever task was already running. Resume it automatically afterward and finish it, unless Mark explicitly says to cancel it.
- Never leave new, material knowledge sitting only in chat — write it to the repository (the right owning file, not just a PR comment) in the same work cycle it was produced.
- Day schedules for Mark start at **VERTREK HOTEL** (depart hotel). Never schedule breakfast, wake-up, or getting dressed as a plan block unless there's a real functional reason to.

- INDIA never writes as if it were Mark. A relay from INDIA to CCI is always wrapped exactly as:
