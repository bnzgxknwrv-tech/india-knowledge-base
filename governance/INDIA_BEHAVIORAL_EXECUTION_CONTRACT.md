### 5A. DISTANCE-FIRST DECISION SURFACE — HARD / ABSOLUTE
Mark cannot be expected to know Indian geography. Therefore A/B/C/A*/A+ judgment is burden-sensitive and the geography must be supplied BEFORE Mark is asked to judge.

For every actionable location in a cluster presentation, show enough geometry for Mark to understand the real choice without knowing the map himself:
- km + conservative travel time from selected hotel/sleep base when materially relevant;
- km + conservative travel time from nearest/logical retained A+ or A;
- km + conservative travel time from immediately preceding/following stop in proposed bundle;
- walking realism + walking km/time when relevant;
- taxi/auto/private car need;
- logical `JE BENT ER TOCH` combinations;
- reverse case when an attractive item actually creates a new half/full day or major backtrack.

Use conservative operational times, not optimistic map minima. The minimum presentation surface is a small distance matrix / logical-combination map sufficient to see what is cheap, what bundles naturally and what creates extra travel.

## 6. PARENT / MICROCLUSTER VETO
One physical compound/site/ashram/temple complex/historic property normally gets one Mark decision. Rooms, shrines, trees, platforms, courtyards, caves and other same-site microsites remain nested unless they require a materially separate visit/access/detour.

Do not make Mark score the same physical terrain repeatedly.

## 7. HISTORIC-CONTINUITY VETO
Keep separate event certainty, site/grounds continuity, structure continuity and exact microplace continuity.

## 8. LOSSLESS / NO-SILENT-DROP VETO

## 9. SEQUENCE-AWARE GEOGRAPHY VETO
Never judge a non-local place only by distance from hotel/city centre.

Before classifying it as detour/half-day/day, determine actual predecessor, successor, retained sleep base/anchor, incoming/outgoing corridor, natural companions, direct baseline transfer, route via candidate, NET extra km/time/human burden, backtracking and transfer-day capture.

A site 70 km from the hotel can be cheap if it lies on a required transfer. A nearer site can be expensive if it points the wrong way. Never infer corridor fit from straight-line distance.

## 10. WHOLE-HUMAN-TIME VETO
On-site dwell is NOT the attraction's trip cost.

Where decision-relevant include hotel/base departure/return or transfer relationship, road/rail/flight access, loading/check-out/check-in, waiting/security/baggage, traffic/fog/winter buffer, driver/baggage repositioning, walking/ropeway/boat access, food/toilet/rest and usable daylight/energy.

Never present `1.5 h visit` when the real choice consumes a 6–8 h excursion. If a day only works under perfect assumptions, label it OVERLOADED.
