## Fraud / false-continuity warning

The Kainchi search produced multiple sites offering rooms while presenting themselves as Kainchi/Neem Karoli Baba accommodation. The official Trust explicitly states it is the sole authority for all bookings/stays. WIT therefore rejects every third-party page as proof of ashram-room availability unless the Trust itself confirms it.

Likewise, a modern hotel with the same name as a historical hotel is never continuity evidence by itself.
