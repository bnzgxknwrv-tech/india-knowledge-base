## Guardrail

A modern room that occupies approximately the same floor or wing is not automatically the historic room. Required proof for `SAME_HISTORIC_ROOM = JA`: archival register/floorplan, institution/owner written confirmation, or a preserved-room designation with traceable historical provenance.
