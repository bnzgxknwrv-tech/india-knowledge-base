## Interpretation

A `HERITAGE_STAY` candidate requires evidence that the person actually slept/lived/stayed at the place, not merely visited. Private residences, ashram rooms, hermitages, inns, hotels, huts and guest houses are retained even when they are not commercially bookable today. `BOOKABLE_SAME_ROOM` is never inferred from same-site continuity.
