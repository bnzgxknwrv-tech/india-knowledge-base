## Executive conclusion

The physical destination can be identified with high confidence as **Haidakhan Vishwa Mahadham, P.O. Haidakhan, District Nainital, Uttarakhand 263126**, the Haidakhandi Samaj main ashram/temple complex on the **Gautami Ganga / Gaula river**, not the separate Anandpuri/Chilianaula (Ranikhet) complex.

The best-supported map position for the Haidakhan complex is approximately **29.24728 N, 79.65585 E** (OSM way 676012408; plus code 7JXX6MW4+W8). A second independently surfaced Google-derived place listing uses **6MW4+QCW** for a Haidakhan Babaji Ashram component on the same complex/riverbank. Those pins are close enough to represent different points/components of the same ashram terrain, not a Ranikhet site. The official postal code is **263126**; generic map/listing results showing **263139** should not override the official ashram postal address.

Kathgodam Railway Station is independently fixed at about **29.2666 N, 79.5467 E** (OSM node 919678285 / GeoNames / Wikidata; geotagged Wikimedia station photo gives 29.2651,79.545414).

The straight-line separation is only about 11 km, but the river/mountain geometry prevents treating that as road distance. Evidence converges on a **motor-road journey in roughly the 27–40 km class**, with practical local sources clustering around **35–40/45 km** and **~1.5 h or more**, rather than 90 km. For travel planning, WORK does **not** accept the 44-minute generic-calculator time. A prudent December working allowance is **2 h**, with **2 h 30 min contingency** when a train must be protected, plus station buffer.

The official Haidakhandi Samaj **~90 km** figure is very likely a website/data error rather than the physical Kathgodam→Vishwa Mahadham road distance. The strongest internal evidence is that its “How to Reach” page gives the same ~90 km railhead distance and ~120 km Pantnagar distance to both the Ranikhet/Chilianaula Anandapuri Ashram and Haidakhan Vishwa Mahadham, while their Delhi road distances differ (373 vs 337 km). Independent Ranikhet sources put the Ranikhet Haidakhan/Chilianaula temple roughly 80–90 km from Kathgodam. This strongly suggests copy/reuse of the Ranikhet rail-distance block into the Haidakhan section.

## Phase 3 — road reconstruction

### Endpoints

Start: Vishwa Mahadham complex, approx. **29.24728,79.65585**.  
Finish: Kathgodam Railway Station, approx. **29.26665,79.54664** (OSM); geotagged Wikimedia station image **29.26510,79.545414**.

### Physical corridor

The historical river approach begins Kathgodam → Ranibag → Jamrani side; older descriptions distinguish a river route and mountain route. A modern state road now exists: **Kathgodam–Haidakhan–Sanniband–Similiya Bend Motor Road (SH-103)**.

This is not speculative: an active Uttarakhand PWD/BIIU tender published Aug/Sep 2026 is specifically for landslide treatment at **km 2.025–2.295 on SH-103**, naming the road “Kathgodam Haidakhan Sanniband Similiya bend Motor Road”. Another 2026 tender references a bridge at **km 48 (ch. 47.900)** on the same named SH-103, showing it is a substantial through state-highway corridor, not a 7-km local lane.

Relevant current evidence:
- https://uttarakhandhub.com/tenders/2026-biiu0-99503-1
- https://tenderdetail.com/Indian-Tenders/TenderNotice/57146934/3726aa1b00b04e143fbe23821a221733

The 2026 landslide-treatment tender is operationally important: the Kathgodam end of this road has a known chronic slope/landslide problem. December is outside monsoon, but a train-critical plan should still check road status locally shortly before travel.

### Distance/time triangulation

Evidence classes:
- Generic calculator: **27 km / 44 min** to “Haidakhan Babaji Ashram, Chhakhata Range 263139”. The coordinate identity is broadly compatible, but 44 min is too optimistic for planning because the source is generic and does not model local mountain-road/roadwork uncertainty robustly.
- Local taxi operator: **35 km / 1.5 h** Kathgodam→Haidakhan Babaji Temple.
- Independent tourism/photo source: **~35 km from Haldwani**, motorable road.
- ToursByLocals guide: **40 km from Haldwani-Kathgodam by road** to Haidakhan Vishwa Mahadham; also describes the older Jamrani river-hike route.
- Tour package source: **45 km from Kathgodam**.
- Official Haidakhandi Samaj: **~90 km**, but this is internally suspect for the copy/reuse reason above.

WORK therefore freezes the **distance class**, not a fake single-number precision:
**normal motor-road order: ~30–40 km; allow up to ~45 km depending on exact vehicle pickup point/routing.**
The evidence does not support 90 km as the normal Kathgodam↔Village Haidakhan Vishwa Mahadham road distance.

Normal practical driving expectation: **~1.5–2 h**.
Train-protection planning allowance in December: **2 h**, preferably **2 h 30 min road contingency** when missing the train would materially damage the itinerary.

## Phase 4 — source-conflict explanation

**27–32 km:** likely automated shortest motor route to a Chhakhata Range Haidakhan Babaji Ashram pin. The destination is not obviously the wrong Ranikhet ashram, but the time estimate is algorithmically optimistic.

**35–50 km:** this is the strongest practical band. Multiple independent/local travel sources identify the correct river-valley Haidakhan/Vishwa Mahadham and give 35, 40 or 45 km. Differences plausibly arise from Haldwani vs Kathgodam origin, exact ashram-side/entrance endpoint, and route choice.

**~90 km:** almost certainly contaminated by **Anandapuri/Chilianaula/Ranikhet** information or duplicated website content. The official page itself repeats the same ~90 km and ~120 km figures under both distinct ashrams. Independent Ranikhet material independently produces the ~80–90 km Kathgodam distance expected for the Ranikhet site.
