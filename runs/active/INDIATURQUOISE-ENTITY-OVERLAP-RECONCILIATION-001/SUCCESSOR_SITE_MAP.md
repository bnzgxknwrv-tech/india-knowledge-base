## Explicit non-successor cases

- Serampore railway station is not a successor of Sri Yukteswar’s ashram.
- Bhowanipur disciple-house event is not a predecessor of Ranchi campus; they are separate Yogananda–Anandamayi events.
- Karar Ashram rooms are micro-sites inside the same complex, not successors of one another.
- Prayagraj Kumbh event zones across different years are repeated-use geographic/event areas, not one unchanged physical building.
- Kolkata metropolitan lineage sites are separate physical entities, not successor stages of one site.

## Interpretation

A successor relation preserves historical continuity without deleting either endpoint. Downstream route/ABC agents must not treat a modern memorial as proof that the historical person occupied the modern structure unless the source layer explicitly establishes structural continuity.
