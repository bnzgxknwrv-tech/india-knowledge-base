# Ramakrishna — India physical-location atlas — PRE-COMPARE FREEZE

**Run:** `TOP11-PARALLEL-CHATGPT-SWEEP-001`  
**Freeze date:** 2026-08-18  
**Discovery mode:** independent external blind sweep; no internal person atlas, CCI output, prior candidate list, reconciliation file, or other person's candidates were consulted.  
**Scope:** verifiable physical places in present-day India where Sri Ramakrishna (Gadadhar Chattopadhyay, 1836–1886) was personally present, plus a very small number of direct posthumous lineage/physical-remains sites explicitly marked `NEE`. Recall is favoured over precision; unresolved physical identities are retained rather than silently discarded.

## Evidence and normalization rules

- `PERSONALLY_PRESENT` means the source independently supports presence; a later institution or a place merely mentioned in discourse does not.
- `PHYSICAL_IDENTITY` uses exactly `EXACT`, `DEELS`, `ALLEEN_PLAATS`, or `ONBEKEND`. `EXACT` means a defensible surviving building/site or uniquely identified physical feature, not necessarily unchanged fabric. `DEELS` means a known complex/footprint but an altered, vanished, or only approximately localized subsite. `ALLEEN_PLAATS` means the city/village/route is known but not the concrete premises. `ONBEKEND` means the source names a host or type of premises but the physical identity has not been independently fixed.
- Contemporary street addresses are included only where an official/institutional or explicit site-history source supports continuity; none are inferred.
- Dates in the Gospel are observation dates unless the speaker is recalling an earlier visit.
