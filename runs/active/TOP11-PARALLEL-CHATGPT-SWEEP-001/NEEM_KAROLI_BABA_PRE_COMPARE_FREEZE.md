## Adversarial non-upgrades

- Institutional lists name more than one hundred temples, but only locations with separate personal-presence evidence are `JA`.
- Panki is deliberately `ONZEKER`: the close devotee memoir explicitly says he declined bodily attendance at the opening. A miracle narrative is not substituted for physical travel.
- The current Rishikesh/Veerbhadra complex was substantially built after 1984; his lease/plans alone do not prove he visited its precise parcel.
- The 2001 Akbarpur temple and Vrindavan Mahasamadhi Mandir are later memorial structures. Birth/cremation-site associations do not establish lifetime presence inside those later buildings.
- India Hotel, Nainital is attached to devotee Tularam in the swept memoir, but no clean passage placed Maharajji inside it; it is not included as a presence row.
