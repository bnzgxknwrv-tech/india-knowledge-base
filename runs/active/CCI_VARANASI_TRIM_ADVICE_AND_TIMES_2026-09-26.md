## CRITICAL NEW FINDING — CHANGES THE LAHIRI MAHASAYA DAY

**Lahiri Mahasaya's original/family house (A039, [A+]) is only open to visitors once a year, on Guru Purnima (a June/July full-moon day). It is closed the rest of the year, including January.** This is new information not previously in the repo (which only flagged "January interior access must be confirmed" as unverified).

The good news: **Lahiri Mahasaya Samadhi / Satyalok (A040, [A])** is a *different, separately visitable* site — a live temple with a large Shiva Lingam, statues including one of Lahiri Mahasaya, pictures of various yogis, and — importantly — a small adjoining cave-temple dedicated to **Babaji**, described by a visitor as "a perfect place to sit in meditation." This is regularly open, not Guru-Purnima-gated.

**My recommendation**: do not plan on entering A039's interior in January — treat it as an exterior/photo stop only if you pass nearby, and put the real time and meditation focus into **A040 Satyalok**, which gives you a genuine sit-and-meditate Babaji space anyway. This doesn't lose the lineage content, it relocates it to the site that's actually open.

## SARNATH (separate town day — Buddhist world, not your Kriya/AOAY lineage, but a fixed A+ parent)

Mahabodhi/Bodh Gaya already carries your Buddhist-history content; Sarnath is a genuinely separate historical layer (Buddha's first sermon, not enlightenment). If you need to shorten the *trip*, not just a day, this whole day (not individual stops within it) is the honest place to ask "do I need both Bodh Gaya's depth and a full Sarnath day" — but that's a bigger question than trimming Varanasi legs, and Sarnath's parent is currently A+, so cutting it is a real grade question for you, not a scheduling nudge from me.
