Therefore no one-change rail path beats either 12988 or 12320.

### Flight and road controls

- Current flight searches show **no nonstop Agra–Gaya and no nonstop Agra–Patna** service. Connecting products are typically 7+ hours airborne-system time before airport transfers; the usable routings do not beat a one-seat overnight train and can require a geographically absurd Bengaluru/Kolkata connection.
- Backtracking 220+ km to Delhi airport after the Taj adds about 4–6 whole-human hours before airport process and still does not create a robust same-evening Bodh Gaya arrival.
- Agra to Bodh Gaya is roughly **850–865 road km**. Map engines show 10.5–11.5 hours of wheel time, but a safe whole-human private-car plan is about **13–15 hours** with meals, toilet, traffic and driver margins. It converts a sleeper rail night into a long waking car day and conflicts directly with Mark's train preference.

The official Mahabodhi Temple information places Gaya Junction about **17 km** from Bodh Gaya. Plan **45–60 minutes** from platform arrival through exit, driver rendezvous and road, plus hotel check-in:

- 12988 scheduled 07:50 → realistic hotel **08:45–09:15** if reasonably punctual;

No surviving post-Taj path gets Mark into a Bodh Gaya bed on the evening of 31 December. The true choice is therefore the quality of the overnight move, not “wait in Agra versus sleep in Bodh Gaya that night.”
