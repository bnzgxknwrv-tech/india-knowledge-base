## 2. LIVE OPERATIONAL CONTEXT

### Mahabodhi hours and why walking distance matters

The current official Bodhgaya Temple Management Committee schedule says Mahabodhi is open daily from **05:00 to 21:00**, with morning chanting/meditation 05:30–06:00. A hotel that is truly walkable to the public security/visitor approach therefore makes a 05:00 start and an unhurried evening return materially easier.

Official source:
- https://bodhgayatemple.com/temple-timing/

### Peak-season availability/noise warning

BTMC has published its 2026–27 annual puja calendar. The 38th Nyingma Monlam Chenmo is publicly announced for **8–17 January 2027**. Bodh Gaya's December–January period contains multiple major international prayer gatherings. This is spiritually valuable but means:
- room prices shown in August 2026 are not reliable winter quotes;
- quietness can change by exact room position and event dates;
- a refundable reservation should be made soon after the actual Bodh Gaya dates and hotel choice are known;
- exact event dates and hotel inventory should be rechecked at booking.

Sources:

## 3. WALKING-DISTANCE METHOD / ENTRANCE TRAP

The comparison target is the **usable public Mahabodhi security/visitor approach**, not the temple spire, site centroid or closest outer wall.

Evidence combined:
- exact official property addresses;
- official hotel map embeds / public Google hotel-map records;
- OTA walking-route estimates where they explicitly say walk, not straight-line distance;
- recent traveler reports that describe the actually used path;
- conservative route ranges when two current platforms disagree.

Important correction:
- some listings say Maya Heritage is “30 metres” from Mahabodhi. That is not a defensible door-to-public-entrance walk. Other current route surfaces give about 7–13 minutes. The lower marketing number was rejected.
- the temple has boundary walls/security access and the closest map point is not automatically an entrance.
- Google Maps' interactive directions page did not render reliably in the research browser. Therefore no invented turn-by-turn precision is used. Only candidates with converging address + walking-route + traveler evidence survive the final shortlist.

Distance status in this report:
- `HIGH`: explicit practical walking time supported by more than one current surface/reviewer.
- `MEDIUM`: route is clearly walkable but exact entrance distance varies between sources.

### 4.1 NIRANJANA HOTEL BODHGAYA — degelijk 68-kamerhotel tegenover het archeologisch museum in de tempelkern

- **Official name:** Niranjana Hotel Bodhgaya.
- **Address:** Opposite Archaeological Museum, Domuhan–Bodhgaya Road, Bodh Gaya, Bihar 824231.
- **Practical Mahabodhi walk:** about **600–750 m / 8–10 min** via the museum/Thai-Temple side to the public visitor approach. Official “300 m from Mahabodhi” describes proximity to the complex and should not be read as a guaranteed 300 m entrance walk. Confidence `HIGH`.
- **Character:** calm, conventional mid-range pilgrimage hotel; more hotel-like and less personal than a guesthouse; tour-group/business capacity but current reviews still repeatedly describe quiet rooms.
- **Room/bathroom:** official room sizes 26–47 m² depending type; private bathrooms; some balconies; premium bedding/large beds on some types. Current positive pattern: spacious, clean, comfortable, sometimes “spotless.” Negative pattern: isolated dusty-room reports and hot water that can take several minutes. Oil heater in some rooms / heater on request is a concrete winter advantage, but must be confirmed for the booked room.
- **Food:** Sujata Restaurant on site, open for breakfast/lunch/dinner; broad Indian/Asian/international menu. Breakfast receives strong recent signals, but inclusion depends on rate.
- **Very early Mahabodhi:** 24-hour front desk; a 04:50 departure for the 05:00 opening is operationally credible. Ask for room away from lift/restaurant/road.
- **Taxi/baggage:** main-road property with on-site parking and airport/rail pickup on request; taxi can reach the hotel door. Temple-side vehicle restrictions do not harm hotel arrival.
- **Review pattern:** Booking 8.6/10 from about 200 reviews in the current result set; current positives centre on staff, cleanliness, breakfast, spacious rooms and location. Main risks are age/maintenance variation and delayed hot water, not a structural location problem.
- **Current public price order:** roughly `MID-RANGE`; public 2026 listings show about US$39–40 class before winter-event repricing. Treat only as order of magnitude.
- **Bookability:** official direct booking plus Booking/Expedia/Trip listings are live. Exact Dec 2026/Jan 2027 inventory was not defensibly visible without exact cluster dates.
- **Special risk:** large property can receive groups; quiet-room allocation matters during Monlam/puja weeks.
- **Verdict:** strongest all-round balance for multiple nights.

- **Review pattern:** Booking 9.2/10 from roughly 293 reviews in the current result set; repeated positives are owner/staff warmth, cleanliness, hot water, food, safety and quiet. Main negatives: compact rooms, alley/approach, occasional older bathroom/renovation concern.
