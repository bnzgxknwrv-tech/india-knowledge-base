## 8. TIRU4 AS LIVED — WHAT IS ACTUALLY LOST VS. THE 5N INCUMBENT

Under 5 nights, arrival (Thu 14 Jan) is a pure travel day with no forced content, and Fri 15 Jan — the Sri Chakra Puja day — gets a full day built around it, arriving fresh the night before. Under 4 nights, arrival lands **on** Friday 15 Jan itself. With an aggressively early CCU→MAA flight, arrival at Tiruvannamalai could be as early as ~12:30–13:30, which technically leaves a window before the 18:00 puja — so **it is not flatly impossible**, contrary to how starkly I put it in the earlier same-day stress test. But it still means: flight + immigration + 3.5–4.5h road transfer + check-in, then a 2h45 evening programme, all on the same day, with no fallback if the flight is even moderately late. That is a materially more taxing day than the 5-night version's "arrive, rest, sleep" — and Mon 18 Jan simultaneously has to absorb the content block that the 5-night version gave its own dedicated day, while also being final-packing day. **Net effect: Tiru4 is content-complete, but it removes the one clearly protected recovery slot the 5-night version had (its flexible Day 5), and creates the single most demanding day of the entire 33-night trip.** This is the concrete, lived version of the finding from the earlier stress test — sharper now that an optimistic early-flight assumption has been tested and still doesn't fully resolve it.
