## KOL-NEW-50-AMHERST — 50 Amherst Street, Kolkata — **OPEN**
Official YSS says this is where Yogananda's mother died while the family was living there. Master Mahasaya later lived here for years. YSS additionally records Yogananda and his brother having a vision of their mother while meditating here with Master Mahasaya.

This is direct Yogananda + mother + Master Mahasaya + meditation-event geography and must be shown to Mark rather than silently omitted.

## KOL-NEW-NAGENDRA-MATH — Nagendra Math / Bhaduri Mahasaya house — **OPEN**
Official YSS places Nagendra Math roughly 0.5 km from the Garpar family home. Bhaduri Mahasaya is the `Levitating Saint` of *Autobiography of a Yogi*; Yogananda regularly stopped there after school for spiritual discussions. Current YSS wording says the house is open to devotees.

This is especially important as a classification-integrity issue because it is nearly next door to already-retained A+ Garpar geography.

## KOL-NEW-TULSI-BOSE — Tulsi Bose Shrine / historic Tulsi Bose family home — **OPEN**
This is a distinct physical property from both A064 4 Garpar Road and A065 YSS Garpar Dhyana Kendra.

Current Ananda Kolkata describes it as the preserved home of Yogananda's close boyhood friend Tulsi Bose, where Yogananda repeatedly meditated and where he stayed during his 1935–36 India return. The shrine preserves beds used by Yogananda and Sri Yukteswar, guru relics, and meditation-room/pilgrimage use. Current YSS independently says its Garpar centre is behind/adjacent to Tulsi Bose's house, confirming these are not the same entity.

This is a major classification omission and must be graded by Mark.
