**Wat is het fysiek?**
The surviving historic property of Sri Yukteswar's main Serampore hermitage, at/around 3A Buro Bibi Lane, now held by YSS with the Smriti Mandir on the grounds. YSS says it has acquired the complete hermitage property and intends to restore as much of the original hermitage as possible.

**Waarom is dit voor Mark belangrijk?**

SRF/YSS currently calls this one of the official **Serampore Pilgrimage Sites** and describes it as the place of Yogananda's life-transforming spiritual training.

**Concrete life-event layer:**
Yogananda received sannyas/swami vows from Sri Yukteswar in Serampore in July 1915. Sri Yukteswar's Serampore world is therefore one of the central physical guru-disciple geographies of Yogananda's formation.

**Wat doet Mark daar?**
Visit the Smriti Mandir / hermitage property, orient to the original guru-house geography, and deliberately sit/meditate rather than treat it as a quick memorial stop. Exact 2027 public-access zones need live confirmation.

**Recommended dwell:** 75–90 minutes.

## 2. RAI GHAT SACRED BANYAN — BABAJI APPEARS TO SRI YUKTESWAR AFTER *THE HOLY SCIENCE* [UNGRADED]

**Wat is het fysiek?**
A sacred banyan/riverfront micro-site in the Rai Ghat area, a short walk in the Sri Yukteswar hermitage world.

**Waarom is dit voor Mark belangrijk?**
In the AOAY/YSS tradition this is the tree/place where Mahavatar Babaji appeared to Sri Yukteswar in 1894 after completion of *The Holy Science*. This directly joins Babaji -> Sri Yukteswar -> Yogananda in one physical Serampore micro-location.

**Current institutional status:**
SRF/YSS explicitly treats it as a **Serampore Pilgrimage Site**. YSS has worked with Serampore municipality on the approach and acquired adjacent property to create/preserve a meditative environment.

**Wat doet Mark daar?**
Walk from the hermitage world toward Rai Ghat, find the YSS-recognized sacred banyan area, and sit quietly/meditate by the Ganges rather than simply photograph the tree.

## 3. ANANDALOKA / YSS SERAMPORE RETREAT — YOGANANDA'S ACTUAL STUDENT-ERA ROOM, NOW SHRINE [UNGRADED]

**Wat is het fysiek?**
The historic house of Yogananda's uncle Sarada Prasad Ghosh at **57 Netaji Subhas Avenue, Serampore**, now YSS Serampore Retreat / Dhyana Kendra. YSS identifies Yogananda's ground-floor student-era room as the shrine **Anandalok(a) — 'The Abode of Joy'**.

**Waarom is dit voor Mark belangrijk?**
This is not a successor center standing somewhere else. It is the actual family house in which young Yogananda stayed during his college years, with his room preserved as a shrine/meditation room. For Mark's preference for exact life-sites and meditation rooms, this is materially stronger than a generic institutional visit.

**Current physical continuity:**
Independent heritage inventory identifies the same historic Sarada Bhavan property at 57 Netaji Subhas Avenue as an old bungalow now under YSS ownership. YSS has operated the property as a retreat centre since 2012.

**Wat doet Mark daar?**
Enter the retreat if current visitor access permits, seek Anandaloka specifically, and meditate in Yogananda's preserved room. If a public group meditation/satsanga fits, that may be more valuable than a short architecture visit.

**Recommended dwell:** 60–90 minutes; longer only if joining an actual practice block.
