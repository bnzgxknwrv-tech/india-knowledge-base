## CONFLICTS
1. Gurumurtam chronology: accessible timeline labels the move under 1898, while Mahadevan’s narrative says “a little less than six months” after Sep 1896, implying 1897. Site presence is secure; exact month/year needs direct reconciliation against the cited early editions.
2. Tirukoilur journey micro-chronology varies slightly among later retellings (night allocation/train timing), but the sequence Villupuram → Mambalappattu → Araiyaninallur → Kilur/Tirukoilur → Tiruvannamalai is repeatedly supported.
3. Banyan-tree tradition: later devotional retellings can identify the seen tree with a puranic siddha-banyan; Ramana’s own 1946 account says he merely became curious on seeing a remarkable tree and did not set out to find the puranic tree. Freeze preserves the physical occurrence without endorsing the later identity claim.
