# PHOTO EVENT LEDGER — Anandamayi Ma × Paramahansa Yogananda

Research date: 2026-08-20
Scope: joint still photographs and film footage during Yogananda's 1935–36 India return. Crops/reproductions are deduplicated to underlying photographic events.

## Result
The scoped evidence supports **two joint image events**, not a larger set of independent locations:

1. **Bhowanipur, Calcutta — first meeting / automobile-street event** — joint imagery exists, including the widely reproduced Yogananda + Anandamayi portrait and a Yogananda + Anandamayi + Bholanath image. The official Anandamayi photo archive captions the latter "in Calcutta". AOAY fixes the encounter to the home of an unnamed disciple in the Bhowanipur section, with the actual first-contact scene in/at an open-topped automobile immediately outside/near that house. The Sangha chronology independently dates the Calcutta meeting to December 1935. **Exact host identity/property was not recovered from the scoped official/archival sources; disposition is explicit UNRESOLVED, not dropped.**

2. **Ranchi Vidyalaya — school main building + garden photographic session** — AOAY states that Anandamayi Ma visited Yogananda's boys' school at Ranchi, entered the main building, sat beside Yogananda, and then at Yogananda's request went **to the garden**, where Richard Wright took **many photographs**. This is the strongest physically resolved joint-photo event. A later official Anandamayi/SRF reminiscence says a motion-picture film of Anandamayi Ma and Yogananda existed from his 1936 India visit; no scoped source proves that the surviving film sequence is a third location. The dedicated 1930s-film archive labels a segment "Mâ & Paramahansa Yogananda" but supplies no location metadata. Because AOAY explicitly documents Wright photographing them in the Ranchi garden, Ranchi is the strongest provenance for at least the documented still-photo session; **film micro-location remains UNRESOLVED unless frame-level provenance emerges.**

## Event P01 — Bhowanipur first meeting
- Published image(s): widely reproduced two-person Yogananda/Anandamayi image; separate three-person Yogananda/Anandamayi/Bholanath image.
- Published presence: Paramahansa Yogananda, Anandamayi Ma; Bholanath in the three-person version.
- Date: December 1935 (Sangha chronology).
- Historical place: disciple's home, Bhowanipur section of Calcutta; street/open automobile at departure.
- Exact physical point: **UNRESOLVED**. Neither AOAY nor scoped official Sangha material names the disciple/host or street number.
- Resolution: **R4** — neighborhood and event secure; parcel/micro-site unresolved.
- Current identity/access 2026: Bhowanipore is public urban fabric; unknown historical host parcel cannot responsibly be marked visitable.
- Cluster relation: **CERTAIN_CLUSTER — Kolkata**, conditional on central cluster casting; governance makes it a `MUST_VISIT_WITHIN_INCLUDED_CLUSTER` candidate if the historical property is later resolved usefully.
- Evidence chain: AOAY/Sangha chapter text -> disciple's Bhowanipur home + automobile scene; Sangha life chronology -> December 1935 Calcutta meeting; Sangha photo archive -> joint image captioned Calcutta.
- Duplicate handling: web copies with erroneous 1910/1915 metadata are not treated as separate events; those dates conflict with the documented 1935 meeting.

## Event P02 — Ranchi Vidyalaya garden
- Published event: AOAY describes Anandamayi Ma at Ranchi school and explicitly says Richard Wright would take pictures in the garden; she posed for many photographs.
- Published presence: Paramahansa Yogananda and Anandamayi Ma; school party/devotees present around the visit.
- Date: after the December 1935 Calcutta meeting, during Yogananda's India return; best safe range late 1935 / early 1936.
- Historical place: Ranchi Vidyalaya, main building followed by **garden** photo session.
- Current identity: Yogoda Satsanga Vidyalaya / YSS Ranchi campus historical continuity; exact garden camera station not established.
- Resolution: **R2 for campus/garden event; R4 for exact camera position**.
- Current access 2026: managed YSS institutional campus; do not assume unrestricted school/garden access. Contact YSS before travel for heritage/photo-location access.
- Cluster relation: **CERTAIN_CLUSTER — Ranchi**, conditional on central cluster casting; `MUST_VISIT_WITHIN_INCLUDED_CLUSTER` candidate under governance.
- Evidence chain: AOAY chapter 45 -> Ranchi school -> main building -> explicit garden -> Wright photographs; official Sangha reproduction independently preserves same narrative.

## Event P03 — surviving 1930s motion-picture footage
- Media: archive segment titled "1930's Film — Mâ & Paramahansa Yogananda"; 1959 Daya Mata account says a motion picture film of Ma and Guruji was taken during his 1936 India visit.
- Presence: Anandamayi Ma + Paramahansa Yogananda.
- Date: described retrospectively as 1936.
- Physical place: **UNRESOLVED AT FRAME LEVEL**. Available metadata does not say Bhowanipur, Ranchi, or another site.
- Resolution: **R5 for film-specific micro-location**.
- Disposition: retain separately because moving footage is a distinct media event; do not invent a third physical site. Candidate linkage to Ranchi is plausible because AOAY documents an intentional photo session there, but not proven for the film.
- Cluster relation: **CANDIDATE_CLUSTER — Kolkata/Ranchi pending frame provenance**.

## Exhaustiveness / anti-drop
Scoped searches found no defensible third joint physical photo location. Serampore station is a later in-person encounter described in AOAY but no scoped source establishes a joint photograph there, so it is **not promoted into the photo ledger**. Likewise 1959 Daya Mata visits concern Anandamayi Ma after Yogananda's death and are not joint-photo events.
