This shortlist is not a destination decision. It identifies clusters that look travel-relevant because multiple project persons or multiple strong sites already converge there in durable data.

## Tier P1 — strongest overlap clusters already visible

### Prayagraj / Allahabad
Why it stands out: current conservative city-level lower bound is **5 unique persons**: Mahavatar Babaji, Sri Yukteswar, Lahiri Mahasaya, Paramahansa Yogananda and Neem Karoli Baba. The overlap is heterogeneous rather than one shared structure: 1894 Kumbh event, later Kumbh attendance, 4 Church Lane and station-related material. That makes the city strong as a cross-lineage junction while requiring strict subsite separation.

Travel-relevance signal: **VERY HIGH OVERLAP DENSITY**.
Decision status: **UNDECIDED_BY_MARK**.

### Varanasi / Kashi
Why it stands out: conservative lower bound **5 persons if Babaji tradition-presence is counted, 4 hard-historical persons without it**. Lahiri Mahasaya and Sri Yukteswar share a strict exact house overlap; Ramakrishna has a city-level pilgrimage link; Anandamayi Ma has an ashram/verblijf layer; Babaji has a reconciled tradition/source claim at Dashashwamedh Ghat. The city already has an existing deep repository candidate layer, so this is not a new destination recommendation but a strong integration signal.

Travel-relevance signal: **VERY HIGH, with strongest exact-site lineage overlap at Lahiri residence**.
Decision status: existing Mark-protected decisions remain untouched.

### Kolkata / Hooghly lineage belt
Why it stands out: Yogananda, Vivekananda, Ramakrishna and Anandamayi Ma all have durable metropolitan/near-metropolitan links; the broader West-Bengal belt additionally includes Sri Yukteswar, Serampore and Lahiri’s Nadia birth-region context. Strong identifiable sites include Dakshineswar, Cossipore, Vivekananda’s ancestral house and Serampore lineage locations.

Travel-relevance signal: **VERY HIGH REGION-CLUSTER DENSITY**, but this is a belt, not one walkable city-site.
Decision status: **UNDECIDED_BY_MARK**.

### Vrindavan
Why it stands out: **4-person lower bound**: Neem Karoli Baba, Ram Dass, Anandamayi Ma and Ramakrishna. NKB + Ram Dass converge on the exact NKB ashram/mahasamadhi complex; Ramakrishna’s Akrura Ghat is a separate site; Anandamayi has her own ashram layer.

Travel-relevance signal: **HIGH** because both cross-person exact overlap and separate person-linked sites coexist.
Decision status: **UNDECIDED_BY_MARK**.

## Tier P2 — compact, high-quality exact-overlap clusters

### Tiruvannamalai / Arunachala
Ramana Maharshi supplies a dense four-site lifetime cluster and Yogananda adds a confirmed Sri Ramanasramam visit. This remains `LOCKED_BY_MARK` A-anchor. The task therefore records the overlap but does not re-rank or reconsider Mark’s choice.

Travel-relevance signal: **HIGH exact-site quality, lower person count**.
Decision status: **UNDECIDED_BY_MARK**.

Decision status: existing Kumaon/Mark decisions unchanged.

### Serampore
Sri Yukteswar + Yogananda converge strongly on the guru/ashram lineage context. Anandamayi Ma also has a separate later station encounter with Yogananda in Serampore, but that station must not be merged with the ashram.

Travel-relevance signal: **MEDIUM-HIGH** pending fuller Yogananda reconciliation.

It is not yet a multi-person city overlap in the conservative table, but it remains travel-relevant because single-person importance can exceed overlap count. It is retained here specifically to prevent an overlap-only heatmap from erasing high-value unique anchors.

### Kukuchina / Dunagiri

Lahiri Mahasaya shares the initiation-event lineage context, but the exact cave evidence remains governed by Core-Kriya reconciliation and Babaji’s special epistemic rule.

## Guardrail for later heatmap/route builders

A later travel heatmap should combine at least three separate dimensions: (1) overlap density, (2) single-person mission-critical anchors, and (3) coverage confidence. Otherwise cities with many provisional links could incorrectly outrank a uniquely important Mark-locked site.
