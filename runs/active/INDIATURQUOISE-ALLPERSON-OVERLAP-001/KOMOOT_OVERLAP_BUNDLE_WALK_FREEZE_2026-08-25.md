### Person + nature / scenic overlap

The walk turns an already-required Ramana/Yogananda exact-overlap site into a scenic hill traverse with views over Tiruvannamalai, a wooded/stone cave path, and two additional already-relevant Ramana sites. The nature/scenic component comes from the trail itself; it is **not** used to manufacture a new person claim.

### Normal route / drive comparison

- direct Sri Ramanasramam ↔ Arunachaleswarar Temple road transfer: approximately **2–3 km by road**;
- one current distance service estimates about **3.0 km / 4 min driving**; treat the 4-minute number as an approximate routing-service estimate, not a guaranteed travel time, especially during festival/full-moon congestion;
- bundle walk: **2.41 km trail, 2 h 05 recorded elapsed**.

So this is **not a time-saving walk**. Relative to a normal short auto-rickshaw transfer, expect roughly **+1 h 45 to +2 h** once cave stops are allowed for. Its value is content consolidation, not speed.

### Bundelwinst

**ZEER HOOG.** One point-to-point movement can replace a plain taxi hop while preserving all of these as separate stops:
- one exact Ramana + Yogananda shared anchor;
- Skandasramam;
- Virupaksha Cave;
- arrival in the Big Temple zone;
- scenic hill/view content.

No separate taxi out-and-back is needed between the cave approach and the temple side if the point-to-point traverse is open that day.

### Winter fit — Dec 2026 / Jan 2027

**GOOD, CONDITIONAL ON DRY/OPEN CONDITIONS.** Cooler winter hours are preferable to midday heat. However, recent landslide/rain closures on Arunachala mean access must be checked locally on the day after heavy rain. Stone/boulder sections can become less forgiving when wet.

### Best time of day

**Morning optimum: about 08:15–11:00.**

Why:
- Sri Ramanasramam visitor guidance gives public visiting hours for Skandasramam/Virupaksha Cave of **08:15–16:30**;
- the route is explicitly not recommended in non-daylight hours;
- early start reduces heat exposure.

Do not start so early that you simply arrive before cave access opens.

### Solo safety

**DAYLIGHT / SIGNED-PATH SOLO: acceptable with normal hill caution; OFF-ROUTE SOLO: no.**

Operational rules:
- use the signed path from the Sri Ramanasramam back gate;
- at the early fork, follow the sign toward Skandasramam and do not continue onto the heavily wooded wrong path described by the ashram;
- do not follow informal guides toward summit, “secret cave”, secluded or restricted forest variants;
- do not schedule the wooded traverse after dark;
- carry water and use footwear with grip unless intentionally walking barefoot for religious reasons and conditions are suitable.

### Wildlife / animal exposure

Recorded trail reports mention **monkeys and dogs**. Keep food concealed, do not feed or provoke animals, secure loose belongings, and give animals space. The wider hill is protected wildlife habitat; this is another reason not to leave the visitor corridor.

### Legal/access closure

**Critical distinction:**
- Sri Ramanasramam currently publishes visitor directions for the lower Skandasramam/Virupaksha corridor, and its May 2026 newsletter reports ongoing path maintenance due to rising visitor numbers;
- separately, the Forest Department reiterated in January 2026 that climbing/trekking Annamalai Hill in restricted forest terrain is prohibited;
- therefore this freeze permits only the **signed lower visitor corridor**, not the higher/full Komoot route or summit/off-trail extension.

Same-day closure check at Sri Ramanasramam back gate is mandatory, especially after heavy rainfall or temporary district/forest orders.

### Freeze verdict

`KEEP — BEST CURRENT TURQUOISE BUNDLE WALK.`

It is unusually strong because the walking act itself connects an exact two-person anchor with two additional existing Ramana locations and a scenic hill corridor. The benefit survives strict dedupe and does not depend on any new person claim.
