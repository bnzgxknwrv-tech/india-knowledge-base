## SOURCE_ACCESS_BLOCKERS

- *Miracle of Love* full searchable edition was not available from an official free full-text source in this run; official LSRF excerpts and an indexed scan/catalog were used selectively. Full page-by-page extraction therefore remains incomplete.
- *By His Grace* was available as web-indexed full text on a third-party document host and in official excerpt form, but the official publisher's full ebook is commercial/DRM. Exact printed-page reconciliation was not performed.
- *The Divine Reality* PDF was text-searchable and was the strongest broad corpus. The PDF screenshot endpoint failed with a cache-miss when explicitly tested; parsed PDF text remained accessible.
- Several private houses are identified in recollections only by devotee surname/role. Exact present-day parcel/building identity cannot safely be inferred.
- Historical district labels in sources are retained where necessary; Akbarpur was described as Agra district in source-era text, while modern administrative placement is Firozabad district.
