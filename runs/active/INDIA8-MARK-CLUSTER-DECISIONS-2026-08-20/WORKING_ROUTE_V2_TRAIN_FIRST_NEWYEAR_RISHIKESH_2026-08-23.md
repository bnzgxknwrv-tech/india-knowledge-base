Hard frame: arrival DEL 19 Dec 2026 10:15; departure DEL 21 Jan 2027 12:20; 33 India calendar nights, including intentional overnight-train nights.

## HARD TRANSPORT RULE
Long-distance preference: TRAIN first, FLIGHT second when rail costs too much usable time, PRIVATE CAR third/for mountains and last-mile, BUS excluded.
Compare door-to-door time and usable daylight, not vehicle time alone.

## KEY V2 GAINS
1. 19 Dec Delhi -> Kathgodam = overnight train instead of Delhi hotel + next-day long road transfer.
2. 4 Jan Mathura -> Prayagraj = overnight train instead of losing a waking day.
3. 5 Jan Prayagraj -> Varanasi = evening fast train; no Prayagraj hotel night needed.
4. Result: Varanasi gains one full night/day while the trip still fits exactly 33 India nights.
5. 31 Dec is deliberately Rishikesh / Parmarth Niketan New Year, not Haridwar, Agra or transit.
