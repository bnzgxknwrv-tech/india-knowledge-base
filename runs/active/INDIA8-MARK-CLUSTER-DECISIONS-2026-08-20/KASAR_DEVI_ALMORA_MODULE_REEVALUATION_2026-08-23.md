A. KEEP FULL ALMORA/KASAR MODULE (~3 nights)
- preserves Turiya heritage stay + Kasar/Crank's Ridge + Bodh Ashram + Almora sites + Jageshwar day.
- strongest for slow seeker/counterculture/Vivekananda atmosphere.

C. DROP DEDICATED ALMORA/KASAR SLEEP MODULE
