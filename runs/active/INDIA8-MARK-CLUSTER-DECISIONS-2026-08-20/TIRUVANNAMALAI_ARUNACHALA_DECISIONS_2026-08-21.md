## Final-trip presentation requirement
For every A-day / A-site in Tiruvannamalai-Arunachala, the final travel report must provide the relevant historical/person context BEFORE the visit, including the story and exact relevance of each site. Mark explicitly wants to be able to read the relevant passage/story that morning or before entering the site so he does not discover afterward that an important event happened there.

Specific mandatory example: Paramahansa Yogananda visited Ramana Maharshi at Sri Ramanasramam. The final Sri Ramanasramam visit card must state this clearly, identify the relevant historical setting/room if reliably known, summarize what happened, and point to the relevant AOAY/person-source context. Do not bury this in micro-notes.

Micro-sites inside Sri Ramanasramam (Old Hall, Ramana Samadhi, Mother's Shrine, Nirvana Room, etc.) remain under the single parent A and must be surfaced as essential on-site details in the final travel guide, not as separate ABC decisions.

Giripradakshina/Girivalam is not just transit between sites; it is an A experience in its own right and must be scheduled with realistic walking time and interaction with the other Arunachala A-sites.
