- The large 'Nainital High' group photograph was taken at/around the hotel's front patio/porch area.
- Other devotee testimony confirms rooms and terraces were actively used by the group.

- no reliable evidence found that the current top-floor room visible in modern reviews is proven to be Ram Dass's room;
- therefore final guide must NOT invent a room number or claim exact room continuity.

### Current overnight practicality
- Hotel is still traceable as an operating accommodation.
- Recent 2024–2025 reviews are highly mixed and include serious complaints about maintenance, cleanliness, hot water and amenities; heater may be charged separately.
- Other recent guests still value the people, food, location and emotional/historic character.
- Modern top-floor/terrace/balcony rooms exist, but historic identity is not proven.

### Operational disposition
Hotel Evelyn remains A for historical meaning and remains the current Nainital anchor in canon, but the final booking should be treated as **heritage-first / comfort-risk**. Before money is paid, direct contact should ask specifically about:
1. working hot water/geyser;
2. room heater and cost;
3. clean/dry room condition in winter;
4. which balcony/front-patio areas are accessible;
5. whether staff/family can identify the 1970–71 devotee rooms or photo porch from family knowledge.

This direct-property question is the only route to improve exact room identity now; public-web research has reached an honest evidence limit.
