# Haridwar / Kankhal / Rishikesh corridor decision

Date: 2026-08-22

## Cluster status
B — attractive but currently not strong enough to earn a dedicated route branch in a ~32-day trip.

## Why
- Mark is personally attracted to Rishikesh, but there are too many higher-priority clusters competing for time.
- Kankhal contains a genuine A-level Anandamayi Ma magnet (her Mahasamadhi / active veneration), but that alone is not enough to force a dedicated regional detour.
- Haridwar station is C / historical transit context, not a destination driver.
- Kankhal/Daksheswar details remain available as nearby/combined additions if this corridor returns.

## Reopen rule
Only promote this corridor back toward A if a future sweep or consolidation reveals one or more exceptionally important additional sites in the same geographic corner, strong enough to materially change route value.

## Important distinction
This is a CLUSTER-level B. Individual sites such as Anandamayi Ma Kankhal may remain A in content/value; they are not deleted or downgraded. The cluster simply does not currently justify its own travel branch.
