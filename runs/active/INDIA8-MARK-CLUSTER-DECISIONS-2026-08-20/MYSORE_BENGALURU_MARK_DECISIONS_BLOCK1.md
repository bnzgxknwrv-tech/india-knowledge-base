## Functional building rule confirmed by Mark
Schools, hospitals, town halls, offices and ordinary private homes that remain in normal use are generally B or C rather than standalone A, unless they contain an active shrine/pilgrimage function or become effectively free to include because they sit directly beside/on the route to an A-site. For final routing, show proximity to A-sites and promote as an opportunistic stop when practical.
