## CONFLICTS
1. Exact date/year details for some early Kamarpukur sadhana episodes and the marriage are handled conservatively because early biographies themselves note chronology uncertainties.
2. Varanasi residence: sources agree Mathur rented two side-by-side houses near Kedar Ghat; later institutional tradition identifies Kalinath Bapuli’s house/Paduka Mandir as one relevant house, but the second remains unresolved and relic claims are not treated as historical proof.
3. Navadvip journey: the broader pilgrimage is documented, but the accessible indexed corpus did not expose enough occurrence-level text to assign specific Navadvip temples/houses without risk of importing a later pilgrimage itinerary. No speculative Navadvip site is therefore frozen.
4. Several Kolkata memorial/property identifications have shifted street names or structures; historical presence is retained while modern “same building” status is marked DEELS/ALLEEN_PLAATS where not independently audited.
