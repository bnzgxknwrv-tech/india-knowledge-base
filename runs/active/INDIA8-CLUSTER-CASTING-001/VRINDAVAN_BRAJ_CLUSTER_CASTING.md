## HUMAN LEGEND
Vrindavan/Braj = Krishna pilgrimage region south of Delhi, including Vrindavan, Mathura and Govardhan-area sites. This is not merely a general Hindu stop: existing person-first sweeps show concrete Yogananda, Neem Karoli Baba, Ram Dass, Ramakrishna and Anandamayi Ma links.

## WHY THIS CLUSTER MAY MATTER TO MARK
- AOAY chapter 11 is explicitly set around Yogananda's 'penniless test' in Brindaban/Vrindavan; Yogananda is personally there.
- AOAY chapter 42 brings Yogananda back to Swami Keshabananda's Brindaban ashram.
- Neem Karoli Baba repeatedly resided in the Vrindavan Ashram; his Mahasamadhi Mandir/cremation spot is inside the current ashram.
- Ram Dass repeatedly stayed at the NKB Vrindavan Ashram and at Jaipuria Bhawan; he visited Banke Bihari and Anandamayi Ma's Vrindavan Ashram.
- Sri Ramakrishna's 1868 pilgrimage is unusually localisable: Fouzdar Kunj upper room, Banke Bihari, Kaliyadah Ghat, Nidhivan, Govardhan, Radha Kund, Shyam Kund, Kusum Sarovar, Dhruva Ghat.
