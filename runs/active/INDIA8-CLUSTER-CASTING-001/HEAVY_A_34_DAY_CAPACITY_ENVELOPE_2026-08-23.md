## TRANSFER / ARRIVAL / RETURN CAPACITY
A 34-day trip cannot treat all transfers as free.
At minimum reserve capacity for:
- international arrival / Delhi exit;
- north-mountain entry/exit;
- Agra↔Vrindavan movement;
- north/central transfer toward Varanasi;
- Varanasi↔Bodh Gaya;
- north/central ↔ south India flight/transfer;
- Tiruvannamalai ↔ airport / final Delhi return connection.

Realistic transfer consumption across the whole route: roughly 4–6 day-equivalents, even when some transfers happen on partial visit days.

## FIRST CAPACITY CONCLUSION
- Compressed heavy-A local footprint ~24 + ~4–6 transfer day-equivalents = ~28–30 days.
- Preferred heavy-A footprint ~29–30 + ~4–6 transfer equivalents = ~33–36 days.

Therefore the 34-day window is already close to full before adding Haridwar/Kankhal/Rishikesh.

Operational implication:
1. Haridwar/Kankhal/Rishikesh should remain B/reserve until route geometry is built.
2. Its light-A sites (Har Ki Pauri Aarti, Beatles Ashram; Kankhal Mahasamadhi strong A) do NOT justify stealing breathing room from Varanasi, Arunachala or the Babaji/Kumaon core.
3. If the reserve cluster fits geographically with little net route cost, test a short insertion; otherwise omit without downgrading its site-level A's.
4. Mysore/Bengaluru dedicated module has no capacity case at present.
5. Prayagraj/Allahabad must be reconciled before final dates because any retained stay there tightens capacity further.
