## HUMAN LEGEND
Prayagraj/Allahabad = the sacred confluence city where Ganga and Yamuna meet at the Triveni Sangam, and one of the Kumbh Mela cities. For this project it is unusually dense in cross-person history despite having fewer room-level sites than Vrindavan.

## WHY THIS CLUSTER MAY MATTER TO MARK
- AOAY places Yogananda personally at the 1936 Allahabad Kumbh Mela in chapter 42.
- AOAY chapters 34/36 preserve the 1894 Kumbh encounter involving Sri Yukteswar and Babaji; this makes Prayagraj a core Kriya-lineage scene, not just general pilgrimage.
- Sri Ramakrishna personally stayed three nights in Prayag in 1868 and bathed at the Triveni Sangam.
- Neem Karoli Baba repeatedly used 4 Church Lane as a major winter base; his small room there is a concrete room-level site.
- Ram Dass stayed about five days at the same 4 Church Lane with a 24-person group in 1971.
- Ram Dass/NKB also have a strong lead at the reclining Hanuman temple by the Sangam.
- Anandamayi Ma repeatedly attended Allahabad/Kumbh; her official chronology contains direct Sangam/Kumbh occurrences.
