### Mark's new question, answered independently of spirituality
**Is Rishikesh a genuine scenic/nature/adventure magnet for non-spiritual people?** Yes, demonstrably — fresh research this session confirms Rishikesh has transformed over recent decades from a purely pilgrimage town into "one of India's most visited travel hubs," explicitly described as attracting "backpackers, spiritual seekers, adventure lovers, and weekend tourists alike," now positioned as "a dynamic center for experiential travel" rather than only ascetic retreat. The concrete non-spiritual draws: the Himalayan-foothill/Ganges-valley scenery itself (forests, mountains, the river), the iconic suspension bridges (Lakshman Jhula, Ram Jhula) with river views, and it functions as a gateway base for trekking/mountaineering further into the Himalayas.

**Upstream/foothill scenery vs. central-town character — genuinely different, correctly separable per the task's instruction:** the central Rishikesh ghat/market area has become notably commercialized and crowded in recent years (one independent traveler account found in this session's research is titled bluntly "Rishikesh – Not Worth Visiting Anymore," reflecting a real overtourism/commercialization complaint, not a fringe opinion) — while the upstream stretch (Shivpuri, toward Kaudiyala) and the outward highland magnets already catalogued in the prior audit (Devprayag Sangam, Kunjapuri sunrise viewpoint, Neelkanth Mahadev forest pilgrimage trek, Tehri Dam/Lake) remain genuinely scenic and much less commercially saturated. **This is the real distinction Mark's question is probing: the town itself is a mixed bag, but the surrounding river/foothill geography is the honestly beautiful part.**

**Season fit for late December/New Year, verified fresh this session:**
- Rishikesh: daytime **19–22°C**, nights **3–7°C** (occasionally colder), around **82% clear/sunny sky** in December — genuinely pleasant walking weather, cold but not harsh nights.
- Haridwar: roughly **7.5–21°C** across the day in December.
- **Rafting:** December is within the operating season and late December (16–31) specifically is described as bringing "comfortable conditions" with minimal weather disruption for the activity itself — but **water temperature is 8–12°C, genuinely cold**, requiring proper wetsuit/gear, not a casual splash. This matches the existing repo's own prior caution ("winter cold-water operations to verify").
- Daylight is short (winter solstice window) — relevant for planning outdoor activity timing, not independently researched to the minute this session.

**Conclusion on the non-spiritual question:** the scenic/adventure appeal is real and well-documented, not manufactured — but it competes with genuine commercialization complaints about the town center specifically, and cold-water rafting is a real (not fictional) caveat for a December visit, not a disqualifier.
