Date: 2026-08-23
Owner: Mark
Status: HARD TRAVEL PREFERENCE / ROUTE GOVERNANCE

## MARK PREFERENCE ORDER FOR LONG DISTANCES
1. **TRAIN FIRST** — preferred when practical, especially when an overnight sleeper converts transfer time into sleep or when train does not materially destroy useful daytime.
2. **FLIGHT SECOND** — use when train is so long that flying saves substantial usable trip time after accounting for airport transfer, early arrival/check-in/security, baggage, waiting and destination transfer.
3. **PRIVATE CAR THIRD** — use when rail/air geometry is poor, for mountain transfers, short/medium intercity hops, airport/rail last-mile, or when private car is clearly more efficient door-to-door than a forced rail detour.
4. **BUS: EXCLUDED / AVOID.** Do not route Mark by intercity or long-distance bus. Bus is not a fallback in normal planning.

## OPTIMIZATION RULE
Never compare only scheduled vehicle duration. Compare **door-to-door trip cost**:
- hotel/ashram -> station/airport;
- required reporting/check-in/security time;
- waiting/connection risk;
- train/flight/car duration;
- station/airport -> next sleep base;
- useful daylight/visit hours lost;
- whether an overnight train replaces a hotel night and preserves a full sightseeing day.

A slower overnight train can beat a faster flight if it preserves a whole daytime block. A direct private car can beat a train in the mountains if the rail station detour and early departure destroy more time than they save.

## CLASS PREFERENCE
For overnight rail, target **1A / First AC** where available; 2A is fallback only if Mark later accepts it. Do not design sleeper-train logic around non-AC sleeper class.

## TIMETABLE CAUTION
Indian railway/airline schedules found in Aug 2026 are planning evidence, not booking locks for Dec 2026/Jan 2027. Recheck exact train number, running day, departure/arrival, class availability and flight schedule when the reservation window is open. Route architecture may rely on the service pattern, but final booking cards must revalidate it.

- Delhi -> Kumaon railhead: overnight train is strongly preferred if 1A is available because it converts the first northbound transfer into sleep.
- Kumaon mountain legs: private car, not bus and not forced rail detours.
- Nainital -> Rishikesh: private car currently wins door-to-door despite train-first preference because the useful train requires a very early transfer to Kathgodam and adds complexity/time.
- Rishikesh -> Agra: train is attractive because the route day is a Taj-closed Friday positioning day.

- Bodh Gaya -> Tiruvannamalai: flight + private-car last mile; train is too long.
- Tiruvannamalai -> Delhi: private car to Chennai airport + nonstop flight; train is too long.
