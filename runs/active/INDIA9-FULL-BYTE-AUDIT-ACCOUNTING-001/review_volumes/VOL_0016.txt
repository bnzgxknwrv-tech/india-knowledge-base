Mark explicitly stated that knowing the exact physical place of Yogananda's meeting with Ramana Maharshi is highly important for him and materially increases his desire to visit Arunachala/Tiruvannamalai.

## Strongly verified facts
1. Paramahansa Yogananda visited Sri Ramana Maharshi at Sri Ramanasramam, Tiruvannamalai, on 29 November 1935.
2. `Talks with Sri Ramana Maharshi`, Talk 106, records Yogananda arriving with four others at 8:45 a.m. and that the group had lunch in the Ashram.
3. Sri Ramanasramam's archival-film research booklet identifies surviving footage from this visit.
4. That booklet physically locates the filmed scene: Maharshi is seated on a bench placed immediately north of the Old Hall, facing south; Yogananda and Paul Brunton are present nearby.

## Critical precision
The north-of-Old-Hall bench is an exact physical location for the SURVIVING FILMED SCENE. Current evidence does not yet prove that the entire verbal meeting/conversation took place only at that bench. Keep these as two different claims:
- `FILMED_SCENE_EXACT_SITE = bench north of Old Hall` — strong.
- `FULL_CONVERSATION_EXACT_SITE = unresolved within Sri Ramanasramam` — do not overstate without an additional direct source.
