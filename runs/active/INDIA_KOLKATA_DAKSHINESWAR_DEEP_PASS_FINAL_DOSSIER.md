# 1. Main conclusion: is Dakshineswar really a concentrated Ramakrishna world?

**Yes. Strongly.** The deep pass supports Kolkata/Dakshineswar as a genuine concentrated eastern world rather than a thin urban fallback.

The strongest Ramakrishna/Vivekananda/Sarada geography is:

1. **Dakshineswar Kali Temple** — Ramakrishna's central mature-life spiritual anchor, with his preserved room and Panchavati; Sarada Devi/Dakshineswar context; also a direct Yogananda overlap because official YSS material records his repeated visits and meditation here.
2. **Belur Math** — permanent Ramakrishna Math/Mission headquarters created under Vivekananda, with Ramakrishna, Sarada Devi and Vivekananda shrines, museum, monastery and major architecture on the Hooghly.
3. **Cossipore Udyanbati** — Ramakrishna's final-life/Mahasamadhi place.
4. **Baranagar Math** — exact first monastery site of Ramakrishna's monastic disciples after his death.
5. **Alambazar Math** — second monastery, now materially more worthwhile because its restored heritage building/museum reopened in January 2026.
6. **Balaram Mandir** — repeated Ramakrishna gathering place and the house where Vivekananda formally founded the Ramakrishna Mission in 1897.
7. **Mayer Bari / Udbodhan** — principal concentrated Sarada Devi home-shrine in Kolkata.
8. **Vivekananda Ancestral House** — large restored birthplace/home cultural centre, not a plaque-only biography pin.

RKM's own modern activity confirms that this is a coherent circuit: its programmes/processions have linked several of Cossipore, Baranagar, Alambazar, Balaram Mandir, Mayer Bari and Vivekananda's ancestral house in one historical-spiritual route. This is exactly the “many important events concentrated in a few places” pattern requested by Mark.
