## Classification notes

- **R1** is used where the historical event/residence is tied to a concrete current site/building/room or unambiguous grounds, even when visitor policy or the precise historic sleeping room remains unknown.
- **R2** is used where current successor/memorial/heritage grounds reliably preserve the historical spot but original fabric has changed or room-level continuity is incomplete.
- **R3** is limited to tightly bounded complexes/local zones where finer historical precision is unavailable.
- **R4** preserves broad-place-only claims where the city/locality is known but the actual property is not discriminated.
- **R5** preserves unresolved historical identity or modern continuity; none were deleted.
