## 1. KUMAON / NAINITAL — Naini Lake-rondwandeling

**Existing status/anchor:** `Naini Lake — A+` (existing Mark selection; unchanged).

**Best-searchable Komoot object:** `Nainital` (Komoot Highlight) with nearby route `Nainital loop from Nainital` (Komoot: 6.10 km / 1:50 / +200 m). This Komoot loop is broader than the selected pure lake circuit and must NOT be treated as an exact geometric match. For the actual selected walk, search `Nainital` / `Naini Lake` in Komoot and follow the lakefront roads/promena­de rather than importing the 6.10 km loop blindly.

**Physical walking start:** Mallital lakefront / Naina Devi Temple end of Naini Lake. Tallital bus-stand end is equally usable.

**Operational selected walk:** ca. **3.5 km / 55–75 min walking FROM Mallital/Tallital lakefront**, full lake circuit; essentially flat to gently undulating. Independent 2026 mapping signal gives ~2.2 mi / 3.5 km with ~0 ft recorded gain; local tourism descriptions commonly put the walk at about one hour. Treat elevation as **<50 m practical gain** rather than the +200 m of Komoot's broader loop.

**Route form:** loop.

**Terrain / difficulty:** paved/urban lakeside roads and promenades; easy walking; main operational hazard is traffic/road edge on narrower sections, not mountain terrain.

**Anchor → start:** Naini Lake A+ is the attraction itself; **0 km / 0 min drive** if starting from the lakefront. If dropped at Mallital or Tallital, start immediately.

**Parking / drop-off:** numerous taxi/drop-off opportunities at Mallital/Tallital; parking is constrained in central Nainital and should not be assumed immediately beside the preferred start.

**Driver:** `DRIVER_NOT_REQUIRED_TO_WAIT` — urban transport/taxi recovery is straightforward; driver may reposition.

**Mobile/remoteness:** urban Nainital; normal town-level connectivity expected, but operator-specific signal not officially verified in this pass. Not remote.

**Best window:** **morning** preferred for quieter roads, clearer lake surface and less traffic; **late afternoon** acceptable while fully light. Dark walking adds avoidable traffic/dog/visibility risk.

**Winter fit (Dec 2026–Jan 2027):** **GOOD**, subject to cold, morning frost, occasional fog and wet/slippery pavement. No high-altitude snow dependency. Use footwear with grip if frost/rain has occurred.

**Legal access / gate times:** public urban lakefront; no forest gate applies. Naini Lake is an official tourism attraction. Temple/property opening hours are separate from the public lake circuit.

**Wildlife / safety check:** urban lakefront is not a forest trail. Monkeys/langurs and free-ranging dogs can occur in Himalayan towns; do not carry exposed food near monkeys. District-level leopard conflict is material for surrounding forested hills, not a reason to classify the central lake promenade as a leopard trail.

**Terrain risks:** traffic edge, wet paving, winter frost, fog; no river crossing, exposed cliff, landslide-dependent trail or navigation problem on the standard lake circuit.

**solo_safety:** `SAFE_SOLO` in daylight / normal busy hours. Avoid purposeless dark laps on quiet sections.

**Mitigation:** walk in daylight, use quieter Thandi Road-side sections where appropriate, stay road-aware, no headphones in traffic, do not feed monkeys/dogs.

**Sources checked 2026-08-25:** Komoot Highlight `Nainital` / nearby `Nainital loop from Nainital`; Uttarakhand Tourism Nainital itinerary; official Uttarakhand tourism material listing Naini Lake; current mapping/traveler distance cross-check.

## 2. KUMAON / DHOKANEY–SUYALBARI — Dhokaney Waterfall short walk

**Existing status/anchor:** `Dhokaney Waterfall — A`; operational anchor required by central layer: **Kainchi Dham — A+**. Grades unchanged.

**Best-searchable Komoot route/highlight:** **NO RELIABLE EXACT KOMOOT MATCH INDEXED as of 2026-08-25.** Search terms to use in Komoot: `Dhokaney Waterfall`, `Dhokaney`, `Dokaney`, `Suyalbari`. Do not substitute a similarly named waterfall elsewhere. Official Uttarakhand tourism training material independently lists `Dhokanay` among Nainital-region waterfalls.

**Physical walking start:** signed/unpaved access descending from the Dhokaney village-side road toward the waterfall, before/near Dhokaney village on the Suyalbari–Mukteshwar side road.

**Operational walk:** local route descriptions indicate only about **5–10 min each way** from the upper road if the vehicle stops before the rough descent. Allow **0.8–1.2 km total / 30–45 min** including cautious descent, waterfall viewing and return; this retains the central layer's selected operational envelope. Exact Komoot-derived length/elevation is unavailable.

**Elevation:** **not Komoot-verified**; short but clearly downhill-to-waterfall / uphill return. Treat as roughly **50–100 m practical vertical** until GPS-closed locally.

**Route form:** out-and-back.

**Terrain / difficulty:** unpaved downhill access, potentially loose/wet rock and muddy forest/village track; easy in dry conditions but slippery near water.

**A+ anchor fully written:** **Kainchi Dham / Neem Karoli Baba Ashram — A+**.

**Anchor → trailhead drive:** approximately **25–30 km / 45–60 min** from Kainchi Dham to the Dhokaney access, depending on mountain-road traffic and exact drop-off. One current accommodation mapping source gives 28.3 km drive to Kainchi Dham from a Dhokaney property; use this as road-distance order of magnitude, not a precise trailhead fix.

**Parking / drop-off:** roadside/village drop-off is more dependable than taking a normal car down the rough spur. Do not assume formal paved parking at the waterfall.

**Driver:** `DRIVER_WAIT` recommended. The trailhead is rural, the walk is short, and relying on on-demand return transport is unnecessary risk/friction.

**Mobile/remoteness:** **not reliably verified**. Rural hill-road setting; do not rely on signal at the waterfall. Easy retreat to the road if using the short standard access.

**Best window:** **late morning to early afternoon**. Avoid dawn/dusk because of wildlife visibility and cold/slippery conditions; avoid dark.

**Winter fit:** **CONDITIONAL-GOOD**. December/January usually removes monsoon-flow and landslide intensity, but shaded wet rock may be slick and cold; water volume may be lower. Do not descend after rain, freeze or visible ice.

**Legal access / gates:** Dhokanay/Dhokaney is listed as a tourism waterfall by Uttarakhand tourism material; no official forest-gate or permit rule for this short public access was found. That absence is not a blanket right to enter private/closed land: obey local signs/caretaker instructions the same day.

**Wildlife evidence / relevance:** the Dhokaney/Suyalbari-Ramgarh belt is forested Kumaon, and Nainital district experienced a serious cluster of fatal leopard attacks in late Dec 2025/early Jan 2026 in Dhari/Okhalkanda/Ramgarh-area hill villages. This does not establish a leopard on the waterfall path, but it is sufficient to reject an automatic solo-safe assumption for a quiet forested spur. Wild boar, snakes and monkeys/langurs are plausible local hazards but were not independently incident-verified for this exact waterfall in this pass. No tiger/elephant-specific evidence for the exact trail was found.

**Terrain risks:** slippery rock, steep short descent, wet edge, possible loose material; no planned river ford on the normal visitor spur. Monsoon-type landslide risk is lower in winter but mountain-road rockfall/landslide checks remain prudent after bad weather.

**solo_safety:** `PREFER_COMPANY`.

**Mitigation:** daylight only; ask locals/caretaker same morning about animal sightings and track condition; driver waits; do not wander off the signed spur; no headphones; turn around if path is icy/slippery or local people advise against walking.

**Sources checked 2026-08-25:** central Komoot layer; Uttarakhand Tourism training material listing Dhokanay; local Dhokaney access description; recent Nainital-district leopard reporting.
