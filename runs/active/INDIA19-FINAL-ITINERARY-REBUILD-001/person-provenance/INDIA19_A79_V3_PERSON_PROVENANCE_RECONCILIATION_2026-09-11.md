### A004 / A005 — Kainchi versus Bhumiadhar first meeting

CCI and WORK exposed a real conflict over the first Ram Dass–Neem Karoli Baba meeting.

Reconciliation after additional independent source check:
- Ram Dass’s own first-contact account describes the small temple and mother/spleen episode but does not name the location in the surfaced primary telling.
- A University of Kent study of Neem Karoli Baba explicitly identifies Bhumiadhar as the setting of the initial Ram Dass–Maharajji meeting in 1967.

Therefore v3 uses:
- **A005 Bhumiadhar** = best-supported current location of the first meeting / mother-spleen turning point, with exact microspot not claimed;
- **A004 Kainchi Dham** = central subsequent Maharajji/Ram Dass guru-home, sadhana and satsang world, NOT labelled as the first-meeting site.

This corrects the earlier chat statement that the first meeting was at Kainchi.
