## Core correction

A012 `Historic Haidakhan cave / gufa` and Haidakhan Babaji's famous 1970 long meditation on the local Kailash summit are **not the same physical place**.

The Haidakhandi Samaj tradition describes two successive physical sites in the same Haidakhan sacred landscape:

1. **Historic cave / gufa at the foot of the local Kumaon Mount Kailash / Mount Adi Kailash** — the 1970 appearance/reappearance place associated with Haidakhan Babaji.
2. **Small old Shiva temple on the summit of the local Kailash mountain above Haidakhan** — shortly afterwards, in September 1970, Babaji climbed to this summit and remained there for **45 days** in yogic meditation. Sources also say this is where he began giving his first teachings.

Therefore future Mark-facing text must never imply that A012's cave itself is the 45-day summit meditation place.

## Naming warning

The local Haidakhan mountain is called variously in Haidakhandi sources:
- `Kumaon Mount Kailash`
- `Mount Kailash`
- `Mount Adi Kailash`

This must **not** be confused with:
- the famous Mount Kailash in Tibet;
- `Om Parvat` near the India-Nepal-Tibet frontier;
- the much higher and geographically separate `Adi Kailash / Chhota Kailash / Jonglingkong` in Pithoragarh district.

There is also a **local** mapped `Chota Kailash Shiv Temple` in the Haidakhan/Haldwani-Bhimtal area. A recorded route from Haidakhan Ashram to this summit temple is approximately **4.37 km one way**, with about **1,072 m of ascent**, max elevation around **1,724 m**, and is rated difficult. This is strong practical evidence that the summit remains a concrete pilgrimage/hiking destination accessible from the Haidakhan base, but exact historical-seat identification should be confirmed locally with Haidakhandi Samaj before a final itinerary label is frozen.

## Mark-facing meaning

For Mark, the spiritually relevant sequence is:

**CAVE → SUMMIT KAILASH TEMPLE**

- Cave: appearance/reappearance/origin point in the 1970 Haidakhan Babaji narrative.
- Summit temple: the 45-day meditation/tapasya place and early-teaching point.

The summit is therefore potentially at least as personally important as the cave and should not be hidden inside A012's generic description.
