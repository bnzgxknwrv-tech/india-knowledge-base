## Skeptical challenge by class

### Exact-life sites

`EXACT_LIFE_SITE` means the physical-person link survives, not that every current wall is original or every devotional detail is historically demonstrable. A009, A020/A021, A024, A031, A041, A054/A056 and A059 need tradition-level caveats or narrower subplace wording. Exact-site claims should always identify the encounterable micro-target.

### Direct institutions

A010, A019, A038, A062 and A078 are living institutions. Their relevance can be substantial without importing a founder's physical presence into current buildings. This is particularly important for YSS Dwarahat, Bhadury Sadan and YSS Dakshineswar.

### Memorials and successors

A008, A018, A040, A047, A055, A058 and A065 should be described as later containers of memory, relic, art or practice. “Later” does not mean “weak”: A040 and A055 remain strong because the relic/object content is physically specific.

### No-person-link sites

The 29 `NAME_ONLY_OR_NO_PERSON_LINK` records are not automatically dispensable. Kashi Vishwanath, the Ganges dawn boat, old-city lanes and experiential parent walks can remain powerful independently. Conversely, restaurants, snacks, waterfall and cinema should not acquire spiritual relevance merely because they sit inside an A-ledger.

## Final independent challenge

The key risk is not that the A79 includes “too many” places; this task cannot and does not decide that. The risk is semantic flattening. A Mark-facing clock plan becomes misleading when a private historic room, a modern successor center, a traditional event marker, a vast sacred landscape, a food stop and a restaurant are all described with the same location-level confidence. The matrix repairs that by making the physical class, event, subplace, uncertainty and dwell logic explicit for every A-number while preserving all 79 unchanged.
