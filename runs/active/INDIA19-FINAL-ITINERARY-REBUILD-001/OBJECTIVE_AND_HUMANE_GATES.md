## 1. OBJECTIVE HIERARCHY (LEXICOGRAPHIC — HIGHER TIERS DOMINATE LOWER ONES)

This is the objective hierarchy already agreed in the three-party consensus, consolidated from `decisions/FULL_A_COVERAGE_AND_GLOBAL_ROUTE_TOPOLOGY_REOPTIMIZATION_2026-09-09.md` §6 and `runs/active/INDIA18_FINAL_EXTRACTION_2026-09-09.md` §3:

1. **Hard scope / canon / date / 33-night feasibility.** Exactly 33 India nights (19 Dec-20 Jan); AI156/AI155 fixed; every locked duration honored (§`INPUT_CONTRACT.md` §3); FINAL OUT worlds never reopened; every retained physical A+/A/A* individually accounted for (not merely a cluster total).
2. **International-flight protection / connection-failure tolerance.** AI155 safety on 20-21 Jan outranks any content. Any self-connect or tight domestic transfer must be scored for what happens if the preceding leg is delayed, not only for its nominal timetable margin.
3. **Every retained physical A+/A has a real, recognizable time-block home.** Not hidden inside a vague cluster label; a parent/child pair may legitimately share one block, but neither entity may disappear (see the coverage ledger's MISSING/CONDITIONAL/HIDDEN/SPECIAL rows — a solve is not complete while these remain unresolved).
4. **Door-to-door / opening / access feasibility.** Real access windows, weekly closures (e.g. Sarnath museum, Belur Math on 12 Jan), festival/crowd conflicts, and actual door-to-door transfer times — not timetable time alone.
5. **Humane sleep/recovery/protected quiet-time viability.** Haidakhan's 2 complete protected quiet days must remain real rest, not disguised transfer days; adequate sleep opportunity around each transfer.
6. **Waking transport burden / base changes.** Minimize whole-human waking hours lost to intercity movement and the number of hotel/base changes, not merely map distance or airborne time.
7. **Robustness to winter fog/delay/fallback.** Especially for Delhi-area, Kumaon mountain roads, and any tight self-connect; each fragile edge needs an identified fallback.
8. **Experience/date quality.** Date-specific opportunities (e.g. a genuine YSS Christmas-season meditation opportunity, if verified) and avoidance of festival/crowd penalties where they would degrade a visit.
9. **Cost/convenience** — only after all higher-order criteria pass.

A route that wins on a lower tier can never be preferred over one that is worse on a higher tier. Mark's mild north/Kumaon tie-break preference (`INDIA18_FINAL_EXTRACTION_2026-09-09.md` §3) applies **only** when a choice is genuinely tied after tiers 1-8; it may not override safety or content fit, and a Chennai option can beat it if Chennai independently produces materially stronger content or final-flight geometry (`decisions/CHENNAI_BUFFER_AND_EARLY_DELHI_FLIGHT_PREFERENCE_2026-09-07.md`).

## 2. HUMANE-FIT METRICS TO AUDIT LATER (CONCRETE, MEASURABLE)

A later solve must report each of these per candidate route, per `decisions/FULL_A_COVERAGE_AND_GLOBAL_ROUTE_TOPOLOGY_REOPTIMIZATION_2026-09-09.md` §7 output requirements:

- **Sleep opportunity**: nights in an actual bed vs. sleeper-train nights vs. genuinely poor-sleep nights (e.g. an arrival around 04:00 is a real cost, not a free overnight substitute — `INDIA18_FINAL_EXTRACTION_2026-09-09.md` §6.4).
- **Transfer buffers**: door-to-door time including station/airport access, security/immigration/baggage process, and egress — not timetable time alone.
- **Consecutive heavy days**: count of days with combined transfer + sightseeing burden exceeding a normal day, and whether any protected quiet day is contaminated by hidden logistics.
- **Protected quiet blocks preserved**: explicit confirmation that Haidakhan's 2 full quiet days remain untouched by transfer/checkout logistics.
- **Number of hotel/base changes** and **number of flights**.
- **Number of sub-05:30 wake-ups** and **number of arrivals after 22:00**.
- **Total intercity waking travel hours**, split into road / awake-rail / sleepable-rail / airport-process hours.
- **Fragile-edge count**: transfers with materially less than 2 hours of slack against a plausible single-point delay, with an identified fallback for each.
- **33/33 proof**: an explicit night-by-night accounting that sums to exactly 33 India nights with no duplicate or missing night.

## 3. MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED — GENUINELY SUBJECTIVE THRESHOLDS

CCI does not invent numeric thresholds that belong to Mark's own comfort/taste. The following are flagged `MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` rather than pre-decided by this packet or by any later solver acting alone:

1. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — Bodh Gaya 2 vs 3 nights.** This is an explicitly open sensitivity (`INPUT_CONTRACT.md` §4); a solver may present the whole-human trade-off (extra content/rest vs. calendar cost elsewhere) but the final choice, if genuinely marginal after the objective hierarchy, is Mark's.
2. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — Tiruvannamalai 4 vs 5 nights**, on the same basis, including whether the standalone full Sri Ramanasramam immersion day content is judged sufficient at 4 nights.
3. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — how much whole-human burden is "too much" on a single day.** Governance already gives concrete definitional anchors (a sub-05:30 wake-up, a post-22:00 arrival, a 04:00-class arrival being a real penalty — `INDIA18_FINAL_EXTRACTION_2026-09-09.md` §3/§6.4), but no numeric ceiling exists for "maximum acceptable heavy days in a row" or "minimum acceptable sleep-quality score" before a route is rejected outright. A solver must show its count; Mark or an explicit prior Mark rule must set the rejection line, not the solver's own taste.
4. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — whether the released Tiruvannamalai night (if 4n wins) goes to Chennai content/rest, extra Kumaon, or pure operational buffer**, if the objective hierarchy leaves this genuinely tied (`decisions/CHENNAI_BUFFER_AND_EARLY_DELHI_FLIGHT_PREFERENCE_2026-09-07.md` §1 already frames this as a true solver comparison, not a pre-decided allocation).
5. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — global macro world order** (incumbent Kumaon-first-then-south vs. the four-AI Varanasi-first challenger, or any synthesized alternative). This is the central open question of the whole reopened topology exercise and is explicitly not for CCI, WORK or INDIA19 to close unilaterally by taste once the evidence is presented — `decisions/FULL_A_COVERAGE_AND_GLOBAL_ROUTE_TOPOLOGY_REOPTIMIZATION_2026-09-09.md` closing line: "No final Mark lock. Return evidence and recommendation only."
6. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — accepting the unprotected self-connect risk** (e.g. a separate-ticket domestic flight after AI156, such as the DEL->VNS same-day candidate) versus a safer but less efficient alternative. This is a personal risk-tolerance threshold, not an objectively computable one.
7. **`MARK_DECISION_REQUIRED` — the five MISSING/two CONDITIONAL Varanasi/Sarnath items and the Bodh Gaya ridge item**: whether Mark wants each one placed in the final schedule at all now that they are visible (rather than silently absorbed by a cluster label), and if so, at what cost to other content on that day. These are content-inclusion decisions, not calendar-arithmetic ones.

No other subjective threshold has been invented in this packet. Where governance already supplies a concrete numeric rule (e.g. exact night counts, exact quiet-day counts), that rule is treated as CLOSED FACT (see `CLOSED_FACTS_OPEN_VARIABLES.md` Category 1), not restated here as a fresh choice.
