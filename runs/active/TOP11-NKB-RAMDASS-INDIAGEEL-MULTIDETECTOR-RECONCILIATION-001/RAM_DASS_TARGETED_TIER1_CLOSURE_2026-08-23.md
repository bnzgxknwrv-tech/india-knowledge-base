## 1. Dharamsala / McLeod Ganj — Swarg Ashram

### Historical claim
Ram Dass's published memoir *Being Ram Dass* places the 1967 overland journey at Dharamsala/McLeod Ganj after Amarnath and describes going to the gate of **Swarg Ashram**, then the Dalai Lama's headquarters, followed by an audience with the Dalai Lama. The searchable online text copy was used only to locate/check the memoir passage; it is not treated as an official publisher-controlled edition.

Independent identity control is unusually strong:
- the official Office of His Holiness the Dalai Lama chronology states that the Dalai Lama took up residence at **Swarg Ashram in Dharamsala on 30 April 1960** and moved to Thekchen Choeling in **1968**;
- Tibet Documentation identifies the former Swarg Ashram as the first Dharamsala residence and says the building currently houses the **Regional Mountaineering Centre, Tushita Road, McLeod Ganj**;
- *The Indian Express* independently documented the surviving structure in July 2025 and reported that the historical rooms remain substantially preserved, while normal interior access is limited.

## 2. Ganeshpuri — Muktananda / Gurudev Siddha Peeth

### Historical claim
This gap is now supported from two Ram Dass-side routes:
- the official Sounds True *Being Ram Dass: A Celebration* site carries a photograph captioned **"With Papa Trivedi at Ganeshpuri, 1971"**;
- a Ram Dass teaching transcript recounts returning to Muktananda's temple in **Ganeshpuri** and meditating in an inner room of the meditation hall.

The present physical complex is independently identified by Siddha Yoga's own documentation: **Gurudev Siddha Peeth**, near Ganeshpuri, is the foundational Siddha Yoga ashram, initially constructed in 1956 for Swami Muktananda and established as a public trust in 1962.
