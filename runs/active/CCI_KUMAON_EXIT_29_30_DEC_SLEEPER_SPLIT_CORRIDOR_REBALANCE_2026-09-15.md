### 1. Baseline (incumbent): 12039 Kathgodam→New Delhi all the way + early 30 Dec Delhi→Agra

### 2. Genuine sleeper/night-train exit
An overnight working exists on this exact route pair in the reverse direction (15091 Old Delhi→Kathgodam, departs 00:25, arrives 08:27, ~8h) ([Goibibo](https://www.goibibo.com/trains/12040-detail/)), which strongly suggests a same-route overnight service running Kathgodam→Delhi also exists, but **I could not independently confirm its exact current number/timing** — this is a genuine `LIVE_RECHECK` gap, not a finding I'm willing to assert with a fabricated train number. **If confirmed**, this would let Mark sleep through the transfer instead of a seated ~5h35 daytime leg, arriving Delhi early morning 30 Dec — worth checking on IRCTC/current timetables before deciding, but not something I can respond in this pass without inventing a specific service.

### 3. Split corridor via Mathura (the real find)
- **29 Dec:** Haidakhan → mountain-road exit (~90 km, as already established) → Kathgodam → board the daytime Delhi train (~5h35) → arrive Delhi area evening → overnight in a Delhi-adjacent transit hotel. This is **not materially different from the baseline's own 29-Dec transfer** — the day was already going to end with a Delhi-area overnight either way. No new burden added here.
- **30 Dec:** Delhi → Mathura (~1h15–1h20 by frequent train, dozens of daily departures) → taxi to Vrindavan (~20–30 min) → Neem Karoli Baba Ashram/Samadhi Mandir [A] with real, unhurried dwell → back to Mathura Junction → Mathura → Agra (~35 min by train) → **arrive Agra in the afternoon** instead of before dawn.

**What this wins over the baseline:** the previously-wasted 30-Dec waiting day becomes a real pilgrimage stop at an existing Maharajji-thread site, and Agra arrival shifts from "very early, nothing to do" to "afternoon, settled in before the 31-Dec Taj morning" — solving Mark's stated 30-Dec complaint directly. **What it costs:** roughly 1h15-2h of extra transfer/taxi time on 30 Dec versus a direct Delhi→Agra run, easily absorbed by the day that used to be empty anyway.

**What it does NOT do:** it does not reduce or replace the Delhi-area overnight on 29 Dec (per the sleeper gap above, that would need a confirmed night-train, which I could not verify). It does not require re-opening Braj as a dedicated world — this is exactly the scoped, corridor-only, already-graded-site exception Mark authorized.
