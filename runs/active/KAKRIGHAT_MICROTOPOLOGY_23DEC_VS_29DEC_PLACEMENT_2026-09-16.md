## PART 1 — KAKRIGHAT MICROTOPOLOGY

### 1. Vivekananda / Jnana Vriksha / Karkateshwar Mahadev side
- Location: Kakrighat, on the Nainital–Almora road (NH109), by the Kosi river, ~20 km from Ramakrishna Kutir Almora and from Kainchi Dham. Source: almora.rkmm.org (Ramakrishna Mission's own Almora branch site).
- Physical type: a small roadside Shiva temple ("Karkateshwar Mahadev") beside the river, plus a sacred Peepal tree ("Jnana Vriksha") at the confluence of the Kosi and Suyal rivers.
- The original tree dried up by mid-2014; a cloned sapling from the same tree was replanted on the same spot on 15 July 2016 — so what is visitable today is the same spot, not the original 1890 tree itself. Source: almora.rkmm.org.
- Evidence for the Vivekananda link: primary institutional (Ramakrishna Mission's own Almora Kutir page) — in August 1890, Vivekananda and Swami Akhandananda meditated under this tree and Vivekananda had his microcosm/macrocosm realization here. This is the strongest class of source available (the Mission that maintains the site itself).

### 2. Neem Karoli Baba / Maharajji Kakrighat Dham (Hanuman temple / Sombari Baba) side
- Same village/compound area, same river frontage, same road (Almora-Nainital road, ~20 km from Kainchi Dham). Sources: nkbmeditation.org, maharajji.love, allseasonsz.com (independently converging on the same location description).
- Physical features reported: a Hanuman temple, a Shiva lingam shrine, a shrine "nestled in the roots/trunk of a massive tree," and Sombari Baba's samadhi/old hut with preserved "Raj Asan, Lingam, and sacred trees."
- Evidence: secondary/devotional-institutional sources agree independently that Sombari Baba lived and meditated at Kakrighat, that Neem Karoli Baba later continued the same lineage/ashram there, and that Panjabi Baba is part of the same shared-sacred-place tradition. No single source gives a precise address/coordinate distinct from "Kakrighat."

- Kasar Devi is ~8.5 km from Almora (source: travanya.com/euttaranchal.com), i.e. a short additional leg past/near Almora, not a backtrack.

- **Chronology**: Hotel Evelyn (humane ~08:00–08:30 departure) → Kakrighat stop (~1–1.5h in, given it's roughly midway to Almora) → 30–45 min at Kakrighat → continue toward Kasar Devi/Grot Vivekananda (Almora + 8.5 km, another ~1–1.5h from Kakrighat) → meaningful cave time (protected, per Mark constraint) → onward Almora area → Dwarahat (61 km) → Dunagiri (14 km more), a further ~2–2.5h.
- **Total picture**: this is already a long, full mountain day (on the order of 5–6h of driving alone across ~140–150 km of hill road, before any stop time), which matches why this is already the plan for a single dedicated travel day (23 Dec) rather than a half-day. Kakrighat adds essentially no extra road km here — it is directly on the Nainital→Almora leg that the day already requires.
- **Extra road time versus a no-Kakrighat baseline: effectively zero.** Kakrighat is a stop on the direct route, not a detour, given it sits on NH109 between Nainital and Almora.
