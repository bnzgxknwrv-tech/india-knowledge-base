## 3. Bundle full-journey logic

### A. Dakshineswar + YSS + Belur

- From north-central base: one outbound Blue-Line journey to Dakshineswar, not three separate returns.
- Walk/short local transfer between temple and YSS: official YSS ~1 km.
- Cross to Belur by ferry if confirmed; current 2026 Dakshineswar official site states ferry services link Chandni Ghat and Belur Math.
- Return from Belur by road/rail/ferry according to actual evening service.
- **Net geometry result:** the three anchors are much more efficient as a single river bundle than as separate city round trips.

### B. Cossipore + Baranagar + Alambazar

- All lie on the north-bank institutional arc.
- RKM's own retreat/procession evidence shows this is a real historical circuit rather than an invented route.
- **Net geometry result:** one north-corridor vehicle block eliminates repeated central returns; added movement between sites is measured in tens of minutes, not new half-days.

### C. Balaram Mandir + Mayer Bari + Kumartuli

- These are all in/around Bagbazar–Kumartuli.
- **Net geometry result:** Kumartuli's craft/process payoff can be added to the Ramakrishna/Sarada content with very low travel overhead.

### D. Vivekananda Ancestral House + Jorasanko + Marble Palace

- Compact north-central heritage belt.
- **Net geometry result:** if based near Girish Park, transport overhead can be almost negligible relative to the actual visits.

### E. Sri Yukteswar + Serampore College + Rai Ghat + Danish Serampore

- YSS explicitly describes the principal Yogananda/Sri Yukteswar places as clustered in Serampore; the Danish heritage is in the same town.
- **Net geometry result:** one regional transfer buys both AOAY/Kriya and general-traveler content. This is the strongest `JE BENT ER TOCH` crossover outside the Dakshineswar–Belur core.

### F. Burrabazar + attar + Howrah Bridge/river

- Market lanes and river/bridge edge are in the same broad central-west urban belt.
- **Net geometry result:** nuts, fragrance, wholesale logistics, bridge engineering and ferry/metro can be sampled with one city block rather than separate special-interest trips.

### G. Victoria/Maidan + Nandan

- Close south-central culture cluster.
- Coffee/Kalighat only joins if those candidates survive Mark's grading; do not force them into the route merely to fill a day.

### H. Kamarpukur + Jayrambati

- These are the only two presented anchors whose geometry is genuinely **outside** Kolkata.
- **Net geometry result:** never visit them on separate Kolkata round trips. If both survive, pair them in one rural excursion/possible overnight and decide calendar later.

## 4. Geometry decision implications before A/B/C

1. **Dakshineswar is not too isolated to function as a serious content anchor.** Blue Line access and the currently stated Belur ferry connection materially reduce friction.
2. **Belur is content-rich but sleep-base weaker than content-base.** It works superbly inside Bundle A even if Mark never sleeps there.
3. **North-central Kolkata remains the provisional balanced base** because Bundles B–D sit around it and Blue Line reaches Bundle A.
4. **Serampore should be graded as one concentrated AOAY + Danish bundle**, not as four independent far-away pins.
5. **Kamarpukur/Jayrambati are a true satellite** and must carry their own travel-time burden in Mark's grade.
6. **Transport attractions #20–22 should mostly have zero net calendar cost**: ride them only when they replace transport already required.
7. **No exact hotel or itinerary should be optimized until Mark grades the 36 material candidates.**
