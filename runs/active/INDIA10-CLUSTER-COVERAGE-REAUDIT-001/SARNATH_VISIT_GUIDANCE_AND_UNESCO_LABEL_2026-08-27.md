## WHAT MARK ACTUALLY VISITS — GUIDED ORDER
Starting/ending base: `Sahi River View Guesthouse, Assi Ghat — LOCKED_BY_MARK`.

### S1 — Chaukhandi Stupa [UNESCO WH component]
What: monumental stupa marking the traditional place where Buddha met his first five disciples before the first sermon.
Planning allowance: ~20–30 min unless Mark wants to linger.

### S2 — Archaeological Remains of Sarnath [UNESCO WH component]
This is the real core, not just one stupa.
Include at least:
- Dhamek Stupa — major surviving first-sermon/pilgrimage monument;
- Dharmarajika Stupa remains;
- Ashoka pillar remains/context;
- monastic/vihara ruins;
- ancient Mulagandhakuti/main shrine remains;
- Deer Park / Isipatana sacred-landscape context where accessible within the circuit.
Planning allowance: ~75–90 min unhurried.

### S3 — Sarnath Archaeological Museum [interpretive must-see, do not call the building a UNESCO component]
Why: holds the Ashokan Lion Capital associated with Sarnath and important Sarnath-school Buddhist sculpture, including the famous preaching-Buddha tradition.
Planning allowance: ~45–60 min.
Operational note: exact current hours/weekly closure must be rechecked for actual calendar; existing planning evidence has Friday closure.

### S4 — Mulagandha Kuti Vihara [modern living Buddhist temple, not the ancient archaeological ruin]
Why: gives living Buddhist ritual/temple context beside the archaeological world; do not confuse it with the ancient Mulagandhakuti remains inside the archaeological component.
Planning allowance: ~20–30 min.

### S5 — Shreyansanath Jain Tirth / Simhapuri — current A
Separate Jain layer already retained by Mark/canon in Sarnath.
Include on the same Sarnath outing because it is geographically compatible, but keep it conceptually separate from the Buddhist UNESCO property.
Planning allowance: ~30–45 min plus local transfer/entry.
