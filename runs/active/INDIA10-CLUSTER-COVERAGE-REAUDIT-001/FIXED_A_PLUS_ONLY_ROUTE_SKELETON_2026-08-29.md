## WHY THIS ORDER IS THE CLEAN BASELINE
- International flights hard-anchor Delhi at both ends.
- Delhi -> Kumaon is the natural northern gateway; Kumaon already has a closed internal eastward order and a full travel-day exit burden.
- After finishing eastern Kumaon, moving down to the plains/Agra corridor avoids forcing a later return north.
- Agra -> Bodh Gaya has an efficient current overnight-train hypothesis, so the long west-east movement can be absorbed during sleep.
- Bodh Gaya -> Varanasi is a strong natural eastern pair with a short direct daytime rail connection.
- Varanasi is the strongest current airport gateway from the eastern chain toward the Ramana/Arunachala south world.
- Tiruvannamalai/Arunachala is therefore likely the final major content world before returning to Delhi.

Therefore CURRENT WORKING GATEWAY HYPOTHESIS:
`VARANASI -> midday direct flight to BENGALURU -> private car to TIRUVANNAMALAI`

Why:
- longer road than Chennai, but the midday flight permits a same-evening arrival;
- avoids an extra Chennai airport hotel night or a 00:30-04:00 road transfer;
- whole occupied day remains FULL TRAVEL DAY, but human burden is much better.

This is PROVISIONAL, not frozen: exact January date may fall after the currently published validity block and all VNS->BLR/VNS->MAA services must be live-rechecked before calendar/booking lock.

### TIRUVANNAMALAI / ARUNACHALA -> DELHI
CURRENT WORKING EXIT HYPOTHESIS:
`TIRUVANNAMALAI -> private car to CHENNAI AIRPORT -> direct flight CHENNAI -> DELHI`

Current evidence:
- Tiruvannamalai -> Chennai Airport road ~171.6 km; public route products ~4 h class; conservative private-car planning should not use optimistic raw minima.
- Chennai -> Delhi has very dense nonstop service. Air India currently advertises many daily direct flights and January 2027 availability; current direct block is roughly 2 h 45-3 h.

Why Chennai for EXIT despite Bengaluru for ENTRY:
- materially shorter road from Tiruvannamalai;
- extremely dense direct Chennai->Delhi schedule;
- no reason to drive west to Bengaluru merely to fly north.

So the clean south geometry is intentionally ASYMMETRIC:
`VARANASI -> BENGALURU -> TIRUVANNAMALAI -> CHENNAI -> DELHI`
