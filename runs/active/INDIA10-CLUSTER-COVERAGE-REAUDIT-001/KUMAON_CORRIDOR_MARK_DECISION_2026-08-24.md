## Decision principle
A site's practical grade must be judged against the mandatory corridors between retained A+ anchors, not from isolated distance to one base. A previously awkward site can remain/upgrade in practice when it is a natural low-increment transfer catch; a historically attractive site can be cut when it requires a real side excursion or a long alternate corridor.

## Hard corridor lesson for all INDIA
Before ordinary A/B/C review in any fixed cluster, INDIA must first construct the mandatory A+ corridors and classify every candidate as:
1. ON_CORRIDOR — essentially on the mandatory path;
2. SMALL_DETOUR — low incremental detour from a mandatory corridor;
3. ALTERNATIVE_CORRIDOR_BUNDLE — longer route that captures several meaningful sites together;
4. TRUE_SIDE_EXCURSION — separate outward trip with real time cost;
5. OFF_CORRIDOR / DROP — practical cut unless intrinsically strong enough to justify reopening.

For every candidate shown to Mark, include the corridor endpoints, extra driving/walking time compared with the mandatory baseline, visit time, whether taxi capture is realistic, and resulting incremental day impact. Never ask Mark to infer geography from place names.
