8. **FORUM / COMMUNITY SLEEPERS** — Reddit, travel forums, travelogues, local blogs and repeated traveler tips that guidebooks overlook.
9. **SEASON / WINDOW** — what is actually compelling/possible in late Dec–Jan.
10. **GEOGRAPHY AFTER DISCOVERY** — only after the universe is visible: corridor fit, km/time, natural bundles and displacement.
