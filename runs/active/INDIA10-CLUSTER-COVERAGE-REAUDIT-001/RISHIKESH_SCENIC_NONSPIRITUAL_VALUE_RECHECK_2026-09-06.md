## QUESTION
Mark is reconsidering Haridwar–Kankhal–Rishikesh not only for spirituality but as a possible beautiful-India / landscape / adventure world. Determine whether Rishikesh is independently compelling for non-spiritual travellers and whether the likely late-Dec / New-Year window is a good fit.

## BOTTOM LINE
Rishikesh is genuinely attractive for non-spiritual travellers, but its strongest non-spiritual appeal is **river + foothills + adventure + backpacker/wellness atmosphere**, not "India's ultimate mountain-landscape destination" in the same class as higher Himalayan scenery.

The official Uttarakhand Tourism policy is revealing: Rishikesh appears prominently under pilgrimage, health/rejuvenation and MICE, while the state's dedicated Nature & Landscape list highlights places such as Nainital, Mussoorie, Almora, Kausani, Lansdowne, Mukteshwar and Ranikhet rather than Rishikesh. At the same time, official state/national tourism material explicitly markets Rishikesh as a rafting centre, backpacker hang-out, gateway to Himalayan treks, trekking/waterfall destination and adventure hub.

Interpretation: **Rishikesh is scenic, but scenery is strongest as part of the river/adventure/foothill experience rather than as a standalone high-Himalaya panorama town.**

## WHY NON-SPIRITUAL PEOPLE GO
- white-water rafting on the Ganges, especially upstream Shivpuri/Kaudiyala stretches;
- trekking/walking in Himalayan foothills and to waterfalls/viewpoints;
- bungee/adventure sports;
- backpacker/café/social atmosphere;
- riverside walking, beaches and sitting by the Ganges;
- Beatles Ashram / 1960s counterculture history;
- nearby Rajaji National Park / wildlife potential;
- wellness/yoga even for travellers without religious motivation;
- gateway feel: the point where plains turn into Himalayan foothills.

## WHERE THE BEAUTY ACTUALLY IS
### Central Rishikesh / Tapovan / Swarg Ashram
Strength:
- green/turquoise Ganges through town;
- forested hills immediately behind the river;
- ghats, pedestrian riverbank, temples/ashrams adding visual character;
- early morning / late afternoon light.

Weakness:
- commercial, traffic-heavy/noisy sections;
- dense development and tourism businesses;
- not a pristine mountain town;
- community reports consistently warn that the town can feel crowded/commercial if treated as a checklist destination.

### Upstream Shivpuri / Kaudiyala
This is the stronger pure-landscape layer:
- clearer/narrower Ganges valley;
- rocky riverbanks and beaches;
- forested hillsides;
- rafting through the scenery rather than just viewing it;
- Kaudiyala is officially promoted as a scenic adventure/water-sports location roughly 40 km upstream.

### Kunjapuri / Narendranagar side
Roughly 25 km from Rishikesh on the road toward Tehri; official tourism explicitly notes beautiful natural vistas from this mountain-road sector. On a clear winter morning, a high viewpoint can give layered foothills and distant snowy Garhwal Himalaya views.

This is important for Mark's question: the strongest "MOOI India" argument is not just the urban Rishikesh riverfront; it is **Rishikesh + upstream Ganges valley + one high foothill viewpoint**.

Weather class:
- December long-term normals are roughly daytime around 20–21 C and nights around 6–10 C depending dataset/site;
- January roughly daytime around 19 C and nights around 5–10 C;
- winter is relatively dry, often clear/crisp, with short ~10-hour daylight;
- river water is cold; rafting can still operate in winter under normal seasonal conditions, but clothing/wetsuit/operator/weather conditions matter;
- mornings/evenings by the river can feel genuinely cold;
- winter air can improve distant mountain visibility compared with humid/monsoon periods.

Season verdict:
- **good to very good for sightseeing, river walks, clear views and cool-weather activity**;
- **good but colder for rafting**;
- not the lushest/greenest season; some lower slopes can look drier than post-monsoon Oct/Nov;
- waterfalls may be less dramatic than in wetter seasons;
- New Year itself can add holiday crowds, so do not assume "winter = empty" around 30–31 Dec.
