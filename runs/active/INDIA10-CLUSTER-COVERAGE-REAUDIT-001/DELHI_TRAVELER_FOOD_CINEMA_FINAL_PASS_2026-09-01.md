# 1. FOOD AS DELHI TRAVEL CONTENT

## **Moti Mahal / Daryaganj butter-chicken lineage — historic post-Partition tandoori/Mughlai restaurant story (Daryaganj / Old Delhi) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
WHY IT MATTERS:
- Kundan Lal Gujral came from Peshawar after Partition and re-established Moti Mahal in Daryaganj in 1947.
- The Moti Mahal family history links the restaurant to the development/popularisation of tandoori chicken, butter chicken / murgh makhani and dal makhani.
- This is one of the strongest answers to Mark's example: 'een gerecht dat we allemaal kennen en dat met een bepaalde plek in Delhi verbonden is.'

HISTORICAL CAUTION — HARD:
Do **not** present 'butter chicken was definitively invented at this exact Delhi restaurant' as uncontested fact. National Geographic's detailed 2024 history documents a live dispute between the Gujral and Jaggi family lines and notes that some versions place the development earlier in Peshawar, while others place the accidental creation at Moti Mahal in Daryaganj. Food historian Pushpesh Pant also disputes a simple single-inventor story.

SAFE FINAL-DOCUMENT WORDING:
`Moti Mahal in Daryaganj is one of the central historic places through which butter chicken became a Delhi and then worldwide restaurant classic; the exact invention story is contested.`

TRAVEL USE:
If an Old Delhi/Daryaganj corridor is chosen, eating butter chicken/dal makhani here or at a historically defensible successor can make the meal part of the travel story rather than a generic restaurant stop. Verify the exact operating original/successor premises before the final day card because the historic brand has multiple branches/lineages.

Sources checked: Moti Mahal official histories; National Geographic 16 Aug 2024 origin investigation.

## **Karim's — 1913 Old Delhi Mughlai institution beside Jama Masjid (Jama Masjid / Old Delhi) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
Delhi Tourism identifies Karim's as established in **1913** by Haji Karimuddin near Jama Masjid, with the explicit idea of bringing 'royal food to the common man'. Current Delhi Tourism highlights qormas, tikkas, karahi gosht and khamiri roti.

WHY IT MATTERS:
This is not a trendy restaurant inserted for comfort. It is a surviving Old Delhi food institution whose location, history and food are part of the Old Delhi traveler experience.

NATURAL CORRIDOR:
Jama Masjid / Chandni Chowk / Old Delhi / Delite Cinema / Daryaganj worlds. If that corridor is selected, Karim's can replace a generic meal without creating a separate cross-city trip.

Source: Delhi Tourism current food-tour page.

## **Wenger's — historic European-style bakery/confectionery operating in Connaught Place since the early 20th century (Connaught Place) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
Official Wenger's history:
- catering operation began **1924** for British troops;
- tea room/confectionery inaugurated in **1926**;
- moved into Connaught Place in the late 1920s/early 1930s;
- Wenger's describes itself as the **oldest surviving establishment in Connaught Place**.

WHY IT MATTERS:
This is precisely a cult historic shop/bakery with place-memory. If a Connaught Place/Jantar Mantar/State Emporiums corridor is used, Wenger's is a high-quality travel-texture stop rather than a random pastry shop.

Source: Wenger's official website/current history.

## **Kuremal Mohan Lal Kulfi Wale — Old Delhi kulfi family famous for fruit-stuffed kulfi (Chawri Bazaar / Old Delhi) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
Official Kuremal history dates the family kulfi business to **1906**. The family history says Mohan Lal expanded the range and introduced the stuffed-fruit kulfi; Indian Express also documents the century-old Old Delhi lineage. Current signature examples include fruit shells such as mango/orange/pomegranate filled with kulfi.

WHY IT MATTERS:
This is one of the clearest 'small weird delicious thing that belongs to Delhi' discoveries. It has very high traveler reward for a short stop when already in Old Delhi.

NATURAL CORRIDOR:
Chawri Bazaar / Jama Masjid / Karim's / Chandni Chowk. No separate day.

Sources: Kuremal official; Indian Express family history.

## **Indian Coffee House — worker-cooperative coffee-house institution with Delhi roots in the 1950s (Delhi) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
The current Indian Coffee House Delhi official history traces the network to the Coffee Board and worker cooperative movement; it records the first New Delhi Indian Coffee House opening in 1957 after retrenched Coffee Board workers formed cooperatives with political support.

WHY IT MAY MATTER:
More cultural/historical than gastronomic: the India Coffee House network is part of post-independence urban intellectual/political coffee-house culture.

CAUTION:
Current official Delhi contact page points to Kamla Nagar; older traveler material often associates the famous terrace Coffee House with Mohan Singh Place. Final exact venue/open status must be live-checked before presenting a specific branch as the historic experience.

Source: Indian Coffee House Delhi official history.

# 2. CINEMA — TECHNICAL EXPERIENCE VS CULTURAL EXPERIENCE

## **PVR Priya IMAX — large standalone laser-IMAX in the historic Priya cinema location (Vasant Vihar / south Delhi) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
Current venue evidence:
- Priya Highstreet/PVR describes it as a state-of-the-art standalone IMAX with next-generation laser projection and multi-channel IMAX sound;
- current 2026 cinema-enthusiast comparisons repeatedly place Priya among the strongest overall Delhi-NCR IMAX choices, with a large ~69-ft-class screen and 12-channel sound cited in detailed community measurements.

CORRECT CLAIM:
`strongest current enthusiast pick for a large modern IMAX experience in Delhi`.

DO NOT CLAIM:
- 'best cinema in the world';
- true 1.43:1 dual-laser IMAX;
- 70mm IMAX.
Current enthusiast sources specifically distinguish Delhi's current laser/xenon installations from true dual-laser 1.43 venues.

## **PVR Select Citywalk IMAX — smaller premium laser-IMAX alternative (Saket / south Delhi) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
Current enthusiast comparisons often credit Select Citywalk with strong projector/picture quality but a smaller screen than Priya. It is a legitimate second choice if its film/showtime fits the day's geography better.

PLANNING LOGIC:
Do not cross Delhi merely because one projector is marginally better. Once the final hotel and spare-day corridor are known, choose the best cinema that fits the actual film + time + route.

## **Delite Cinema — surviving 1954 Delhi movie palace with huge-hall Bollywood crowd culture (Daryaganj / Old Delhi edge) [OPEN / NOT GRADED]**
Indian Express documents Delite opening in **1954**, one of the first large cinemas built in Delhi after Independence, originally with about 1,100 seats and also hosting live theatre. Hindustan Times/Delhi Walla describes its surviving grand interiors and the communal whistles/applause atmosphere of a huge hall.

CRITICAL CURRENT CHECK:
It is not merely historical: BookMyShow has active Aug/Sep 2026 schedules at Delite, including a **4K Laser Atmos** auditorium. So this remains an actionable traveler experience.

WHY IT MAY BE MORE MEMORABLE THAN IMAX:
If a big Hindi blockbuster is playing with a strong local crowd, Delite gives a specifically Indian/Delhi cinema experience that a technically better multiplex may not. This is a culture choice, not a projector-spec choice.

# 3. HOW TO USE CINEMA IN THE DELHI RESERVE
Cinema is NOT automatically worth spending scarce Delhi hours on.

Use it when:
- an interesting Hindi blockbuster / major Indian release is actually playing;
- there is a genuinely special IMAX-format title;
- the cinema sits naturally in the chosen spare-day corridor;
- Mark wants an evening cultural experience after daytime sightseeing.

Do NOT use it when:
- only an ordinary film is showing that could be seen at home;
- reaching the theatre creates a large cross-city detour;
- it displaces a much stronger one-time Delhi experience.

Current decision support:
- **maximum modern spectacle:** PVR Priya IMAX;
- **premium alternative / route-dependent:** PVR Select Citywalk IMAX;
- **most specifically Delhi/Indian cinema-cultural experience:** Delite Cinema with the right Hindi film and crowd.
