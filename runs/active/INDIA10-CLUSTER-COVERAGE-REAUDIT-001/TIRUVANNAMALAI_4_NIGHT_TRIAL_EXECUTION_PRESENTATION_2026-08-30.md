## 8. WHAT FOUR NIGHTS ACTUALLY COSTS
Four nights no longer looks like 'all A must be rushed'. A better description is:

**All protected A can fit, but Sri Ramanasramam immersion becomes distributed rather than receiving one entire standalone local day.**

Approximate protected ashram presence in this trial, excluding arrival bonus and ordinary sleeping:
- Day 1 afternoon/evening: ~6 h;
- Day 2 early morning + afternoon/evening: ~7 h;
- Day 3 afternoon/evening: ~6 h;
- plus meals/guest rhythm if actually staying inside.

So the sacrifice is NOT necessarily 'little ashram'. The sacrifice is:
- no fully unstructured whole day with absolutely nowhere else to go;
- three consecutive purposeful mornings;
- less spare capacity for a slow start after the inbound travel day;
- no room for an outer LP/traveler magnet without displacing protected A/ashram depth;
- if temple queues, leg fatigue, illness or late arrival are worse than normal, there is no spare fifth local day to absorb them.
