## CLASSIC GUIDEBOOK / WORLD-MAGNET LAYER
### Gingee Fort — three dramatic hill citadels and enormous fortified landscape east of Tiruvannamalai — OPEN / NOT GRADED
Current Lonely Planet calls it an enormous, dramatic and very worthwhile stop; three separate hill citadels rise from the Tamil plain and the two climbable main hills require roughly a half day to do properly. Current LP says ~37 km east of Tiruvannamalai. Current traveler reports reinforce scenic/solitary value, irregular stairs and monkey issues.
Existing project status remains historical/dropped with the old additional layer; this repair does not reopen it.

### Sathanur Dam — major reservoir, gardens and crocodile facility southwest of Tiruvannamalai — OPEN / NOT GRADED
Official Tiruvannamalai district tourism: one of Tamil Nadu's major dams, roughly 28–30 km from town, with maintained parks/gardens, a crocodile farm and fish grotto; the gardens have also been used in films.
Traveler value: local picnic/landscape curiosity rather than world-level magnet. No evidence it should challenge the locked Ramana plan.
