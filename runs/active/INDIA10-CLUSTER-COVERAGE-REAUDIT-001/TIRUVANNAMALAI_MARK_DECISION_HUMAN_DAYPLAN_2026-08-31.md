## WHAT THE PLACES ACTUALLY FEEL LIKE — DECISION CONTEXT

### Sri Ramanasramam [A]
Physical/experiential scale: a real working ashram compound with Samadhi Hall, Old Hall, Mother's Shrine, meditation spaces, dining hall, library/bookshop, shaded grounds and guest facilities. Google current signal is ~4.8/5 from ~24k reviews. Official routine runs from 05:30 to 20:30 with chanting, puja, meals and long quiet periods. Photography is prohibited inside.

Human feel: quiet/ordered rather than tourist-attraction energy. The central activity is sitting, meditation, listening, reading, chanting and simply being there. This is the place most likely to expand beyond any technical dwell estimate if it resonates.

INDIA dwell recommendation: one full standalone day PLUS the later afternoons/evenings already built into Days 3–5. If one night must be removed, the standalone day is the main compression lever — not because the ashram matters least, but because repeated later ashram blocks can partly substitute.

### Arunachaleswarar / Annamalaiyar Temple [A]
Physical scale: genuinely enormous living temple — official district source says ~25 acres, more than 100 shrines, thousands of sculptures, ~450 inscriptions, large tanks/courtyards and an east Rajagopuram 217 ft / 66 m high. Google current visitor signal is roughly 4.7/5 with ~45k reviews.

Human feel: completely different from Ramanasramam — bells, puja, queues, barefoot stone courtyards, active local pilgrimage, monumental towers and more sensory load. The Patala Lingam area gives it specific Ramana significance beyond generic temple architecture.

INDIA dwell recommendation: 2.5–3 h early morning. Later morning can be hot/crowded; current official access begins 05:30. Queue variability means the plan needs slack.

### Gurumurtam [A]
Physical scale: small shrine/temple rather than a large attraction. Current map entity has essentially no mass-review signal, which itself tells Mark this is not a famous tourist stop.

Why it matters: young Ramana moved here around Feb/Mar 1897 and lived in extraordinary inward absorption for roughly a year to 18 months. Visitors began to find him here; Palaniswami became his attendant. The value is biographical continuity and standing where this early phase unfolded, not architecture.

Human feel: likely local, small, relatively quiet and visually modest. This is a strong example of a place whose spiritual/biographical value may exceed its physical spectacle.

INDIA dwell recommendation: ~45–60 min. If Mark later wants to cut A content for one shorter world, this is one of the first two A's INDIA would put on the comparison table — recommendation only, grade remains A until Mark changes it.

### Pavalakunru / Pavazhakundru [A]
Physical scale: small hilltop Ardhanarishvara temple, not a big complex. Recent July 2026 visitor reports ~250–300 steps, excellent views, and essentially no other visitor during the visit. Current Google/Justdial signal is tiny (~4.4 with ~10 ratings): again, not a mass-tourism attraction.

Why it matters: Ramana stayed here in 1898. His mother Alagammal came with his brother to plead with him to return home; Ramana stayed silent and wrote his famous note about destiny and remaining quiet. That gives the site a very specific emotional/biographical event.

Human feel: stairs, little hill, view over town/Big Temple, small shrine, potentially very quiet. Current opening information conflicts: public listings favor late afternoon, while a July 2026 visitor found priest access around 09:00. This is an operational-hours issue, not a location-identity issue.

INDIA dwell recommendation: ~45–60 min including climb/view/sitting. Like Gurumurtam, this is a plausible A to compare if Mark later wants to reduce micro-biography stops, but INDIA does not change the A.

### Skandashram [A]
Physical scale: small tree-shaded hillside hermitage, reached by a real climb rather than road drop-off. Current Google signal ~4.8 with ~700+ reviews; reviews repeatedly mention serenity, meditation and city/temple views.

Why it matters: Ramana lived here 1916–1922; his mother joined him and lived the final years of her life in this period. Her death/liberation in 1922 led to the burial at the foot of the hill and ultimately the creation of Sri Ramanasramam below.

Human feel: physical exertion first, then shade, air, view, a much smaller intimate place than the main ashram. Monkeys occur on the hill; food should not be displayed carelessly. January morning is the right thermal window.

INDIA dwell recommendation: ~1 h minimum at the hermitage after the climb.

### Virupaksha Cave [A]
Physical scale: genuinely small cave, Om-shaped according to Sri Ramanasramam, containing the samadhi of Sage Virupaksha. Current Google signal ~4.7 with ~900+ reviews, repeatedly emphasizing silence/meditation. Open-current class ~08:30–16:30.

Why it matters: Ramana lived here for about 17 years, 1899–1916 — one of the strongest physical-continuity places in his life.

Human feel: dim/small/stone, not visually grand. Because the chamber is small, a group can change the atmosphere quickly; arriving earlier in the day is preferable. This is precisely a place where Mark's preference says size does NOT imply short dwell.

INDIA dwell recommendation: 1 full hour.

### Giripradakshina / Girivalam [A]
Physical scale: 14 km full circumambulation of the whole sacred mountain; official district source lists eight directional lingams, eight Nandis and many tanks/mandapas.

Important expectation correction: this is a sacred pilgrimage circuit, largely road/path around the mountain, not a secluded forest hike. The experience is mountain-presence + pilgrims + shrines + roadside life + repetition/foot rhythm. On full-moon/festival days it can become enormous mass pilgrimage; ordinary early morning is much calmer.

Human feel: longest physical load in this world. Around 06:00 in January expect roughly 18–20 C; by midday roughly 29–31 C, so starting early materially improves the experience.

INDIA dwell recommendation: 5–6 h including stops, prayer, chai, photography and short rests. Do not attach another demanding attraction afterward.

### Mango Tree Cave [B / ONLY_IF_NATURAL_ON_A_ROUTE]
Physical scale: tiny rough cave, very small review sample (~4.8 / ~10). Current map geometry places it very close to the Virupaksha/Skanda trail.

Ramana context: part of the early hill-cave period; historically he used a mango-tree cave in this phase/summers. It is not allowed to become its own excursion.

INDIA dwell recommendation: 15–30 min only if the actual path makes it <=10–15 min extra. Otherwise skip.

### Pachaiamman Temple [B / ONLY_IF_NATURALLY_EASY]
Physical scale: normal local temple, current Google signal ~4.7 with ~800+ reviews. Recent July 2026 visitor described roughly 10–15 people during a visit and walked ~1.7 km from Pavalakunru.

Ramana context: he stayed here during plague-period episodes; official Ramanasramam timeline records Jan–Mar 1908 with Ganapati Muni and others.

INDIA dwell recommendation: 30–45 min only if a day creates a nearly free capture. Baseline 5-night plan skips it because it would compete with better Ramanasramam evening/recovery time.
