## GAYA FOOD AS TRAVEL CONTENT
### Gaya Tilkut — winter sesame sweet with local hammering tradition — existing A* / preserve current grade
Official Bihar Tourism describes Tilkut as a winter specialty made from sesame plus sugar/jaggery and explicitly ties it to the cold/dry season and Makar Sankranti. The trip occurs in the ideal season.
This repair strengthens the existing A* texture: it is not merely 'buy a sweet'; the production technique and winter season are part of the experience.

### Gaya street-food microtexture — no new grade
Current Gaya guides repeatedly surface hyperlocal items beyond Tilkut: litti chokha, samosa chaat, aloo kachalu, sattu-based foods, khaja and winter sweets. These can enrich a real Gaya-station/city passage but do not independently justify a Gaya day.
