# TIRUVANNAMALAI — SRI RAMANASRAMAM STAY MODEL — 2026-08-30

Status: **CURRENT OFFICIAL-STAY MODEL / PLANNING INPUT / NO MARK DURATION DECISION**
Branch: `agent/india8-cluster-casting`
Purpose: clarify what staying at Sri Ramanasramam actually means so the 4/5/6-night duration model does not treat the ashram like a hotel or, conversely, invent compulsory retreat rules that do not exist.

## BOTTOM LINE
Sri Ramanasramam accommodation is **not a hotel product**. It is free/donation-based limited lodging for devotees of Bhagavan Sri Ramana Maharshi whose purpose is to deepen their experience/understanding of his teachings in a quiet contemplative environment.

At the same time, Sri Ramanasramam is **not a compulsory-program retreat**. The current official site explicitly states that there is no requirement to participate in any particular belief or worship practice; guests are free to use the ashram's activities and resources as they individually see fit.

Therefore the correct planning model is:
- stay there as a devotee/seeker, not as a tourist using free lodging;
- structure the stay around Ramana/Arunachala immersion;
- participation in chanting, puja, readings etc. is optional, not compulsory;
- self-directed silence, meditation, reading, sitting and rest are legitimate core ashram time;
- visits to Ramana-associated sites, Skandashram, Virupaksha Cave and Giripradakshina are spiritually congruent with the stay;
- general sightseeing/other gurus/personal business should not be the purpose of an ashram stay.

## 1. WHO MAY STAY
Current official accommodation rules:
- accommodation is strictly for devotees of Sri Bhagavan;
- general visitors, temple tourists, general spiritual tourists and those whose main purpose is other gurus/satsangs should lodge elsewhere;
- the application asks for purpose of visit, familiarity with Bhagavan's teachings and previous visits;
- Sri Ramanasramam recommends at least three days to immerse in the spiritual environment;
- foreigners may be approved for up to 14 days, but approval is discretionary;
- current page says only roughly 25% of requests can be accepted in periods of high demand;
- requests are online only; no walk-in/phone booking;
- normal requested check-in window 07:00–19:00, checkout by 13:00;
- passport/visa/foreigner-reporting information is required for foreign nationals.

## 2. WHAT THE ROOM IS — AND IS NOT
Current official information:
- no accommodation/meal charge; voluntary donations accepted;
- basic room: bed, fan, screened windows/doors, attached bathroom; hot water in colder periods;
- no right to a particular room; allocation is at management discretion;
- some guest accommodation is inside/around the ashram, but the ashram has also deliberately built guest facilities **outside the main compound within walking distance** to preserve quiet inside the core premises.

Planning consequence:
Do not promise Mark that 'sleeping in Sri Ramanasramam' necessarily means a room behind the exact main-gate wall. An approved Sri Ramanasramam guest room may be in an ashram-run guest facility within walking distance. This still counts as the desired true Sri Ramanasramam stay experience, but final exact room/building geometry is unknown until allocation.

Meals are only for registered guests/invitees and punctuality is requested; guests should line up at least 10 minutes before meal/program start where stated.

## 4. IS PARTICIPATION MANDATORY?
No compulsory attendance rule was found. The official Sri Ramanasramam home/about wording is unusually clear: there are **no requirements to take part in any particular beliefs or worship practices** and visitors may make use of activities/resources as they individually deem appropriate.

No official rule was found requiring guests to perform chores, work shifts, seva or attend a minimum number of chants/pujas.

That does NOT make the place a hotel. The corresponding conduct rule says devotees are expected to come for quiet study, reflection and meditation; sightseeing, social visits and personal business belong elsewhere.

## 5. WHAT 'FREE TIME' ACTUALLY MEANS
Free time at Sri Ramanasramam is not an entertainment gap between mandatory sessions. It is a major part of the intended experience.

Legitimate self-directed ashram time includes:
- sitting silently in the Old Hall / Meditation Hall / Samadhi spaces when open;
- self-enquiry / meditation;
- reading Ramana texts;
- using the Sri Ramana Library during opening hours;
- visiting Bhagavan's Shrine, Mother's Shrine, Nirvana Room and animal/devotee samadhis;
- quietly walking/sitting in the grounds;
- resting in one's room;
- joining some chants/pujas/readings and skipping others;
- speaking minimally and keeping the phone silent;
- meals at fixed times;
- Ramana/Arunachala devotional outings such as Skandashram, Virupaksha Cave or Giripradakshina.

A recent 2026 devotee report says registered guests with a room key may retain resident access during the public midday closure and may meditate in otherwise quiet halls; treat that as useful anecdotal evidence, NOT as a guaranteed official access right until confirmed on arrival.

## 6. WHAT IS COMPATIBLE WITH AN ASHRAM STAY?
Strongly compatible:
- full ashram day;
- Skandashram + Virupaksha pilgrimage walk;
- Giripradakshina/Girivalam around Arunachala;
- Ramana life-history sites in Tiruvannamalai;
- Arunachaleswarar Temple because it is part of Ramana's own Tiruvannamalai story and the Arunachala sacred world.

Poor fit while using ashram accommodation as the base:
- external Lonely Planet sightseeing days whose main purpose is forts, archaeology, unrelated gurus or general tourism;
- treating the room as free accommodation while spending nearly all waking hours elsewhere.

Practical implication: if Mark stays at Sri Ramanasramam, keep the **outer LP/traveler magnets outside those ashram-stay nights unless the ashram has rejected the stay and a normal hotel is used instead**.

## 7. DOES ONE WHOLE ASHRAM DAY MAKE SENSE?
Yes — as a planning recommendation, not as an institutional requirement.

The official model does not force a full day, but the stated purpose of accommodation is immersion in Bhagavan's presence/teachings and the ashram itself recommends multi-day stays for that reason. A whole day with no external target is therefore the cleanest way to make the stay genuinely different from merely sleeping next to the ashram.

Suggested full ashram day rhythm, deliberately non-compulsory:
- ~05:30/06:00 early hall/chanting if desired;
- 07:00 breakfast;
- 08:00 Veda chanting OR quiet meditation/read instead;
- 09:00 puja OR Old Hall/reading;
- late morning quiet sitting/library/grounds;
- 11:30 lunch;
- midday room rest / reading / self-enquiry during shrine closure;
- 14:00 quiet return to hall/grounds;
- 16:00 tea, 16:30 English reading if desired;
- 17:00 Veda/puja if desired;
- 18:00 parayana if desired;
- 19:00 dinner;
- quiet evening until close.

The important word is **desired**. The value comes from having nowhere else to be.

## 8. EFFECT ON THE 4-NIGHT TRIAL
This research weakens the earlier 4-night trial's most optimistic interpretation.

Four nights can still execute all fixed A if ashram immersion is distributed across three days. That remains objectively possible and institutionally compatible because participation is self-directed and Ramana/Arunachala outings fit the purpose.

However, if the intended qualitative experience is **one genuine whole ashram day with no external objective**, then four nights becomes materially compressed:
- arrival day = transfer;
- one of only three local days = whole Sri Ramanasramam day;
- only two local days remain for:
  1. Skandashram + Virupaksha;
  2. full 14 km Girivalam;
  3. Arunachaleswarar Temple + Gurumurtam + Pavalakunru.

Those are three substantial modules competing for two days. They can only fit by stacking/compressing at least two modules, which reverses the nicer human rhythm achieved in the distributed-ashram model.

Therefore current planning interpretation becomes:
- **4 nights:** objectively viable only if Mark accepts distributed ashram immersion and no full blank ashram day;
- **5 nights:** first clean option that permits arrival + one complete ashram day + caves day + early-Ramana/Temple day + Girivalam day without stacking the major modules;
- **6 nights:** adds another intentionally unstructured/recovery/depth day beyond that.

This is decision support, not a Mark duration lock.
