A walk-link bundle is preferred over taxi only when:
- the route itself has good scenic/spiritual density, not merely road walking;
- legal/public access is verified;

- total day burden improves or meaningfully enriches the experience;
- route endpoints and luggage/driver handling are explicit.

Never force walking just to remove a taxi. A bad/unsafe road walk loses to a taxi.
