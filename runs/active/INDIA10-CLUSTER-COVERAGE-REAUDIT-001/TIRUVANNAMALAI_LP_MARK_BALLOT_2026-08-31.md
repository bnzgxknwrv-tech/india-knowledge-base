### 1. Gingee Fort

**Gingee Fort — enorm drie-heuvel fortlandschap met citadellen, muren, steile klim en grote vergezichten (Gingee, outer Tiruvannamalai) [OPEN] [LP + traveler].**

WHAT:
- enormous fortified landscape with three hilltop citadels and a roughly 6 km fortified perimeter;
- main climbable sectors include Krishnagiri and Rajagiri;
- strongly visual: rock hills, walls, ruined structures and broad Tamil plain views.

WHY IT COULD MATTER:
- this is the strongest visually dramatic non-spiritual LP excursion in the Tiruvannamalai orbit;
- it provides strong contrast with the Ramana/Arunachala spiritual core.

BURDEN:

- serious climbing/site time makes a meaningful visit roughly 5–7 occupied hours door-to-door;
- doing multiple hill sectors can push it toward a full day.

DISPLACEMENT:

- if retained, it competes with ashram depth/recovery or may require additional local time depending final dayplan.

INDIA RECOMMENDATION:
Strongest LP extra to consider if Mark wants one visually spectacular historical excursion outside the spiritual core. Do not include automatically.

### 2. Parvathamalai
Canonical user-facing label:
**Parvathamalai — zware bergpelgrimstocht naar de Mallikarjuna-tempel op een afgelegen top (outer Tiruvannamalai) [OPEN] [LP + nature].**

WHAT:
- mountain pilgrimage/trek to the Abidagujambal–Mallikarjunar temple on the summit;
- serious ascent with steep/exposed sections near the top; not a casual viewpoint walk.

WHY IT COULD MATTER:
- combines mountain nature and living pilgrimage rather than conventional sightseeing;
- could be emotionally strong for someone who specifically wants an additional demanding sacred mountain experience.

BURDEN:

- road planning roughly 45–70 min each way depending trailhead;
- hiking/temple commitment roughly 6–8 h;
- whole excursion roughly 8–10 h: a genuine full day.

DISPLACEMENT:
- duplicates physical load already present in the protected full 14 km Arunachala circumambulation and the Skandashram/Virupaksha hill pilgrimage;
- would consume recovery/ashram time or justify more local time.

INDIA RECOMMENDATION:
Likely skip unless Mark actively wants a second strenuous sacred-mountain day. It is substantial, not a bonus stop.
