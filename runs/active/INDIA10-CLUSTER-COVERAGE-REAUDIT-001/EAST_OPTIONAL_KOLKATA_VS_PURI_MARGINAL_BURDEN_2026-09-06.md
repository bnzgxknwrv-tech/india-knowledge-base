## KOLKATA / BENGAL
Current service evidence checked 2026-09-06:

- CCU -> MAA currently multiple nonstop flights daily, about 2h15–2h35.

Geometry verdict:
- NOT a route-breaking cross-country detour if inserted after Varanasi and before Chennai/Tiruvannamalai.
- It replaces one direct VNS->MAA flight with two direct flight legs separated by the Kolkata stay.

- Operationally easier than Puri because both air legs are frequent and no 60-km feeder road is required.

## PURI / ODISHA
Current service evidence checked 2026-09-06:
- VNS -> BBI nonstop currently about 1h20 and only ~3 weekly at present.
- Puri has no airport; official Puri/Odisha sources place BBI -> Puri at about 60 km / ~1h20 road class.
- BBI -> MAA nonstop is currently daily, about 1h45–1h55.
- Puri/Khurda -> Chennai trains exist but are roughly 18–21h and do not beat the flight route for this trip's human-time objective; some services do carry 1A, but they are not the leading insertion hypothesis.

Geometry verdict:
- ALSO not route-breaking: Puri is southeast of Varanasi and still naturally feeds south toward Chennai.
- More friction than Kolkata: 60-km airport feeder road must be done both inbound and outbound, and VNS->BBI is currently only three weekly.
- Strongly actual-date dependent because the direct VNS->BBI operating day matters.

Human-duration class:
- CREDIBLE MINIMUM = 2 Puri hotel nights: Varanasi -> BBI -> Puri travel/arrival day, one full Puri Kriya day, then Puri -> BBI -> MAA -> Tiruvannamalai full southbound travel day. Relative to baseline about +2 calendar days / +2 hotel nights.
- COMFORTABLE = 3 Puri hotel nights if Mark wants unhurried Karar Ashram/Sri Yukteswar time plus the wider Puri Kriya/Swargadwar/Balighai layer. Relative to baseline about +3 calendar days / +3 hotel nights.
- One Puri night would force two airport/road transfers around a very short spiritual visit and is not recommended.
