## PURPOSE
Ordinary A/B/C cannot be judged responsibly before the intrinsically non-negotiable anchors are identified and the practical proximity of all other candidates to those anchors is known.
Only Mark assigns A+, A, B or C, but once Mark designates a parent complex/compound/sacred micro-world A+, INDIA must apply the inheritance rules below without asking him to vote on every room, shrine or adjacent micro-site.

## HARD DISPLAY RULE
Every non-obvious Indian place/site/institution/festival/local term shown to Mark immediately gets a short plain-Dutch explanation in parentheses, every time it is mentioned. Never rely on remembered names.
Never display `Nirmal Dham` alone; use `Nirmal Dham (rustplaats/Mahasamadhi van Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi)`.

## TERMINOLOGY — HARD
Use **cost/kosten** only for money. For planning use duration, extra travel time, detour, walking/driving time and logistical burden. Never use `gratis` for small time impact.

### A+ LOCATION / EXPERIENCE
A place/experience intrinsically among the real reasons for the India journey, independent of convenience.

## PARENT-COMPLEX A+ INHERITANCE — HARD
Do NOT ask Mark to vote separately on every micro-site when he has already made the meaningful parent A+.

When a parent complex/compound/sacred micro-world is A+:
1. `SAME_PHYSICAL_SITE`, `SAME_PHYSICAL_COMPLEX` and true `PARENT_CHILD` micro-sites inherit A+ automatically as CHILD_A_PLUS.
2. Rooms, halls, shrines, samadhis, courtyards, bridges, rocks, riverbank microspots and other on-compound details stay nested under the parent; they are not separate route votes or night-drivers.
3. A nearby but physically distinct site may also be auto-bundled when it is genuinely walkable/naturally part of the same pilgrimage visit and does not create a material separate excursion. INDIA decides this from real geometry and visit logic; do not bounce obvious micro-decisions back to Mark.
4. A site several kilometres away, across difficult terrain/river, or requiring a material separate drive remains a separate candidate even if thematically related.
5. Display the parent first, then important child details in plain language; never inflate one compound into many A+ counts.
6. If Mark explicitly says an entire sacred local world is A+, INDIA may define a broader parent A+ and include its natural walkable/local-life components, while still flagging any materially separate excursion.

Examples now controlling:
- Kainchi Dham (Neem Karoli Baba-ashramcomplex; kernplek voor Neem Karoli Baba en Ram Dass): its preserved on-compound NKB/Ram Dass child sites inherit the parent A+; no separate votes.
- Mahabodhi Temple Complex (Boeddha-verlichtingscomplex in Bodh Gaya): Bodhi Tree and other internal enlightenment micro-sites are one A+ parent world; nearby but distinct Sujata/Dungeshwari-type excursions are evaluated separately by actual travel.
- Sri Ramanasramam / Arunachala local sacred world: same-compound Ramana micro-sites inherit automatically; hill caves and other direct Ramana sites are bundled or separated according to actual walking/drive geometry, not by asking Mark about every name.

## HARD TRAVEL QUALITY RULE
Fixed core clusters are already retained; determine how much time they truly require when done properly. Do not compress them merely to preserve later clusters.

## DECISION ORDER — HARD
1. Discovery is complete enough: person/AOAY + regional + traveler/LP + world-magnet universes exist.
2. Fixed core cluster set is locked for this costing phase.
3. FIRST A+ PASS: Mark confirms/rejects concise parent/anchor A+ proposals; INDIA applies parent-complex inheritance automatically.
4. OLD-A PROMOTION PASS: before traveler/LP grading, INDIA reviews preserved existing A-locations not already covered by an A+ parent. Mark is asked only about genuinely separate candidates capable of changing route/time, not child micro-sites.
5. Resolve physical identity/coordinate confidence for selected A+ parents/anchors first.
6. Overlay every remaining old A/B/C, new regional candidate and traveler/Lonely-Planet finding against nearest/relevant A+ and compute trustworthy identity, useful distance, real travel time, incremental detour, same compound/walkable/natural bundle/separate excursion and isolation.
7. Only with that context does Mark re-rate ordinary A/B/C.
8. Build realistic complete-execution schedule for the fixed cluster.
9. Derive true minimum duration/nights.
10. Repeat through all fixed core clusters.
11. Only after fixed-core time footprint is known, evaluate route-sensitive/reserve clusters.
12. Global route / quarter-hour itinerary comes last.

## PROXIMITY METRIC
Reuse `PROXIMITY_1KM_3KM_MATRIX.csv` but do not use kilometres alone. Required fields where feasible:
- `nearest_A_plus`
- `distance_km`
- `travel_mode`
- `realistic_travel_time_one_way`

Straight-line distance never substitutes for route reality in mountains, across rivers, restricted zones or poor-road areas.
