## CONFLICTS
1. **1967 first Maharaj-ji meeting location:** later retellings often broadly say Kainchi, while *Being Ram Dass* explicitly identifies the temple where the first meeting occurred as **Bhumiadhar**, followed by transfer to Kainchi for training. Freeze preserves the occurrence-level distinction rather than collapsing both into Kainchi.
2. **1967/1968 return chronology:** some public short biographies compress the first India trip; the contiguous autobiography provides the more granular 1967 route and Kainchi training. No silent harmonization where abbreviated pages differ.
3. **Gandhi Ashram naming:** memoir says 'Gandhi Ashram' at Kausani; present-day likely identity may be Anasakti Ashram, but freeze does not upgrade to EXACT without direct historical continuity evidence.
4. **Reclining Hanuman temple, Allahabad:** descriptive match to the famous Lete/Bade Hanuman Ji near Sangam is strong, but memoir does not give name; retained DEELS.
