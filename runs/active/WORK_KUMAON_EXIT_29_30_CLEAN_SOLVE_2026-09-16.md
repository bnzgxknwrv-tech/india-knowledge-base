## Important weakness / falsification

This is not a free lunch.

The weak point is **04:10 Old Delhi arrival → 06:55 New Delhi departure**. The nominal transfer margin is large because the stations are close, but December fog can delay 15014. Current historical data shows 15014 can run >1 h late on a minority of runs. Therefore:

- if 15014 is ≤60 min late at Old Delhi, the connection remains comfortable;
- around 90 min late, margin becomes meaningfully thinner but can still work with a prebooked taxi and no unnecessary station dwell;
- around 120+ min late, 12280 becomes unsafe to rely on.

This does **not** collapse the topology. If 12280 is missed, Delhi→Mathura has many later trains. Current daily examples include 20424 at 11:20→13:30 and 11078 at 11:30→13:15, but a late arrival would compress or eliminate the Vrindavan visit because of daylight/opening geometry. In that failure case, protect Agra/Taj architecture and drop the corridor A rather than create a frantic day.

Thus the route is robust at trip level, but the A visit is the sacrificial element under severe fog delay.
