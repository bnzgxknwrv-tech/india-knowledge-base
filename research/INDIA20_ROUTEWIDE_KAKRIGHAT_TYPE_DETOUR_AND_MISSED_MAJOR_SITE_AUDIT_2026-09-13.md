## B. KASAR DEVI CAVE — **HIGH-PRIORITY NEW MARK TRIAGE / SAME ERROR CLASS**

Physical place:
**Kasar Devi Cave, Almora / Kasar Devi ridge**

Why it matters:
- official Ramakrishna Kutir Almora material states Vivekananda meditated intensely in the cave for roughly 2–3 days in the first week of Sep 1890;
- it describes a sequence of higher spiritual illuminations there;
- at the climax of those practices he felt the powerful call to leave personal bliss and serve suffering humanity;
- this is therefore not just `a cave Vivekananda once visited`: it is a major mission-turning-point site.

Burden:
- current route sources give the fastest Nainital→Babaji Cave / Dwarahat route at roughly 115 km / 4–5h;
- an Almora/Binta alternative at roughly 145 km / 5–6h;
- therefore routing via Almora/Kasar Devi is roughly +30 km / about +1h driving order-of-magnitude before dwell, rather than a new destination world;
- Nainital→Kasar Devi is commonly about 70–80 km / ~2.25–3h;
- official Ramakrishna Kutir says Kasar Devi Cave is about 9–11 km from the Almora ashrama area.

Interpretation:
**This passes the Kakrighat-type audit strongly.** It must be shown to Mark before finalizing 23 Dec or the recovered 21 Dec day.

Recommended Mark-facing triage: likely A/A+ territory; AI does NOT assign grade here.

## D. CHENNAI — VIVEKANANDA HOUSE / ICE HOUSE — **MAJOR MISSED SITE**

Physical place:
**Vivekananda House / Vivekanandar Illam / former Ice House, Marina Beach, Chennai**

Why it matters:
- official Sri Ramakrishna Math Chennai says Vivekananda stayed in this exact house for nine days, 6–14 Feb 1897, after his triumphant return from the West;
- the first Ramakrishna Math centre in South India initially operated from this Ice House world;
- current Vivekananda House is an active museum / pilgrimage site;
- crucially, visitors can enter the **historic room where Vivekananda stayed**;
- current Ramakrishna Math reporting documents visitors **meditating in that exact room**.

This is precisely the kind of concrete Mark-facing meaning the old cards often omitted.

Current public hours:
- 10:00–12:30;
- 15:00–19:15;
- Sunday to about 19:30;
- closed Monday.

Mark's current Chennai day is Tuesday 19 Jan 2027, so the weekday closure does not conflict.

Burden:
- direct Tiruvannamalai→Chennai Airport drive is currently about 171 km / ~2.5h best-case road estimate;
- Vivekananda House is on Marina Beach and about 15–20 km from the airport;
- unlike Kakrighat it is not literally on the airport-line: it would require entering Chennai city and then returning toward the airport hotel;
- therefore this is **major content but moderate/high marginal burden**, not a free corridor stop;
- exact Jan-19 day placement must protect the very early Jan-20 flight wake.

Conclusion:
**Must be shown to Mark for grading; do not silently omit.**

Sources:

- https://vivekanandahouse.org/visit-us
- https://imedia.chennaimath.org/blogs/post/catch-them-young-2024-photos
