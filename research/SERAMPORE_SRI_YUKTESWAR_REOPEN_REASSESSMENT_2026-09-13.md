## Strong current institutional confirmation — not just an old travel blog

Current SRF/YSS source:
`https://yogananda.org/blog/srf-yss-president-brother-chidananda-visits-india`

In its section **Serampore Pilgrimage Sites**, SRF/YSS states that two Serampore sites are being developed/preserved as pilgrimage sites:

1. **Sacred banyan by the Ganges / Rai Ghat world** — YSS says this is the tree under which Mahavatar Babaji visited Sri Yukteswar in 1894, as recounted in *Autobiography of a Yogi*. YSS entered an agreement with Serampore municipality to manage/maintain the approach and acquired adjacent buildings with the intention of developing a meditative environment.
2. **Sri Yukteswar's main Serampore ashram/hermitage** — YSS describes this as the place where Yogananda received life-transforming spiritual training. YSS had long maintained the Sri Sri Swami Sri Yukteswar Smriti Mandir on part of the property and states it acquired ownership of the complete hermitage property, with plans to restore as much of the original hermitage building as possible.

This materially raises confidence that these are current, institutionally recognized pilgrimage targets, not merely locations reconstructed by a blogger.
