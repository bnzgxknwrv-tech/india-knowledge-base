## 1. UBER RESERVE VS INTERCITY

Uber India currently distinguishes normal/Reserve-style scheduled city rides from Uber Intercity/outstation rides.

- Uber Reserve advertises booking up to 90 days ahead in supported/select cities.
- Important caveat from Uber itself: a reservation does **not** guarantee a driver will accept; the ride is confirmed when driver details are received.
- Uber Intercity is the relevant product for many longer city-to-city road transfers and route-specific pages may allow scheduling up to 30 days in advance.

Therefore: a scheduled Uber is useful but should not be treated as an absolute mission-critical driver guarantee.

Official Reserve source:
https://www.uber.com/in/en/ride/how-it-works/reserve/

## 3. WHERE UBER IS A GOOD DEFAULT VS WHERE NOT TO RELY ON IT

Good/likely practical default, subject to live app availability:
- Delhi airport/hotel/station city legs;
- Gaya station/airport <-> Bodh Gaya;
- Varanasi station/airport <-> hotel;
- Kolkata airport <-> Dakshineswar base;
- Chennai airport/hotel city legs;
- Chennai <-> Tiruvannamalai via Intercity if live availability and driver confirmation are satisfactory.

For Kumaon/mountain critical legs, do NOT make Uber the sole robustness layer:
- Kathgodam -> Nainital;
- Nainital -> Dunagiri/Kukuchina;
- Dunagiri -> true Haidakhan Vishwa Mahadham;
- especially Haidakhan -> Kathgodam/Haldwani for an onward fixed train.

Reason: remote mountain pickup availability, route familiarity, last-mile conditions and guaranteed return availability matter more than app convenience.

Preferred planning concept for those critical mountain legs:
- pre-arranged named private driver via hotel/retreat/ashram/local trusted operator;
- phone number + pickup time + vehicle confirmed;
- mission-critical rail exit gets a real road-time buffer;
- Uber/Ola may be fallback, not the only plan.

## 4. CURRENT ROUTE PLANNING CONSEQUENCE

Do not hire one driver for the whole 33-day trip by default. A hybrid model is likely best:
- app taxi / Intercity where urban or easily replaceable;
- pre-arranged private driver for remote Kumaon mountain legs;
- rail/air for long inter-regional jumps according to the global route optimizer.
