## The world Mark was remembering

### 1. Crank's Ridge / Hippie Hill / Kasar Devi ridge

This is the pine-covered ridge around / beyond Kasar Devi above Almora. It developed a long western-seeker / bohemian / contemplative history. Sources consistently connect the ridge with people including W. Y. Evans-Wentz, Lama Anagarika Govinda, Alfred "Sunyata" Sorensen, Allen Ginsberg, Gary Snyder, Timothy Leary, Ralph Metzner and later Richard Alpert / Ram Dass; secondary accounts also place other 1960s counterculture figures in this broader world.

For Mark, the relevance is **not** generic hippie nostalgia. The important thing is that this is a physically coherent spiritual landscape containing several distinct historical places and strands: Vivekananda, Sunyata, Lama Govinda / Tibetan Buddhism, Maharajji-era western seekers, and the later hippie/counterculture layer.

Do not flatten this into one generic `Kasar Devi` card.

### 2. Turiya Niwas — Sunyata's hermit house

Alfred "Sunyata" Sorensen, the Danish mystic/hermit, lived for decades on the ridge. Anandamayi-related material describes his stone house on Crank's/Kalimath Ridge as **Turiya Niwas**, with a `Silence!` sign; he called it the abode of the highest consciousness.

A current Airbnb listing (retrieved 2026-09-14) advertises **Turiya Niwas** as a roughly 100-year-old Himalayan heritage home on Crank Ridge, about 10 minutes from Kasar Devi Temple and one minute from the main road, and explicitly says it was once Sunyata Baba's home. The listing offers the entire house, meaning this is not merely a lost historical location; at least one current commercial-access surface claims the physical house survives and can be stayed in.

**Important caution:** current Airbnb wording is useful existence/access evidence but is not itself enough to prove every historical claim. Before treating Turiya Niwas as a locked pilgrimage entity, independently verify the exact house identity / continuity if practical.

Potential travel significance: this could be a powerful low-friction way to inhabit the ridge rather than just visit it. However the trip currently has **Nainital 3 nights locked**. Do **not** silently replace a locked Nainital night with Turiya Niwas. First solve the day-trip / sleep-base geometry and put any sleep-base change to Mark explicitly.

### 3. Lama Anagarika Govinda's Kasar Devi Ashram / Bodh Ashram — strongest direct Ram Dass connection

This is a **different physical/historical entity from Turiya Niwas**.

Sources describe a Buddhist meditation/ashram property on the ridge associated first with W. Y. Evans-Wentz and then Lama Anagarika Govinda and Li Gotami; later it passed into a Drikung Kagyu / Tibetan Buddhist lineage. Current/near-current lineage sources still refer to Govinda's beloved Kasar Devi Ashram and to later retreat use of the property. Secondary descriptions call the place `Bodh Ashram`.

The direct Ram Dass connection recovered in this pass is strong and specific: official Ram Dass material records Maharajji / Neem Karoli Baba telling Ram Dass, **"Go see Lama Govinda"**, and Ram Dass later describing his meeting with Lama Govinda as a profound experience of surrender / coming home. This makes Govinda's Kasar Devi world much more than general counterculture scenery for this trip.

**Do not overclaim the exact building yet.** The historical Govinda estate / present Buddhist centre needs an exact surviving-entity and visitor-access check before a Mark-facing place card is created. But the Ram Dass-to-Lama-Govinda connection itself is durable and should not be lost again.
