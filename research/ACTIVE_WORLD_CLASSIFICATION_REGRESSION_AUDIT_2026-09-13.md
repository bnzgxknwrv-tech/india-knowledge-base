**Status:** `OPEN_UNCLASSIFIED__MUST_PRESENT_TO_MARK`.

1. `WHOLE_WORLD_OUT -> CHILD_PLACES_STAY_OUT EVEN AFTER NEIGHBOUR WORLD RETURNS`.
